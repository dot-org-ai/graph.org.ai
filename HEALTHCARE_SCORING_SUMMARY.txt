================================================================================
SCORING METHODOLOGY INSIGHTS
================================================================================

WHY 73.7% OF HEALTHCARE SCORES 0.26-0.49 (HYBRID):

1. IN-PERSON CLINICAL WORK ESSENTIAL
   - Patient examination requires presence
   - Treatment and therapy hands-on
   - Monitoring and assessment physical
   - Action Score constrained to 0.30-0.40

2. SIGNIFICANT DIGITAL INTEGRATION
   - Universal EHR adoption (96% hospitals, 78% practices)
   - Digital scheduling and appointment management
   - Electronic prescription systems
   - Lab equipment with digital interfaces
   - Result Scores: 0.55-0.75

3. MIXED ACTIVITY PROFILE
   - Direct patient care: 60-75% of work time
   - Documentation/charting: 15-25% of time
   - Administrative work: 5-10% of time
   - Activity Scores: 0.25-0.40

RESULT: Hybrid classification accurately reflects healthcare's nature

WHY SOME SCORE HIGHER (0.50-0.85):

1. LAB & IMAGING SPECIALISTS (0.55-0.75)
   - Primary work: equipment operation with digital output
   - Minimal direct patient contact
   - MRI techs (0.75), sonographers (0.70), lab techs (0.60)

2. ADMINISTRATIVE/RECORDS (0.75-0.85)
   - Electronic records primary responsibility
   - Can be performed entirely remotely
   - Health IT specialists (0.85) highest in healthcare

3. DIAGNOSTIC SPECIALISTS (0.50-0.65)
   - Analysis of digital data (imaging, pathology)
   - Interpretation requires expertise
   - Pathologists (0.65), genetic counselors (0.55)
