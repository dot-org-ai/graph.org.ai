CODE HIERARCHY:
================

Each subsector includes multiple levels:
- 4-digit level (broad category, e.g., 5411)
- 4-digit with decimal (subcategory, e.g., 5411.0)
- 5-digit level (industry group, e.g., 54111)
- 6-digit level (national industry, e.g., 541110)

This allows for both broad and granular scoring queries.

SCORE RANGES:
==============

Action Scores: 0.40 - 0.98
  - 0.98: Pure digital work (custom programming, software)
  - 0.95-0.96: Nearly pure digital (accounting, tax, translation)
  - 0.88-0.92: Highly digital (design, consulting, advertising)
  - 0.80-0.85: Primarily digital (legal, engineering, architecture)
  - 0.58-0.70: Hybrid with significant hands-on (R&D services)
  - 0.40: Primarily physical (veterinary services)

Event Scores: 1.0 (all professional services digitally representable)

Activity Scores: 0.35 - 0.98
  - Mirrors action scores
  - Reflects execution modality (digital vs. physical/field work)

Result Scores: 0.90 - 1.0
  - All professional services produce digital deliverables
  - High scores reflect digital output (reports, code, designs, analysis)

======================

Activity Score: "What's the digital/physical mix of execution?"
Result Score:   "Is the outcome digitally accessible/representable?"
