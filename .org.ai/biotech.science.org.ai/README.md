# biotech.org.ai

[![License: CC BY-SA 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY--SA%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

Comprehensive ontology domain for biotechnology, including genomics, proteomics, cell therapies, biomanufacturing, agricultural biotechnology, and synthetic biology.

## Overview

Biotechnology represents the application of biological systems and organisms to develop new products and services, or to improve existing ones. From molecular-level genetic engineering to organism-level manufacturing, biotechnology spans diverse industries and applications. The field has evolved from academic research to a major economic sector with applications in healthcare, agriculture, chemicals production, and energy. This ontology covers the complete biotechnology landscape with emphasis on scientific principles, industrial applications, and emerging technologies.

Biotechnology is fundamentally reshaping medicine, agriculture, and manufacturing:
- **Market size**: $600+ billion globally, growing 8-12% CAGR
- **R&D investment**: Tens of billions annually from biotech companies, pharma, and government
- **Employment**: Hundreds of thousands of specialized roles
- **Impact**: New drug classes, sustainable production methods, precision medicine

## NAICS Classification

Biotechnology spans multiple NAICS classifications depending on specific sector:

### Primary Classifications

**NAICS 541711 - Research and Development in Biotechnology**
- Basic research and applied research in biotechnology
- Discovery of new biological organisms and processes
- Genetic engineering and genomics research
- Includes: Research institutions, biotech startups, pharma R&D

**NAICS 325414 - Biological Product Manufacturing**
- Manufacturing of biopharmaceuticals and vaccines
- Production of enzymes, antibodies, recombinant proteins
- Cell and gene therapy manufacturing
- Includes: Biologics producers, CMOs, manufacturing facilities

**NAICS 541690 - Other Professional, Scientific and Technical Services**
- Genomic testing services
- Bioinformatics consulting
- Contract research organizations (CROs)

**NAICS 423210 - Drugs and Druggists' Sundries Merchant Wholesalers**
- Distribution of biologics and biotechnology products
- Wholesale of diagnostic tools

### Secondary Classifications (Sector-Specific)

**Pharmaceutical Sector**
- **NAICS 3254**: Pharmaceutical and medicine manufacturing
- **NAICS 42421**: Drug and druggist sundries merchant wholesalers

**Agricultural Sector**
- **NAICS 111**: Crop production
- **NAICS 112**: Animal production and aquaculture

**Food and Beverage**
- **NAICS 311**: Food manufacturing
- **NAICS 312**: Beverage and tobacco product manufacturing

**Environmental/Energy**
- **NAICS 325199**: All other basic organic chemical manufacturing
- **NAICS 21**: Mining, quarrying, oil and gas extraction

## Key Concepts and Definitions

### Biotechnology
Application of biological systems, organisms, or processes to develop or improve products and services.

### Recombinant DNA
DNA molecule created by combining DNA sequences from different sources (typically via genetic engineering).

### Biopharmaceutical
Therapeutic drug manufactured using biotechnology (living cells or organisms) as opposed to chemical synthesis.

### Fermentation
Metabolic process where organisms convert organic compounds (sugars) to energy under anaerobic or aerobic conditions.

### Biomarker
Biological characteristic that can be objectively measured and used as an indicator of health status or disease.

### Gene Therapy
Treatment of disease by introducing genetic material into patient cells to correct or compensate for genetic defects.

### Genomic Medicine
Medical practice that incorporates individual genetic/genomic information into patient care and treatment decisions.

### Synthetic Biology
Engineering discipline that applies engineering principles to design and build artificial biological systems.

### Metabolic Engineering
Rational modification of cell metabolism to increase production of desired compounds or enhance cellular characteristics.
