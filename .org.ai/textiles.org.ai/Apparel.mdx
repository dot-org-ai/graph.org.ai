# Apparel Manufacturing

Apparel manufacturing (NAICS 315) comprises establishments primarily engaged in cutting and sewing fabrics into clothing and apparel. This massive subsector includes the production of men's, women's, and children's clothing in all categories from basic undergarments to outerwear, formal wear, and fashion apparel. Unlike textile mills, apparel manufacturers purchase fabrics and other materials to produce finished garments for consumer and commercial use.

## Overview

Apparel manufacturing is among the world's largest industries, employing millions and producing billions of garments annually. The sector spans from mass-market basics to luxury fashion, serving global consumer demand. Modern apparel manufacturing involves sophisticated design, sourcing, quality control, and supply chain management, with significant competition from global producers.

The subsector has historically been labor-intensive, driving manufacturing to countries with lower labor costs. Contemporary trends include automation, nearshoring, fast fashion speed-to-market capabilities, and increased focus on sustainability and ethical manufacturing practices.

## Industry Groups

### 3151 - Apparel Knitting Mills

Establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing knitted apparel or purchasing yarn and manufacturing knitted apparel.

**Product Categories:**
- T-shirts and tank tops
- Sweaters and cardigans
- Hosiery and socks
- Thermal and base layers
- Underwear and intimates
- Casual knit dresses and tops
- Active and athletic wear

**Knitting Methods:**
- **Circular Knitting**: Creating tubular fabrics for seamless production
- **Flat Knitting**: Creating flat pieces for assembly
- **Warp Knitting**: Creating structures for foundation garments

**Market Segments:**
- Mass-market basics (T-shirts, underwear)
- Fashion and trend-driven items
- Athletic and performance wear
- Luxury and premium products
- Private label production

### 3152 - Cut and Sew Apparel Contractors

Establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing apparel for other companies on a contract basis without purchasing or owning the materials.

**Services Offered:**
- Pattern making and grading
- Cutting and sewing services
- Quality control and inspection
- Finishing and pressing
- Packaging for shipment
- Special order and customization

**Customer Types:**
- Apparel manufacturers and brands
- Retail companies with own brands
- Wholesalers and distributors
- Designer and luxury brands
- International customers

## Manufacturing Processes

### Design and Pattern Development
- **Concept Development**: Design inspiration and sketches
- **Specification Development**: Technical specifications and details
- **Pattern Grading**: Scaling patterns across sizes
- **Prototyping**: Creating first samples
- **Fit Approval**: Testing and refinement

### Sourcing and Materials
- **Fabric Selection**: Choosing appropriate textiles
- **Notions and Trim**: Buttons, zippers, thread, elastic
- **Supplier Qualification**: Ensuring quality and consistency
- **Inventory Management**: Maintaining material availability
- **Cost Management**: Balancing quality and cost

### Cutting Operations
- **Marker Making**: Layout of pattern pieces on fabric
- **Spreading**: Layering multiple plies of fabric
- **Cutting**: Using automated or manual cutters
- **Bundle Organization**: Organizing pieces for sewing
- **Quality Inspection**: Checking for defects and accuracy

### Sewing and Assembly
- **Line Organization**: Production line setup
- **Component Sewing**: Creating functional pieces
- **Assembly**: Joining component pieces
- **Finishing**: Hem sewing, seam finishing
- **Attachment**: Buttons, snaps, zippers
- **Pressing**: Final shape and appearance

### Quality Control
- **In-Process Inspection**: Monitoring during production
- **Final Inspection**: Complete finished product check
- **Size Verification**: Checking dimensions and fit
- **Stitch Quality**: Evaluating seam consistency
- **Appearance Standards**: Color, pattern, finish quality

### Finishing and Packing
- **Final Pressing**: Wrinkle removal and shape setting
- **Labeling and Tagging**: Size, care, origin information
- **Quality Certification**: Meeting standards and specifications
- **Packaging**: Individual and bulk packaging
- **Shipping Preparation**: Organization for distribution

- **Quality Consistency**: Maintaining standards

- **Retail Consolidation**: Power of major retailers
- **Price Competition**: Global sourcing and pricing pressure
- **Fast Fashion Speed**: Accelerating time-to-market
