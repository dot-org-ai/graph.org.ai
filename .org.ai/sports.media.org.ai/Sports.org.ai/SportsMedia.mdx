## Sports Media Profile

### Overview

Sports media encompasses the comprehensive coverage, production, and distribution of sports content across multiple platforms. This includes:

- **Television Broadcasting**: Linear TV networks and cable channels
- **Digital Streaming**: OTT platforms and league-specific streaming services
- **Sports Journalism**: News, reporting, and analysis
- **Content Production**: Game coverage, documentaries, original programming
- **Digital Media**: Websites, mobile apps, social media platforms
- **Sports Photography & Videography**: Content creation for distribution

### Business Models

Sports media organizations operate through multiple revenue streams:

- **Advertising**: Broadcast and digital advertising revenue
- **Subscription Services**: Subscription-based streaming and premium content
- **Licensing**: Content licensing to other platforms
- **Sponsorships**: Corporate partnerships and brand integrations
- **Affiliate Revenue**: Cable and streaming affiliate fees
- **Merchandise**: Branded content and merchandise sales

## Key Performance Indicators

### Viewership Metrics
- **Total Viewers**: Total audience size
- **Average Rating**: Average viewers during broadcast
- **Peak Rating**: Highest rating during broadcast
- **Demographic Performance**: Ratings by age, gender, etc.

### Engagement Metrics
- **Social Media Followers**: Combined followers across platforms
- **Engagement Rate**: User interactions as percentage of audience
- **Website Traffic**: Unique visits and page views
- **App Usage**: Downloads, active users, session duration

### Financial Metrics
- **Cost Per Thousand (CPM)**: Ad rates
- **Subscriber Acquisition Cost (SAC)**: Cost to gain subscriber
- **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**: Revenue per subscriber
- **Churn Rate**: Subscriber cancellation rate
