## Fitness Industry Profile

### Overview

The fitness industry encompasses health clubs, fitness centers, personal training, group fitness classes, yoga studios, and sports training facilities. This includes:

- **Health & Fitness Clubs**: Commercial gyms with equipment and classes
- **Specialized Studios**: Yoga, pilates, dance, cycling, functional fitness
- **Personal Training Services**: One-on-one coaching and programming
- **Group Fitness Classes**: Structured fitness programs for multiple participants
- **Sports Performance Training**: Athletic training and conditioning
- **Wellness Centers**: Holistic health and fitness services
- **Corporate Fitness**: Employer-sponsored fitness programs
- **Digital Fitness**: Virtual classes, apps, and online coaching

### Market Segments

#### Commercial Health Clubs
- Full-service gyms with diverse equipment
- Group fitness class offerings
- Personal training services
- Aquatic facilities
- Childcare and amenities

#### Specialized Fitness Studios
- Yoga and pilates studios
- CrossFit boxes and functional fitness
- Cycling and spin studios
- Martial arts and boxing
- Dance and movement studios

#### Training Services
- Personal training sessions
- Small group training
- Sport-specific coaching
- Athletic conditioning
- Nutrition and wellness counseling

#### Corporate & Workplace Fitness
- On-site fitness facilities
- Wellness programs
- Fitness classes and coaching
- Health incentives and tracking

## Industry Trends

- **Wellness Integration**: Holistic health focus
- **Wearable Technology**: Integration with fitness apps and trackers

## Key Performance Indicators

### Membership Metrics
- **Member Count**: Total active members
- **Member Growth Rate**: Rate of new member acquisition
- **Retention Rate**: Percentage of members renewing
- **Churn Rate**: Percentage of members canceling
- **Member Lifetime Value**: Total revenue per member

### Financial Metrics
- **Revenue Per Available Seat (RAPM)**: Revenue efficiency
- **Average Revenue Per Member (ARPM)**: Member revenue
- **Operating Margin**: Profitability percentage
- **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)**: Cost to gain new member
- **Break-even Capacity**: Utilization needed for profitability

### Operational Metrics
- **Class Utilization**: Percentage of seats filled
- **Peak Hour Utilization**: Maximum capacity usage
- **Facility Utilization**: Overall facility usage
- **Staff Productivity**: Revenue per staff member
- **Member Engagement**: Class attendance frequency
