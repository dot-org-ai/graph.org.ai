## Facility Profile

### Overview

Sports facilities represent the physical infrastructure essential to the sports industry. These include:

- **Stadiums & Arenas**: Large-capacity venues for professional teams and major events
- **Training Centers**: Specialized facilities for athlete development and coaching
- **Specialty Facilities**: Sport-specific venues (ice rinks, golf courses, tennis courts, swimming pools)
- **Multi-Use Complexes**: Facilities hosting multiple sports and events
- **Youth & Development Facilities**: Training grounds for amateur and youth sports
- **Fitness & Recreation Centers**: Facilities supporting fitness and recreational sports

### Facility Categories

#### Major League Stadiums & Arenas
- Capacity: 10,000+
- Multi-sport and event hosting
- Premium amenities and hospitality
- Advanced technology and security systems
- LEED certifications and sustainability features

#### Training Centers & Academies
- Focused on player development
- Sport-specific equipment and courts
- Strength and conditioning facilities
- Sports science and medical facilities
- Video analysis and technology integration

#### Specialty Sports Facilities
- Ice rinks for hockey and skating
- Golf courses and driving ranges
- Tennis courts and racquet clubs
- Swimming pools and diving facilities
- Climbing walls and extreme sports facilities
