## Sports Technology Profile

### Overview

Sports technology encompasses software, hardware, platforms, and services that enhance athletic performance, fan engagement, and sports business operations. This includes:

- **Performance Analytics**: Player statistics, performance tracking, and game analysis
- **Wearable Technology**: Fitness trackers, smartwatches, biometric sensors
- **Video Analysis**: Game footage analysis, coaching tools, motion capture
- **Fantasy Sports**: Fantasy games and predictions platforms
- **Sports Betting**: Betting analytics and odds platforms
- **Fan Engagement**: Apps, gamification, and community platforms
- **Facility Management**: Arena operations and automation
- **Training Systems**: AI-powered coaching and skill development
- **Broadcasting Technology**: Production tools and distribution platforms
