## Sports Organization Profile

### Overview

Professional sports organizations operate at the highest levels of competitive athletics. This includes:

- **Major League Teams**: Professional franchises in established leagues (NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, MLS, etc.)
- **League Operations**: Governing bodies that oversee teams, competition, player contracts, and revenues
- **Professional Athletes**: Elite competitors who earn primary income through sports
- **Sports Management**: Agent representation, player development, contract negotiation
- **Revenue Streams**: Ticket sales, broadcasting rights, sponsorships, merchandise, stadium operations

### Business Model

Professional sports organizations generate revenue through:

- **Media Rights**: Broadcasting contracts (local, national, international)
- **Ticket Sales**: Individual game tickets, season passes, premium seating
- **Sponsorships**: Corporate partnerships and brand endorsements
- **Merchandise**: Team apparel, equipment, collectibles
- **Facility Revenue**: Stadium concessions, parking, premium experiences
- **Digital Revenue**: Fantasy sports, apps, streaming, online communities
