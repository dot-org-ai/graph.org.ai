### ProfessionalSports.mdx
**Schema Type**: https://schema.org.ai/SportsOrganization
**NAICS Code**: 7112 - Spectator Sports
**Best For**: Professional sports leagues, teams, franchises

**Key Topics**:
- Organization structure and leadership
- Athlete and player management
- Revenue models and financials
- Broadcasting and media rights
- Sponsorships and partnerships
- Competition records and playoff history

**Template Variables**:
- `sportType` - Football, basketball, baseball, etc.
- `organizationType` - League, team, franchise
- `league` - League name (NFL, NBA, etc.)
- `owner` - Ownership information

**Use Case**: Model a professional sports team or league

## Getting Started Guide

### Step 1: Choose Your Category
Identify which sports sector you want to document:
- Professional leagues/teams → **ProfessionalSports.mdx**
- Sports venues → **SportsFacilities.mdx**
- Media organizations → **SportsMedia.mdx**
- Technology companies → **SportsTech.mdx**
- Fitness centers → **FitnessIndustry.mdx**
- Equipment manufacturers → **SportsEquipment.mdx**

### Step 2: Review the Template
Open the corresponding MDX file and review:
- The frontmatter structure
- Template variables needed
- Component sections included

### Step 3: Prepare Your Data
Gather information for all template variables:
- Organization/facility name and description
- Founding/opening year
- Location and contact information
- Key personnel and structure
- Financial information
- Relevant NAICS codes

### Step 4: Customize the Template
Fill in template variables:
```mdx
{name} → Your Entity Name
{description} → Your Description
{founded} → 1995
{location} → City, State
```

### Step 5: Populate Components
Add specific data to relevant components:
- Update entity attributes
- Fill in component properties
- Add cross-references
