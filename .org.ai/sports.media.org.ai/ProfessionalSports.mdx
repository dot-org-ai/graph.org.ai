# Professional Sports

Professional sports encompasses organized competitive sports leagues, teams, athletes, and the business ecosystem surrounding elite-level athletic competition for spectator entertainment.

## Overview

Professional sports represents the pinnacle of athletic competition, combining elite performance with entertainment, media, and business. The industry includes major professional leagues, teams, individual athletes, governing bodies, and the infrastructure supporting competitive sports. Professional sports generates revenue primarily through media rights, ticket sales, sponsorships, and merchandise, creating a multi-billion dollar global industry that captivates audiences worldwide.

## Major Professional Sports Leagues

### North American Team Sports ("Big Four")

#### National Football League (NFL)
- **Founded**: 1920
- **Teams**: 32 (across 8 divisions)
- **Season**: September-February (17-game regular season + playoffs)
- **Championship**: Super Bowl (most-watched annual sporting event globally)
- **Revenue**: $18B+ annually (2023)
- **Average Team Value**: $5.1B
- **Key Metrics**:
  - 18M+ attendance annually
  - 100M+ Super Bowl viewership (US)
  - $110B+ media rights deals (2023-2033)

**Structure**:
- American Football Conference (AFC) - 16 teams
- National Football Conference (NFC) - 16 teams
- Divisions: North, South, East, West (4 teams each)

**Business Model**:
- Revenue sharing among teams
- Salary cap: $224M per team (2023)
- Media rights: CBS, NBC, Fox, ESPN, Amazon Prime
- NFL Sunday Ticket exclusivity

#### National Basketball Association (NBA)
- **Founded**: 1946
- **Teams**: 30 (across 6 divisions)
- **Season**: October-June (82-game regular season + playoffs)
- **Championship**: NBA Finals
- **Revenue**: $10B+ annually
- **Average Team Value**: $3.8B
- **Key Metrics**:
  - 22M+ attendance annually
  - 195+ countries broadcast reach
  - $76B media rights deals (2025-2036)

**Structure**:
- Eastern Conference - 15 teams
- Western Conference - 15 teams
- Divisions: Atlantic, Central, Southeast, Northwest, Pacific, Southwest

**Global Presence**:
- NBA China partnership
- Basketball Africa League (BAL)
- International player pipeline
- Summer League and preseason games abroad

#### Major League Baseball (MLB)
- **Founded**: 1869 (National League), 1901 (American League)
- **Teams**: 30 (across 6 divisions)
- **Season**: April-October (162-game regular season + playoffs)
- **Championship**: World Series
- **Revenue**: $11B+ annually
- **Average Team Value**: $2.4B
- **Key Metrics**:
  - 70M+ attendance annually (highest of major sports)
  - 2,430 games per season
  - Regional sports network model

**Structure**:
- American League (AL) - 15 teams
- National League (NL) - 15 teams
- Divisions: East, Central, West (5 teams each)

**Unique Aspects**:
- No salary cap (luxury tax system)
- Minor league farm system
- International amateur signing
- Longest season and most games

#### National Hockey League (NHL)
- **Founded**: 1917
- **Teams**: 32 (across 8 divisions)
- **Season**: October-June (82-game regular season + playoffs)
- **Championship**: Stanley Cup Finals
- **Revenue**: $6B+ annually
- **Average Team Value**: $1.3B
- **Key Metrics**:
  - 22M+ attendance annually
  - Strong Canadian and northern US presence
  - Growing southern and western markets

**Structure**:
- Eastern Conference - 16 teams
- Western Conference - 16 teams
- Divisions: Atlantic, Metropolitan, Central, Pacific

**International Reach**:
- European player development
- Winter Olympics participation (intermittently)
- NHL Global Series (international games)

## Athlete Ecosystem

### Player Contracts & Compensation

#### Salary Structures

**NFL**:
- Salary cap: $224M per team (2023)
- Average salary: $2.8M
- Median salary: $860K
- Top QB contracts: $50M+ annually
- Guaranteed money limited (injury risk)
- Shorter careers (3.3 years average)

**NBA**:
- Salary cap: $136M per team (2023-24)
- Average salary: $10.8M (highest of team sports)
- Max contracts: $50M+ annually
- Designated rookie extensions
- Bird rights and luxury tax

**MLB**:
- No salary cap (luxury tax)
- Average salary: $4.9M
- Arbitration system (3+ years service)
- Free agency (6+ years)
- Mega contracts: $400M-$700M total value
- Deferred compensation common

**NHL**:
- Salary cap: $83.5M per team (2023-24)
- Average salary: $3.5M
- Entry-level contracts
- Restricted and unrestricted free agency
- Guaranteed contracts

#### International Soccer
- Transfer fees: €100M+ for elite players
- Wages: €20M-€50M annually (top stars)
- Agent fees: 10-20% of transfers
- Contract buyout clauses
- Loan systems
