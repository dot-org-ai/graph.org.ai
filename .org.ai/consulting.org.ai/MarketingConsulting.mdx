# Marketing Consulting

Marketing consulting helps organizations develop and execute effective marketing strategies, build strong brands, create exceptional customer experiences, and leverage digital technologies to drive growth. Marketing consultants bring expertise in brand strategy, customer insights, digital marketing, marketing technology, and data-driven decision making to help clients achieve their marketing objectives and maximize return on marketing investment.

## Overview

Marketing has undergone a dramatic transformation driven by digital technologies, changing consumer behavior, data analytics, and the proliferation of channels and touchpoints. Modern marketing consulting encompasses traditional strategic marketing combined with digital expertise, customer experience design, marketing technology implementation, and performance analytics to help organizations navigate the complex and rapidly evolving marketing landscape.

### Key Characteristics

- **Customer-Centric**: Deep understanding of customer needs, behaviors, and journeys
- **Data-Driven**: Analytics and measurement to optimize marketing performance
- **Omnichannel**: Integrated experiences across all customer touchpoints
- **Technology-Enabled**: Marketing technology stack optimization and automation
- **Creative and Strategic**: Balancing creative excellence with business strategy

## Marketing Frameworks and Models

### Marketing Strategy Frameworks
- **STP**: Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning
- **4Ps/7Ps**: Product, Price, Place, Promotion (+ People, Process, Physical Evidence)
- **AIDA**: Awareness, Interest, Desire, Action
- **RACE**: Reach, Act, Convert, Engage

### Customer Journey Frameworks
- **5As**: Aware, Appeal, Ask, Act, Advocate
- **Flywheel Model**: Attract, Engage, Delight
- **Customer Decision Journey**: Consider, Evaluate, Buy, Experience, Advocate

### Digital Marketing Models
- **See-Think-Do-Care**: Google's marketing framework
- **PESO Model**: Paid, Earned, Shared, Owned media
- **Hub-and-Spoke**: Content marketing model
