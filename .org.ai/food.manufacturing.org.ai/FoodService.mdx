# Food Services and Drinking Places (NAICS 722)

The food service industry encompasses restaurants, cafeterias, caterers, and bars that prepare and serve food and beverages for immediate consumption. This sector is a vital part of the U.S. economy, employing over 15 million workers and generating nearly $900 billion in annual sales. Food service is characterized by labor-intensive operations, thin profit margins, high customer interaction, and continuous adaptation to consumer preferences and dining trends.

## Overview

Food service establishments range from quick-service fast food to fine dining restaurants, from workplace cafeterias to wedding caterers, and from coffee shops to nightclubs. The industry serves as a social and cultural hub while providing essential meal solutions for busy consumers who increasingly eat meals prepared outside the home.

## Industry Segments

### 7221 - Full-Service Restaurants

Full-service restaurants provide food and beverage service to patrons who order and are served while seated at tables. Orders are taken by servers, food is prepared to order, and servers deliver meals to the table.

**Casual Dining**
- **Characteristics**: Moderate prices ($10-$25 per person), relaxed atmosphere, full menu variety
- **Examples**: Applebee's, Chili's, TGI Friday's, Olive Garden, Red Lobster, Outback Steakhouse
- **Service Model**: Table service, full bar, family-friendly, suburban locations
- **Trends**: Competition from fast-casual, value perception challenges, emphasis on to-go and delivery

**Fine Dining**
- **Characteristics**: High prices ($50-$200+ per person), upscale atmosphere, sophisticated cuisine
- **Service**: Formal table service, sommeliers, multi-course meals, reservations required
- **Examples**: Ruth's Chris, Morton's, Capital Grille, independent chef-driven restaurants
- **Differentiators**: Quality ingredients, culinary expertise, wine programs, special occasion dining

**Family Dining**
- **Characteristics**: Budget-friendly ($8-$18 per person), all-ages appeal, extensive menus
- **Examples**: Denny's, IHOP, Cracker Barrel, Bob Evans, Friendly's
- **Features**: Breakfast all day, children's menus, senior discounts, high-volume operations

**Ethnic/Specialty Restaurants**
- **Italian**: Pizza, pasta, regional Italian cuisines
- **Mexican**: Tacos, burritos, regional Mexican specialties
- **Asian**: Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Vietnamese, Korean, Indian
- **Steakhouses**: Prime cuts, sides, upscale atmosphere
- **Seafood**: Fresh fish, shellfish, coastal cuisines

**Independent Restaurants**
- **Chef-Owned**: Culinary creativity, local sourcing, unique concepts
- **Neighborhood Restaurants**: Community-focused, regular clientele, local identity
- **Challenges**: Capital access, marketing, purchasing power, brand recognition
- **Advantages**: Flexibility, authenticity, community connection, culinary innovation

### 7222 - Limited-Service Restaurants

Limited-service establishments where customers order at a counter or select items from a cafeteria line, pay before eating, and typically receive orders for carryout or at a counter/table without full table service.

**Quick-Service Restaurants (QSR)**
- **Fast Food Burgers**: McDonald's, Burger King, Wendy's, Five Guys, Shake Shack
- **Pizza**: Domino's, Pizza Hut, Papa John's, Little Caesars
- **Chicken**: KFC, Chick-fil-A, Popeyes, Raising Cane's, Wingstop
- **Mexican**: Taco Bell, Chipotle, Qdoba, Del Taco, Moe's
- **Sandwiches**: Subway, Jimmy John's, Jersey Mike's, Firehouse Subs
- **Coffee**: Starbucks, Dunkin', Tim Hortons, Peet's Coffee
- **Characteristics**: Low prices ($5-$12), speed of service, standardization, drive-thru
- **Business Model**: High volume, low margin, franchising, real estate strategy

**Fast-Casual**
- **Definition**: Higher quality than fast food, limited service, moderate prices ($10-$20)
- **Examples**: Panera Bread, Chipotle, Sweetgreen, CAVA, Noodles & Company
- **Features**: Fresh ingredients, customization, contemporary decor, healthier options
- **Service**: Order at counter, food brought to table or counter pickup, digital ordering
- **Growth Drivers**: Health-conscious consumers, millennial preferences, better ingredients perception

**Cafeterias and Buffets**
- **Cafeterias**: Self-service lines with variety of hot and cold options
- **Buffets**: All-you-can-eat format with multiple food stations (Golden Corral, Old Country Buffet)
- **Institutional**: Workplace, hospital, university cafeterias
- **Declining Segment**: Competition from fast-casual, health concerns, pandemic impact

**Snack and Beverage Bars**
- **Coffee Shops**: Espresso-based drinks, pastries, breakfast items, remote work hubs
- **Juice Bars**: Fresh-pressed juices, smoothies, açai bowls, health-focused
- **Ice Cream/Frozen Desserts**: Baskin-Robbins, Cold Stone, Dairy Queen, gelato shops
- **Bakery Cafes**: Fresh baked goods with coffee and light meals

### 7223 - Special Food Services

**Caterers**
- **Event Catering**: Weddings, corporate events, parties, galas
- **Service Styles**: Plated dinners, buffets, food stations, passed hors d'oeuvres
- **Operations**: Off-site kitchens, mobile equipment, event setup and breakdown
- **Revenue Model**: Per-person pricing, minimum guarantees, rentals, staffing
- **Specializations**: Corporate, social events, dietary accommodations (kosher, halal, vegan)

**Food Service Contractors**
- **Workplace Dining**: Office buildings, corporate campuses, factory cafeterias
- **Healthcare**: Hospital cafeterias, patient meal service, dietary services
- **Education**: School cafeterias, university dining halls, meal plans
- **Corrections**: Prison and jail food service
- **Major Companies**: Aramark, Compass Group, Sodexo
- **Contract Types**: Management contracts, concession agreements, cost-plus

**Mobile Food Services**
- **Food Trucks**: Gourmet trucks, ethnic cuisines, lunch crowds, festivals
- **Mobile Vendors**: Hot dog carts, coffee carts, ice cream trucks
- **Pop-Up Kitchens**: Temporary installations, ghost kitchens, delivery-only
- **Regulations**: Permits, health inspections, parking restrictions, commissary requirements

### 7224 - Drinking Places (Alcoholic Beverages)

**Bars and Taverns**
- **Neighborhood Bars**: Local gathering places, regulars, sports viewing
- **Sports Bars**: Multiple TVs, game day specials, pub food
- **Craft Beer Bars**: Rotating taps, beer-focused, knowledgeable staff
- **Wine Bars**: Curated wine lists, small plates, upscale atmosphere

**Nightclubs and Lounges**
- **Dance Clubs**: DJs, dancing, late-night hours, VIP sections
- **Cocktail Lounges**: Craft cocktails, mixology, upscale ambiance
- **Live Music Venues**: Cover charges, drink minimums, entertainment focus

**Brewpubs and Taprooms**
- **Brewpubs**: On-site brewing with full food service
- **Taprooms**: Brewery-owned tasting rooms, limited food (often food trucks)
- **Regulations**: Production licenses, self-distribution limits, on-premise consumption

## Business Operations

### Front of House (FOH)

**Guest Experience**
- **Greeting and Seating**: Host/hostess managing waitlists, table assignments
- **Order Taking**: Servers, tablets, kiosks, mobile apps
- **Service Delivery**: Food runners, expo coordination, timing
- **Payment Processing**: POS systems, mobile payments, tableside payments
- **Table Management**: Turning tables efficiently, pacing courses

**Staffing Roles**
- **General Manager**: Overall operations, P&L responsibility, staff management
- **Front of House Manager**: FOH operations, staff scheduling, customer service
- **Servers**: Taking orders, serving food/beverages, upselling, tips
- **Bartenders**: Cocktails, beer, wine service, bar management
- **Hosts/Hostesses**: Guest reception, reservations, seating coordination
- **Bussers**: Table clearing, setting, maintaining dining room cleanliness

### Back of House (BOH)

**Kitchen Operations**
- **Prep Kitchen**: Vegetable prep, butchering, sauce making, batch cooking
- **Hot Line**: Sauté, grill, fry stations for à la carte cooking
- **Cold Line**: Salads, appetizers, desserts
- **Expo (Expeditor)**: Quality control, coordinating tickets, communication with FOH
- **Dishwashing**: Ware washing, equipment sanitization, kitchen cleanliness

**Kitchen Staffing**
- **Executive Chef**: Menu development, kitchen management, food cost control
- **Sous Chef**: Assistant to executive chef, line supervision, ordering
- **Line Cooks**: Station cooking (sauté, grill, fry, pantry)
- **Prep Cooks**: Ingredient preparation, recipe execution
- **Dishwashers**: Cleaning dishes, pots, pans, kitchen equipment

**Food Production**
- **Mise en Place**: Ingredient preparation, portioning, organizing stations
- **Cooking to Order**: Preparing dishes as orders come in
- **Quality Standards**: Consistency, portion control, presentation
- **Food Safety**: Temperature control, cross-contamination prevention, hygiene
- **Speed of Service**: Ticket times, kitchen display systems, communication

### Supply Chain and Purchasing

**Food Procurement**
- **Broadline Distributors**: Sysco, US Foods, Performance Food Group (one-stop shopping)
- **Specialty Distributors**: Seafood, produce, meat, ethnic ingredients
- **Direct Sourcing**: Local farms, farmers markets, direct from manufacturers
- **Purchasing Strategies**: Par levels, inventory management, cost control, quality standards

**Inventory Management**
- **Storage**: Dry storage, walk-in refrigeration, walk-in freezers
- **FIFO**: First-in, first-out rotation to minimize waste
- **Inventory Counts**: Regular cycle counts, variance analysis
- **Waste Tracking**: Spoilage, overproduction, trim waste, compost programs

### Technology Systems

**Point of Sale (POS)**
- **Order Entry**: Touchscreen terminals, mobile handheld devices
- **Kitchen Display Systems (KDS)**: Digital ticket management, cook times
- **Payment Processing**: Credit cards, mobile payments, contactless, split checks
- **Reporting**: Sales analysis, labor costs, product mix, server performance
- **Major Providers**: Toast, Square, Clover, Aloha, Micros Oracle

**Reservation and Waitlist**
- **OpenTable**: Online reservations, table management, guest database
- **Resy**: Reservations with VIP management and waitlist
- **Yelp Waitlist**: Virtual waitlist, text notifications
- **Tableside**: iPad-based waitlist and table management

**Online Ordering and Delivery**
- **Third-Party Platforms**: DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, Postmates
- **Direct Ordering**: Restaurant websites and mobile apps
- **Ghost Kitchens**: Delivery-only virtual brands, shared kitchen facilities
- **Commission Challenges**: 15-30% fees, margin pressure, customer ownership

**Back Office Systems**
- **Scheduling**: 7shifts, HotSchedules, Deputy, When I Work
- **Accounting**: QuickBooks, Restaurant365, accounting integration
- **Recipe and Menu Management**: Costing software, menu engineering
- **Payroll**: ADP, Paychex, integrated timeclock systems

## Financial Performance

### Revenue Streams
- **Food Sales**: 70-80% of revenue in full-service, 90%+ in limited-service
- **Beverage Sales**: Alcoholic (bars, full-service) and non-alcoholic
- **Catering and Events**: Off-premise sales, private dining
- **Delivery and Takeout**: Growing channel, lower margins due to commissions
- **Merchandise**: Branded items, retail products (sauces, coffee beans)

### Cost Structure

**Prime Costs (Food + Labor = 60-70% of sales)**

**Food and Beverage Costs**
- **Target**: 28-35% of sales (varies by concept)
- **Quick Service**: 25-30% (limited menus, standardization)
- **Casual Dining**: 30-35% (variety, higher quality)
- **Fine Dining**: 32-38% (premium ingredients, waste)
- **Controllable Through**: Purchasing, portioning, waste reduction, menu engineering

**Labor Costs**
- **Target**: 25-35% of sales (varies significantly by service level)
- **Quick Service**: 25-30% (limited service, automation)
- **Casual/Fast-Casual**: 30-35% (servers, kitchen staff)
- **Fine Dining**: 35-40% (high service ratios, skilled staff)
- **Components**: Wages, benefits, payroll taxes, workers compensation, training

**Operating Expenses**
- **Occupancy**: Rent (6-10%), utilities (3-5%), property taxes, insurance
- **Marketing**: Advertising, promotions, local store marketing (2-5%)
- **Supplies**: Disposables, smallwares, cleaning supplies, uniforms (3-5%)
- **Repairs and Maintenance**: Equipment, facility maintenance (2-3%)
- **Credit Card Fees**: 2-3% of revenue (higher for third-party delivery)

**Profitability**
- **EBITDA Margins**: 10-20% (well-operated concepts)
- **Net Profit Margins**: 3-8% (after all expenses and taxes)
- **Franchise**: Franchisees often 5-10% operating profit, royalties to franchisor (4-8%)

## Regulatory Compliance

### Food Safety
- **FDA Food Code**: Model code adopted by states and localities
- **Health Inspections**: Regular unannounced visits, critical violations, risk ratings
- **Food Handler Training**: State requirements, ServSafe, Food Handler certifications
- **Temperature Control**: Hot holding (135°F+), cold holding (41°F or below)
- **HACCP Principles**: Hazard analysis for critical menu items

### Licensing and Permits
- **Business License**: Local city/county requirements
- **Health Permit**: Department of health approval to operate
- **Liquor License**: State and local alcohol beverage control (costly, limited availability)
- **Signage Permits**: Exterior signage approval
- **Music Licensing**: ASCAP, BMI, SESAC for copyrighted music
- **Fire Safety**: Occupancy limits, suppression systems, inspections

### Employment Regulations
- **Minimum Wage**: Federal, state, local (tipped employees have lower cash wages)
- **Tip Credit**: Difference between tipped minimum and regular minimum wage
- **Overtime**: Time-and-a-half for hours over 40 per week
- **Tip Pooling**: Legal arrangements for sharing tips among staff
- **Labor Law Posters**: Required workplace postings
- **Workers Compensation**: Insurance for work-related injuries

### Accessibility
- **ADA Compliance**: Accessible entrances, restrooms, seating
- **Service Animals**: Accommodations for disability service animals
- **Menu Accessibility**: Large print, braille, online accessible menus

## Industry Trends

### Digital Transformation
- **Mobile Ordering**: Apps for advance ordering, curbside pickup
- **Self-Service Kiosks**: McDonald's, Panera reducing counter transactions
- **Contactless Payment**: Apple Pay, Google Pay, tap-to-pay cards
- **QR Code Menus**: Digital menus accessed via smartphone (accelerated by COVID-19)
- **AI and Automation**: Drive-thru voice AI, robotic cooking, predictive analytics

### Delivery and Off-Premise
- **Third-Party Delivery**: 60%+ of consumers use delivery apps
- **Virtual Brands**: Ghost kitchens, delivery-only concepts, multi-brand operations
- **Packaging Innovation**: Maintaining food quality in transit, sustainable packaging
- **Direct Delivery**: Restaurant-owned delivery to avoid third-party fees

### Health and Wellness
- **Plant-Based Options**: Beyond Meat, Impossible Foods, vegetable-forward dishes
- **Dietary Accommodations**: Gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, allergen awareness
- **Calorie Transparency**: Calorie counts on menus (required for chains 20+ locations)
- **Clean Label**: Simple ingredients, no artificial colors/flavors, transparency
- **Customization**: Build-your-own bowls, dietary modifications, personalization

### Sustainability
- **Local Sourcing**: Farm-to-table, supporting local agriculture, seasonal menus
- **Waste Reduction**: Composting, food waste tracking, donation programs
- **Sustainable Seafood**: MSC certified, aquaculture best practices
- **Packaging**: Compostable, recyclable, reusable containers
- **Energy Efficiency**: LED lighting, Energy Star equipment, water conservation

### Experience and Atmosphere
- **Instagrammable Design**: Photo-worthy interiors, shareable moments
- **Open Kitchens**: Transparency, entertainment, chef interaction
- **Hybrid Concepts**: Restaurant/retail, bar/coffee shop combinations
- **Outdoor Dining**: Patios, sidewalk cafes, rooftops (expanded post-pandemic)

## Challenges

### Labor
- **Worker Shortage**: Difficulty finding and retaining staff at all levels
- **High Turnover**: 70-100% annual turnover, constant recruiting and training
- **Wage Pressure**: Minimum wage increases, competition for workers
- **Burnout**: Long hours, physical demands, stressful environment
- **Benefits**: Limited healthcare, paid time off, retirement benefits

### Competition
- **Saturation**: High number of restaurants per capita, competition for customers
- **Delivery Apps**: Competing with convenience of home delivery
- **Grocery Prepared Foods**: Meal kits, ready-to-eat meals, rotisserie chicken
- **Home Cooking**: Pandemic increased home cooking skills and habits

### Economic Sensitivity
- **Discretionary Spending**: Vulnerable to recession, unemployment, consumer confidence
- **Rising Costs**: Food inflation, labor costs, rent increases, utilities
- **Thin Margins**: Limited ability to absorb cost increases without price raises
- **Value Perception**: Consumer sensitivity to price increases

### Operational Complexity
- **Consistency**: Maintaining quality across locations, shifts, staff changes
- **Supply Chain**: Ingredient shortages, price volatility, delivery reliability
- **Technology Integration**: Managing multiple systems, training staff, tech costs
- **Seasonality**: Sales fluctuations by day of week, season, weather, holidays
