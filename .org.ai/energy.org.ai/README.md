## Industry Overview

The energy sector encompasses multiple interconnected industries:

### Oil & Gas Value Chain
- **Upstream**: Exploration & Production (E&P) - finding and extracting crude oil and natural gas
- **Midstream**: Transportation, storage, and wholesale marketing via pipelines, tankers, rail
- **Downstream**: Refining crude oil into products, petrochemical manufacturing, distribution, retail

### Utilities
- **Electric Power**: Generation (thermal, nuclear, renewable), transmission, distribution
- **Natural Gas**: Pipeline networks, distribution, storage, LNG facilities
- **Water & Sewer**: Collection, treatment, distribution, wastewater management

### Renewables & Storage
- **Solar**: Photovoltaic, concentrated solar power (CSP)
- **Wind**: Onshore and offshore wind farms
- **Hydro**: Conventional dams, pumped storage, run-of-river
- **Storage**: Batteries, hydrogen, thermal storage, mechanical storage

### Mining
- **Energy Minerals**: Coal, uranium, oil sands
- **Metal Ores**: Iron, copper, gold, rare earths
- **Nonmetallic Minerals**: Sand, gravel, limestone, industrial minerals
