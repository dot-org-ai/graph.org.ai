# Mobile Robots

Mobile robots are autonomous or semi-autonomous vehicles capable of navigation through warehouses, factories, hospitals, and outdoor environments. They represent a critical technology for modern logistics, manufacturing, and service delivery, with the market expected to exceed $50 billion by 2030.

## Overview

Mobile robotics has evolved from simple guided vehicles following magnetic strips to sophisticated autonomous systems using computer vision, LiDAR, and AI for dynamic navigation. These systems are transforming material handling, reducing labor costs, and enabling 24/7 operations in logistics and manufacturing.

## Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs)

AGVs follow predetermined paths using various guidance technologies. They represent the mature, established segment of mobile robotics with over 50 years of deployment history.

### Guidance Technologies

**Magnetic Tape/Wire Guidance**
- Magnetic tape applied to floor surface
- Sensors detect magnetic field to follow path
- Low cost, reliable, easy to install
- Path changes require physical tape relocation
- Common in assembly lines, warehouse transport

**Laser Navigation**
- Reflective targets positioned around facility
- Laser scanner triangulates position
- Accurate to ±10mm
- Flexible path programming
- Higher initial cost but no floor modifications

**Inertial Navigation**
- Gyroscopes and wheel encoders track position
- Requires periodic correction via transponders or reflectors
- Works well in structured environments
- Less affected by floor conditions

**Vision Guidance**
- Cameras detect natural features or markers
- AI-based feature recognition
- No infrastructure modifications required
- Sensitive to lighting conditions

### AGV Types

**Tow Vehicles**
- Pull carts with payloads up to 60,000kg
- Common in automotive manufacturing
- Multiple cart train configurations
- Automatic hitch engagement

**Unit Load Carriers**
- Transport pallets, carts, or racks
- Payloads 500kg-3,000kg
- Fork-style or conveyor loading
- Integration with automated storage

**Pallet Trucks**
- Automated pallet jacks
- Payloads up to 2,500kg
- Self-loading capability
- Narrow aisle navigation

**Assembly Line Vehicles**
- Synchronize with production line speed
- Precise positioning for work operations
- Worker-following modes
- Tool and part delivery

## Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs)

AMRs represent the next generation of mobile robotics, featuring dynamic navigation without fixed paths. They use onboard sensors and AI to perceive their environment and plan optimal routes in real-time.

### Navigation Technology

**Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM)**
- Robot builds map while tracking its location
- LiDAR, cameras, or fusion of multiple sensors
- Handles dynamic obstacles and environment changes
- No infrastructure installation required

**Sensor Suite**
- 2D/3D LiDAR for obstacle detection (360° scanning)
- Depth cameras for 3D perception
- Ultrasonic sensors for close-range detection
- Wheel encoders and IMU for odometry
- Safety laser scanners meeting ISO 3691-4

**Path Planning Algorithms**
- A* and D* Lite for optimal path calculation
- Dynamic Window Approach for real-time obstacle avoidance
- Velocity obstacles for multi-robot coordination
- Learning-based approaches using reinforcement learning

### AMR Capabilities

**Dynamic Navigation**
- Real-time route optimization
- Automatic rerouting around obstacles
- Elevator and door integration
- Multi-floor navigation

**Fleet Management**
- Centralized traffic control
- Task allocation and prioritization
- Automatic charging coordination
- Load balancing across robots

**Safety Features**
- Emergency stop systems
- Redundant safety sensors
- Collision avoidance algorithms
- Compliance with safety standards (ANSI/ITSDF B56.5)

## Warehouse Automation Applications

**Benefits**
- Picker productivity increased 2-4x

- Reduced order fulfillment time by 50-70%
- Optimized storage density

**Implementation**
- Mobile robots retrieve storage pods or shelves
- Deliver to ergonomic picking stations
- Put-walls for batch picking efficiency
- Real-time inventory management

**Case Study: Amazon**
- 750,000+ mobile robots across fulfillment network
- 40% reduction in operational costs
- Same-day delivery capability enabled
- Seasonal scalability with robot fleet

### Piece Picking and Sortation

AMRs assist with individual item handling:

- Follow pickers through aisles
- Present totes for item placement
- Automatic sortation to shipping lanes
- Integration with automated bagging/packing

- Accuracy: 99.9%+ with barcode scanning
- ROI: 18-36 months typical payback
