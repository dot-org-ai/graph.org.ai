# Basic Chemicals (NAICS 3251)

Basic chemical manufacturing forms the foundation of the chemical industry, producing high-volume commodity chemicals that serve as building blocks for downstream chemical production and other manufacturing sectors.

## Overview

Basic chemicals are characterized by:
- **High production volumes** (millions of tons annually)
- **Commodity pricing** with tight margins (5-15%)
- **Capital-intensive operations** requiring billion-dollar facilities
- **Continuous processing** for efficiency and economics
- **Integration with petrochemical refineries** and natural gas processing
- **Global trade** with standardized specifications

The sector generates over $200 billion in annual U.S. shipments and employs 150,000+ workers in production facilities.

## NAICS 3251 Subsectors

### 32511 - Petrochemical Manufacturing

The foundation of the organic chemicals industry, converting petroleum and natural gas feedstocks into basic building blocks.

#### Primary Products

**Olefins (Alkenes)**
- **Ethylene (C₂H₄)**: Global production 200M+ tons/year
  - Polyethylene plastics (HDPE, LDPE, LLDPE)
  - Ethylene oxide → ethylene glycol (antifreeze, polyester)
  - Ethylbenzene → styrene → polystyrene
  - Vinyl chloride → PVC

- **Propylene (C₃H₆)**: 130M+ tons/year
  - Polypropylene plastics
  - Propylene oxide → polyurethanes
  - Acrylonitrile → acrylic fibers, ABS plastics
  - Cumene → phenol + acetone

- **Butadiene (C₄H₆)**: 15M+ tons/year
  - Synthetic rubber (SBR, polybutadiene)
  - ABS and SAN plastics
  - Nylon intermediates

**Aromatics**
- **Benzene (C₆H₆)**: 50M+ tons/year
  - Ethylbenzene → styrene
  - Cumene → phenol
  - Cyclohexane → nylon
  - Aniline → polyurethanes, dyes

- **Toluene (C₇H₈)**: 30M+ tons/year
  - Benzene production
  - Toluene diisocyanate (TDI) → polyurethanes
  - Solvents

- **Xylenes (C₈H₁₀)**: 50M+ tons/year
  - p-Xylene → purified terephthalic acid (PTA) → polyester (PET)
  - o-Xylene → phthalic anhydride → plasticizers
  - m-Xylene → isophthalic acid

#### Production Processes

**Steam Cracking (Pyrolysis)**
- Feed: Ethane, propane, naphtha, gas oil
- Temperature: 750-950°C
- Residence time: 0.1-0.5 seconds
- Yield optimization for ethylene/propylene ratio
- Energy intensity: 10-15 GJ/ton ethylene

**Catalytic Reforming**
- Naphtha upgrading for aromatics production
- Platinum/rhenium catalysts on alumina
- Pressure: 3-25 bar, Temperature: 450-550°C
- Produces benzene, toluene, xylenes (BTX)

**Fluid Catalytic Cracking (FCC)**
- Propylene production from refinery processes
- Zeolite catalysts
- Riser/regenerator design

### 32512 - Industrial Gas Manufacturing

Production and distribution of gases used in chemical processing, metal fabrication, healthcare, and electronics.

#### Major Industrial Gases

**Air Separation Products**
- **Oxygen (O₂)**: 300M+ tons/year globally
  - Steel production (blast furnaces, BOF)
  - Chemical oxidation reactions
  - Wastewater treatment
  - Medical/healthcare applications

- **Nitrogen (N₂)**: 400M+ tons/year
  - Inert atmosphere for chemical reactions
  - Food preservation and packaging
  - Metal heat treating
  - Enhanced oil recovery

- **Argon (Ar)**: 2M+ tons/year
  - Welding shielding gas
  - Semiconductor manufacturing
  - Specialty metallurgy

**Process Gases**
- **Hydrogen (H₂)**: 120M+ tons/year
  - Ammonia synthesis (Haber-Bosch)
  - Petroleum refining (hydrocracking, hydrotreating)
  - Methanol production
  - Fuel cells and energy storage
  - Sources: Steam methane reforming (SMR), electrolysis, as petroleum refinery byproduct

- **Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)**: 230M+ tons/year
  - Enhanced oil recovery (EOR)
  - Carbonation (beverages, chemicals)
  - Dry ice production
  - Supercritical CO₂ applications
  - Sources: Ammonia plants, ethanol fermentation, natural gas processing

**Specialty Gases**
- **Helium**: Cryogenics, MRI, leak detection, semiconductor manufacturing
- **Neon, Krypton, Xenon**: Lighting, lasers, electronics
- **Acetylene**: Welding, chemical synthesis
- **Nitrous Oxide**: Medical anesthesia, propellants

#### Production Technologies

**Cryogenic Air Separation**
- Liquefaction at -196°C (N₂) to -183°C (O₂)
- Fractional distillation in separation columns
- Purity: 99.5-99.999% depending on application
- Capacity: 500-5,000 tons/day per plant

**Pressure Swing Adsorption (PSA)**
- Nitrogen/oxygen separation using molecular sieves
- Lower purity (90-95%) but lower cost
- On-site generation for smaller users

**Membrane Separation**
- Polymeric or ceramic membranes
- Nitrogen generation from air
- Lower energy but limited purity

### 32513 - Synthetic Dye and Pigment Manufacturing

Production of organic and inorganic colorants for textiles, paints, plastics, printing, and specialty applications.

#### Dye Classifications

**By Application Method**
- **Reactive Dyes**: Form covalent bonds with cellulose (cotton, viscose)
  - Azo dyes with reactive groups (chlorotriazine, vinyl sulfone)
  - Excellent wash fastness

- **Disperse Dyes**: Polyester dyeing
  - Water-insoluble dyes dispersed as fine particles
  - High-temperature application (130°C)

- **Vat Dyes**: Cotton, indigo for denim
  - Water-insoluble, reduced to soluble form for dyeing
  - Outstanding lightfastness

- **Acid Dyes**: Wool, silk, nylon
  - Anionic dyes for protein fibers

- **Direct Dyes**: Cotton dyeing from aqueous solution
  - Lower cost but moderate fastness

**By Chemical Structure**
- **Azo Dyes** (60-70% of synthetic dyes)
  - Contain -N=N- chromophore
  - Synthesized via diazotization and coupling
  - Wide color range from yellow to black

- **Anthraquinone Dyes**
  - Red, blue, green shades
  - Excellent lightfastness

- **Phthalocyanine Dyes**
  - Brilliant blues and greens
  - Very stable structure

#### Pigment Types

**Inorganic Pigments**
- **Titanium Dioxide (TiO₂)**: 8M+ tons/year
  - White pigment for paints, plastics, paper
  - Anatase and rutile forms
  - Optical brightness and opacity

- **Iron Oxides**: Red, yellow, brown, black
  - Natural and synthetic
  - Construction, coatings, plastics

- **Chromium Compounds**: Greens, yellows
  - Chrome yellow (lead chromate)
  - Chrome oxide green

- **Carbon Black**: 15M+ tons/year
  - Tire reinforcement (70% of use)
  - Black pigment in inks, coatings

**Organic Pigments**
- **Azo Pigments**: Yellows, oranges, reds
  - Monoazo, diazo, naphthol AS

- **Phthalocyanine Pigments**: Blues and greens
  - Copper phthalocyanine
  - Exceptional stability

- **Quinacridone Pigments**: Reds, violets
  - High-performance applications

### 32518 - Other Basic Inorganic Chemical Manufacturing

Production of acids, alkalis, salts, and chlor-alkali products.

#### Major Products

**Chlor-Alkali Products** (75M+ tons/year globally)
- **Chlorine (Cl₂)**:
  - PVC production (vinyl chloride monomer)
  - Water treatment and disinfection
  - Bleaching agents
  - Intermediate for chlorinated organics

- **Caustic Soda (NaOH)**: Co-produced with chlorine
  - Pulp and paper (kraft process)
  - Chemical processing
  - Alumina refining
  - Soap and detergent manufacturing

- **Sodium Hypochlorite (NaOCl)**: Bleach
- **Hydrochloric Acid (HCl)**: By-product and synthesis

**Manufacturing Process**
- **Membrane Cell Technology** (most modern, 65% of capacity)
  - Ion-exchange membrane separates anode/cathode
  - High purity, energy efficient (2,000-2,400 kWh/ton Cl₂)

- **Diaphragm Cell** (30% of capacity)
  - Asbestos or polymer diaphragm
  - Lower purity caustic

- **Mercury Cell** (5% and declining)
  - Being phased out due to environmental concerns

**Sulfuric Acid (H₂SO₄)** (270M+ tons/year globally)
- World's largest volume chemical
- Uses:
  - Phosphate fertilizers (60%)
  - Chemical synthesis
  - Petroleum refining
  - Metal processing

- **Contact Process**:
  1. Sulfur combustion: S + O₂ → SO₂
  2. Catalytic oxidation: 2SO₂ + O₂ → 2SO₃ (V₂O₅ catalyst)
  3. Absorption in 98% H₂SO₄
  4. Dilution to commercial concentrations

**Nitric Acid (HNO₃)** (60M+ tons/year)
- Fertilizer production (ammonium nitrate)
- Explosives (TNT, nitroglycerin)
- Adipic acid (nylon 6,6)

- **Ostwald Process**:
  1. Ammonia oxidation: 4NH₃ + 5O₂ → 4NO + 6H₂O (Pt-Rh catalyst, 900°C)
  2. NO oxidation: 2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂
  3. Absorption: 3NO₂ + H₂O → 2HNO₃ + NO

**Phosphoric Acid (H₃PO₄)** (45M+ tons/year)
- Fertilizers (MAP, DAP)
- Food and beverage acidulant
- Detergents

- **Wet Process**: Phosphate rock + H₂SO₄
- **Thermal Process**: Elemental phosphorus combustion (higher purity)

**Ammonia (NH₃)** (180M+ tons/year)
- Fertilizers (80%): urea, ammonium nitrate, ammonium phosphates
- Explosives
- Nitric acid production
- Refrigeration

- **Haber-Bosch Process**:
  - N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ (ΔH = -92 kJ/mol)
  - Iron catalyst with K₂O, Al₂O₃ promoters
  - Pressure: 150-300 bar, Temperature: 400-500°C
  - Multiple recycle for conversion optimization

**Sodium Carbonate (Na₂CO₃) - Soda Ash** (60M+ tons/year)
- Glass manufacturing (50%)
- Detergents and chemicals
- Water treatment

- **Solvay Process**: NaCl + NH₃ + CO₂
- **Mining**: Trona ore (Wyoming, USA)

### 32519 - Other Basic Organic Chemical Manufacturing

Production of organic intermediates and solvents.

#### Major Product Categories

**Alcohols**
- **Methanol (CH₃OH)**: 110M+ tons/year
  - Formaldehyde production
  - Fuel additive (MTBE, biodiesel)
  - Acetic acid synthesis
  - Emerging fuel applications
  - Production: Synthesis gas (CO + 2H₂ → CH₃OH)

- **Ethanol (C₂H₅OH)**: 120M+ tons/year
  - Fuel ethanol (E10, E85)
  - Industrial solvent
  - Chemical intermediate
  - Production: Ethylene hydration, fermentation

- **Isopropanol**: Solvent, disinfectant, chemical intermediate
- **n-Butanol**: Coatings, plasticizers, intermediates
- **Ethylene Glycol**: Antifreeze, polyester (PET)

**Organic Acids**
- **Acetic Acid (CH₃COOH)**: 18M+ tons/year
  - Vinyl acetate monomer (VAM) → PVA, PVAc
  - Purified terephthalic acid synthesis
  - Cellulose acetate
  - Production: Methanol carbonylation (Monsanto/Cativa process)

- **Adipic Acid**: 3M+ tons/year
  - Nylon 6,6 production
  - Polyurethanes
  - Production: Cyclohexane oxidation

- **Terephthalic Acid (TPA)**: 60M+ tons/year
  - Polyester (PET) production
  - Production: p-xylene oxidation

**Ketones**
- **Acetone**: 7M+ tons/year
  - Methyl methacrylate (PMMA)
  - Bisphenol A (polycarbonate, epoxy)
  - Solvent
  - Production: Cumene process, propylene oxidation

**Aldehydes**
- **Formaldehyde (CH₂O)**: 50M+ tons/year
  - Phenol-formaldehyde resins
  - Urea-formaldehyde resins
  - Melamine resins
  - Production: Methanol oxidation

- **Acetaldehyde**: Acetic acid, pyridines

**Phenol and Derivatives**
- **Phenol (C₆H₅OH)**: 11M+ tons/year
  - Bisphenol A → polycarbonate, epoxy resins
  - Phenolic resins
  - Caprolactam → nylon 6
  - Production: Cumene process (cumene → phenol + acetone)

## Production Economics

### Cost Structure
- **Raw materials**: 50-70% of production cost
- **Energy**: 15-25% (especially for energy-intensive processes)
- **Labor**: 5-10%
- **Depreciation**: 10-15%
- **Other operating costs**: 5-10%

### Economies of Scale
- World-scale plants for cost competitiveness
  - Ethylene: 1-2 million tons/year
  - Ammonia: 2,000-3,500 tons/day
  - Chlor-alkali: 500,000+ tons/year
- Integration benefits: 20-30% cost advantage

### Regional Considerations
- **Middle East**: Low-cost natural gas feedstock
- **North America**: Shale gas advantage for ethane cracking
- **Asia**: Coal-to-chemicals in China
- **Europe**: Naphtha-based, higher cost structure

## Process Safety and Environmental Considerations

### Major Hazards
- **Highly hazardous chemicals** (OSHA PSM threshold quantities):
  - Chlorine: 1,500 lbs
  - Ammonia: 10,000 lbs
  - Ethylene oxide: 5,000 lbs
  - Hydrogen: 10,000 lbs (flammable gas)

### Safety Systems
- Emergency shutdown systems (ESD)
- Relief and flare systems
- Toxic gas detection and alarms
- Pressure relief devices
- Fireproofing and blast protection

### Environmental Controls
- **Air Emissions**:
  - VOC control (thermal oxidation, carbon adsorption)
  - NOx reduction (SCR, SNCR)
  - Particulate control (baghouses, ESPs)

- **Wastewater Treatment**:
  - Biological treatment
  - Chemical oxidation
  - Membrane separation

- **Waste Minimization**:
  - Process integration
  - Recycle and recovery
  - Yield improvement

## Industry Trends

### Technology Development
- **Shale gas impact**: Ethane cracking economics
- **On-purpose propylene**: Propane dehydrogenation (PDH), metathesis
- **CO₂ utilization**: Methanol, polymers, mineralization
- **Bio-based chemicals**: Bio-ethylene, bio-propylene from ethanol
- **Modular production**: Smaller-scale distributed manufacturing

### Sustainability Initiatives
- **Energy efficiency**: Heat integration, advanced process control
- **Emission reduction**: Zero-flare commitments, leak detection and repair (LDAR)
- **Circular economy**: Chemical recycling of plastics
- **Renewable feedstocks**: Bio-based alternatives to petroleum

### Market Dynamics
- Cyclical industry tied to GDP growth
- Regional cost advantages drive capacity additions
- Trade flows impacted by feedstock economics
- Consolidation and vertical integration trends
