# Physical Security

Physical security encompasses the protection of personnel, hardware, software, networks, and data from physical actions and events that could cause serious loss or damage to an enterprise, agency, or institution. This includes protection from fire, flood, natural disasters, burglary, theft, vandalism, and terrorism.

## Overview

Physical security services are classified under NAICS 56161 (Investigation, Guard, and Armored Car Services) and NAICS 56162 (Security Systems Services). These services form the foundation of comprehensive security programs by protecting physical assets, controlling access to facilities, and deterring unauthorized activities.

## Service Categories

### Security Guard Services

Professional security personnel provide on-site protection and monitoring:

- **Static Guards**: Fixed-position security at entry points, reception areas, or sensitive locations
- **Mobile Patrol**: Regular patrols of premises, parking areas, and perimeters
- **Event Security**: Crowd management and access control for events and gatherings
- **Executive Protection**: Close protection services for high-risk individuals
- **Loss Prevention**: Retail security focused on preventing theft and shrinkage
- **Concierge Security**: Customer service combined with security monitoring

**Key Functions**:
- Access control and visitor management
- Surveillance and monitoring
- Incident response and reporting
- Emergency procedures execution
- Asset protection
- Customer service and assistance

### Access Control Systems

Technology-based systems that regulate who can enter facilities and when:

**Physical Access Control Types**:
- **Card-based Systems**: RFID cards, magnetic stripe cards, proximity cards
- **Biometric Systems**: Fingerprint, facial recognition, iris scanning, palm vein
- **PIN/Keypad Systems**: Numeric code entry systems
- **Mobile Credentials**: Smartphone-based access via Bluetooth or NFC
- **Visitor Management**: Check-in systems with temporary credential issuance
- **Turnstiles and Barriers**: Physical barriers integrated with credential readers

**Features**:
- Real-time access monitoring and logging
- Time-based access rules
- Multi-factor authentication
- Integration with HR systems
- Audit trails and reporting
- Emergency lockdown capabilities
- Anti-passback prevention

### Video Surveillance (CCTV)

Comprehensive video monitoring and recording systems:

**Camera Technologies**:
- **IP Cameras**: Network-connected cameras with digital recording
- **Analog HD**: High-definition analog systems (HD-TVI, HD-CVI)
- **PTZ Cameras**: Pan-tilt-zoom cameras for active monitoring
- **Thermal Cameras**: Heat-detection for low-light or outdoor monitoring
- **License Plate Recognition (LPR)**: Automated vehicle identification
- **Facial Recognition**: AI-powered identity verification

**Video Management Systems (VMS)**:
- Centralized recording and storage
- Multi-site monitoring capabilities
- Motion detection and analytics
- Video search and retrieval
- Integration with access control and alarms
- Mobile app access
- Cloud storage options

**Video Analytics**:
- People counting and tracking
- Perimeter intrusion detection
- Loitering detection
- Object detection and removal
- Crowd density monitoring
- Behavioral analysis

### Intrusion Detection Systems

Alarm systems that detect unauthorized entry:

**Detection Technologies**:
- **Perimeter Protection**: Fence sensors, buried cable systems, microwave barriers
- **Door and Window Contacts**: Magnetic switches detecting opening
- **Motion Detectors**: Passive infrared (PIR), dual-technology sensors
- **Glass Break Detectors**: Acoustic sensors detecting window breakage
- **Vibration Sensors**: Detecting forced entry attempts
- **Pressure Mats**: Hidden floor sensors

**System Components**:
- Control panels and keypads
- Wireless and hardwired sensors
- Central monitoring station connectivity
- Battery backup systems
- Mobile app control and notifications
- Integration with security cameras
- Environmental sensors (smoke, flood, temperature)

### Perimeter Security

Securing property boundaries and outdoor areas:

**Physical Barriers**:
- Fencing systems (chain link, ornamental, anti-climb)
- Bollards and vehicle barriers
- Gates and barriers (manual, automatic, crash-rated)
- Security lighting
- Natural surveillance (CPTED principles)

**Detection Systems**:
- Fence-mounted sensors
- Buried cable detection
- Microwave and infrared beams
- Video analytics for perimeter monitoring
- Radar detection systems

### Armored Car Services

Secure transportation of valuables:

- Cash-in-transit services
- ATM servicing
- Precious metals and gems transport
- Document and data media transport
- High-value merchandise transport
- Bank deposits and change orders

### Security Consulting and Risk Assessment

Professional services to evaluate and improve security posture:

**Assessment Services**:
- Security audits and gap analysis
- Threat and vulnerability assessments
- Physical penetration testing
- Security master planning
- Regulatory compliance reviews (SOC 2, ISO 27001)
- Crisis management planning

**Design Services**:
- Security system design and specification
- Integration planning
- Technology selection and RFP development
- Facility security design
- CPTED assessments

## Industry Standards and Best Practices

### Security Officer Training

- Basic security officer training (40-80 hours typical)
- CPR and first aid certification
- Use of force training
- Emergency response procedures
- Customer service training
- Site-specific post orders
- Continuing education requirements

### Regulatory Compliance

- State licensing requirements for security companies
- Security officer registration and background checks
- Insurance requirements (general liability, worker's compensation)
- Industry certifications (ASIS International, IAHSS)
- Data privacy regulations for surveillance systems
- Labor law compliance

### Technology Integration

Modern physical security increasingly integrates with:

- **Building Management Systems (BMS)**: HVAC, lighting, elevators
- **Identity and Access Management (IAM)**: Enterprise credential systems
- **Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)**: Centralized security monitoring
- **Physical Security Information Management (PSIM)**: Unified security operations platform
- **IoT Sensors**: Environmental monitoring, occupancy detection
- **AI and Machine Learning**: Predictive analytics, anomaly detection
