# Risk Assessment and Compliance

Security risk assessment and compliance management encompass the systematic processes of identifying, analyzing, evaluating, and treating information security risks while ensuring adherence to regulatory requirements, industry standards, and organizational policies.

## Overview

Risk assessment provides the foundation for effective security programs by enabling organizations to understand their threat landscape, prioritize investments, and implement appropriate controls. Compliance management ensures organizations meet legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations while maintaining customer trust and avoiding penalties.

## Risk Management Framework

### Risk Assessment Process

**1. Asset Identification**:
- **Information Assets**:
  - Customer data and PII
  - Intellectual property
  - Financial information
  - Strategic business data
  - Employee information

- **Technology Assets**:
  - Servers and workstations
  - Network infrastructure
  - Applications and databases
  - Cloud resources
  - Mobile devices
  - IoT devices

- **Business Processes**:
  - Critical business operations
  - Revenue-generating processes
  - Support functions
  - Third-party dependencies

**2. Threat Identification**:
- **Threat Actors**:
  - Nation-state attackers
  - Cybercriminals and organized crime
  - Hacktivists
  - Insider threats (malicious and negligent)
  - Competitors
  - Script kiddies

- **Threat Types**:
  - Malware (ransomware, trojans, spyware)
  - Phishing and social engineering
  - Denial of service attacks
  - Data breaches and exfiltration
  - Supply chain attacks
  - Physical security breaches
  - Natural disasters
  - Human error

**3. Vulnerability Assessment**:
- Technical vulnerabilities (software flaws, misconfigurations)
- Procedural vulnerabilities (inadequate policies, lack of training)
- Physical vulnerabilities (inadequate access controls)
- Organizational vulnerabilities (insufficient resources, lack of oversight)

**4. Risk Analysis**:
- **Qualitative Risk Analysis**:
  - Risk matrices (likelihood vs. impact)
  - Risk ratings (high, medium, low)
  - Expert judgment
  - Scenario analysis
  - Bow-tie analysis

- **Quantitative Risk Analysis**:
  - Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)
  - Single Loss Expectancy (SLE)
  - Annual Rate of Occurrence (ARO)
  - Return on Security Investment (ROSI)
  - Monte Carlo simulation
  - Factor Analysis of Information Risk (FAIR)

**5. Risk Evaluation**:
- Compare risks against risk appetite
- Prioritize risks based on severity
- Identify unacceptable risks
- Determine risk treatment strategies
- Document risk register

**6. Risk Treatment**:
- **Risk Avoidance**: Eliminate the risk by discontinuing the activity
- **Risk Reduction**: Implement controls to mitigate the risk
- **Risk Transfer**: Share the risk with third parties (insurance, outsourcing)
- **Risk Acceptance**: Accept the risk when cost of mitigation exceeds impact

**7. Monitoring and Review**:
- Continuous risk monitoring
- Periodic risk reassessment
- Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)
- Risk reporting and dashboards
- Emerging threat tracking

### Risk Management Frameworks

**NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF)**:
1. **Prepare**: Organizational context and resources
2. **Categorize**: Information systems and information
3. **Select**: Baseline security controls
4. **Implement**: Security and privacy controls
5. **Assess**: Control effectiveness
6. **Authorize**: Accept risk and authorize operation
7. **Monitor**: Continuous monitoring and updates

**ISO 31000 Risk Management**:
- International standard for risk management
- Principles, framework, and process
- Applicable to all types of organizations
- Integration with organizational governance

**OCTAVE (Operationally Critical Threat, Asset, and Vulnerability Evaluation)**:
- Self-directed risk assessment
- Organizational risk view
- Three phases: build asset-based threat profiles, identify vulnerabilities, develop security strategy

**FAIR (Factor Analysis of Information Risk)**:
- Quantitative risk analysis framework
- Standard taxonomy for risk factors
- Statistical modeling approach
- Monetary loss measurement

### Vulnerability Prioritization

**Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS)**:
- Base Score: Intrinsic vulnerability characteristics
- Temporal Score: Time-sensitive factors
- Environmental Score: Organization-specific context
- CVSS v3.1 scale: 0.0-10.0
- Severity ratings: None, Low, Medium, High, Critical

**Exploit Prediction Scoring System (EPSS)**:
- Probability that a vulnerability will be exploited
- Daily updated scores
- Complement to CVSS
- Data-driven approach

**Vulnerability Prioritization Frameworks**:
- **SSVC (Stakeholder-Specific Vulnerability Categorization)**: Decision tree for prioritization
- **VPR (Vulnerability Priority Rating)**: Tenable's risk-based scoring
- **KEV (Known Exploited Vulnerabilities)**: CISA's catalog of actively exploited vulnerabilities

**Risk-Based Prioritization Factors**:
- Vulnerability severity (CVSS score)
- Exploit availability
- Threat intelligence context
- Asset criticality
- Exposure (internal vs. internet-facing)
- Compensating controls
- Business impact

### Patch Management

**Patch Management Process**:
1. **Inventory**: Maintain current asset inventory
2. **Assess**: Evaluate patch criticality and compatibility
3. **Test**: Validate patches in test environment
4. **Deploy**: Roll out patches systematically
5. **Verify**: Confirm successful deployment
6. **Document**: Maintain patch records

**Patch Prioritization**:
- Critical security patches (within 72 hours)
- Important security patches (within 30 days)
- Moderate security patches (within 60 days)
- Optional patches (as scheduled)
- Emergency out-of-band patches (immediate)

**Patch Management Tools**:
- Microsoft WSUS/SCCM/Intune
- Red Hat Satellite
- ManageEngine Patch Manager Plus
- Ivanti Patch Management
- SolarWinds Patch Manager
- Automox

**Virtual Patching**:
- IPS/WAF rules to block exploit attempts
- Temporary mitigation until patch deployment
- Protection for legacy/unsupported systems

## Compliance Management

Ensuring adherence to regulatory, legal, and contractual requirements:

### Major Compliance Frameworks

**SOC 2 (Service Organization Control)**:
- **Type I**: Point-in-time assessment of control design
- **Type II**: 6-12 month assessment of control effectiveness
- **Trust Service Criteria**:
  - Security: Protection against unauthorized access
  - Availability: System availability for operation and use
  - Processing Integrity: Complete, valid, accurate, timely processing
  - Confidentiality: Protection of confidential information
  - Privacy: Personal information collection, use, retention, disclosure

**SOC 2 Control Categories**:
- CC1: Control Environment
- CC2: Communication and Information
- CC3: Risk Assessment
- CC4: Monitoring Activities
- CC5: Control Activities
- CC6: Logical and Physical Access Controls
- CC7: System Operations
- CC8: Change Management
- CC9: Risk Mitigation

**ISO/IEC 27001 Information Security Management**:
- **Certification Process**:
  - Gap analysis and risk assessment
  - ISMS implementation
  - Internal audit
  - Management review
  - Certification audit (Stage 1 and Stage 2)
  - Surveillance audits (annual)
  - Recertification (every 3 years)

**Annex A Controls** (93 controls across 4 themes):
- Organizational controls (37)
- People controls (8)
- Physical controls (14)
- Technological controls (34)

**PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)**:
- **12 Requirements**:
  1. Install and maintain firewall configuration
  2. Do not use vendor-supplied defaults
  3. Protect stored cardholder data
  4. Encrypt transmission of cardholder data
  5. Protect systems against malware
  6. Develop and maintain secure systems
  7. Restrict access to cardholder data
  8. Identify and authenticate access
  9. Restrict physical access to cardholder data
  10. Track and monitor network access
  11. Test security systems and processes
  12. Maintain information security policy

**Validation Levels**:
- Level 1: >6M transactions/year (annual on-site audit)
- Level 2: 1-6M transactions/year (annual SAQ)
- Level 3: 20K-1M e-commerce transactions/year (annual SAQ)
- Level 4: <20K e-commerce or <1M transactions/year (annual SAQ)

**HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)**:
- **Security Rule Requirements**:
  - Administrative Safeguards (9 standards)
  - Physical Safeguards (4 standards)
  - Technical Safeguards (5 standards)
  - Organizational Requirements
  - Policies and Procedures
  - Documentation Requirements

**Covered Entities**:
- Healthcare providers
- Health plans
- Healthcare clearinghouses
- Business associates

**GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)**:
- **Principles**:
  - Lawfulness, fairness, transparency
  - Purpose limitation
  - Data minimization
  - Accuracy
  - Storage limitation
  - Integrity and confidentiality
  - Accountability

**Individual Rights**:
- Right to be informed
- Right of access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object
- Rights related to automated decision-making

**Data Protection Requirements**:
- Privacy by design and default
- Data protection impact assessments (DPIA)
- Data breach notification (72 hours)
- Data Protection Officer (DPO) appointment
- Records of processing activities

**CCPA/CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act/Privacy Rights Act)**:
- Consumer rights to know, delete, opt-out
- Non-discrimination for exercising rights
- Sale of personal information restrictions
- Privacy notices and disclosures

**Federal/Industry-Specific**:
- **FISMA**: Federal Information Security Management Act
- **FedRAMP**: Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program
- **CMMC**: Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (defense contractors)
- **GLBA**: Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (financial institutions)
- **FERPA**: Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act
- **SOX**: Sarbanes-Oxley Act (financial reporting)
- **NERC CIP**: North American Electric Reliability Corporation Critical Infrastructure Protection

## Security Metrics and KPIs

### Risk Metrics

- **Risk Score**: Aggregate organizational risk level
- **Risk Velocity**: Rate of risk change
- **Mean Time to Remediate (MTTR)**: Average remediation time
- **Risk Coverage**: Percentage of risks with controls
- **Control Effectiveness**: Percentage of effective controls

### Vulnerability Metrics

- **Critical Vulnerabilities**: Count of critical severity findings
- **Vulnerability Age**: Average time vulnerabilities remain open
- **Patch Compliance Rate**: Percentage of systems patched
- **Mean Time to Patch (MTTP)**: Average patching timeline
- **Vulnerability Density**: Vulnerabilities per asset

### Compliance Metrics

- **Compliance Score**: Overall compliance percentage
- **Control Compliance**: Percentage of controls implemented
- **Audit Findings**: Number and severity of findings
- **Remediation Status**: Percentage of findings closed
- **Policy Acknowledgment**: Employee policy acceptance rate

### Security Posture Metrics

- **Security Score**: Composite security rating
- **Attack Surface**: Exposed assets and services
- **Third-Party Risk Score**: Vendor security ratings
- **Incident Rate**: Security incidents per period
- **Breach Likelihood**: Probability of breach occurrence
