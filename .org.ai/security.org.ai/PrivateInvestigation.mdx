# Private Investigation Services

Private investigation encompasses professional investigative services provided to individuals, businesses, attorneys, and organizations for purposes including background checks, surveillance, fraud detection, due diligence, litigation support, and asset searches. The industry is classified under NAICS 561611 - Investigation Services.

## Overview

Private investigators combine traditional investigative techniques with modern technology and databases to gather information, document activities, locate individuals, verify claims, and uncover fraud. Services range from simple background checks to complex corporate investigations involving multiple jurisdictions and sophisticated surveillance operations.

## Service Categories

### Corporate Investigations

Professional investigation services for businesses and organizations:

**Employee Investigations**:
- **Misconduct Investigations**: Workplace harassment, discrimination, policy violations
- **Theft and Fraud**: Employee theft, embezzlement, expense fraud
- **Time and Attendance Fraud**: Timecard fraud, unauthorized absences
- **Intellectual Property Theft**: Trade secret theft, data exfiltration
- **Workplace Violence Threats**: Threat assessment and prevention
- **Workers' Compensation Fraud**: False injury claims, exaggerated injuries
- **Conflict of Interest**: Undisclosed relationships, competing businesses

**Investigation Methods**:
- Employee interviews and statements
- Document and email review
- Surveillance and monitoring
- Computer forensics and data analysis
- Financial records examination
- Covert operations when appropriate

**Executive Investigations**:
- Background checks for C-suite candidates
- Reputation and character assessment
- Conflict of interest examination
- Social media and public records research
- Professional credential verification
- Past employment verification
- Criminal and civil litigation history

**Corporate Fraud Investigations**:
- **Financial Statement Fraud**: Revenue recognition manipulation, asset overvaluation

- **Vendor Fraud**: Invoice fraud, shell companies, inflated pricing
- **Financial Mismanagement**: Asset misappropriation, unauthorized transactions
- **Accounting Irregularities**: Book manipulation, off-balance-sheet items

**Intellectual Property Investigations**:
- Trade secret theft detection

- Counterfeiting operations
- Reverse engineering analysis
- Competitive intelligence gathering

**Internal Investigations**:
- Policy violation investigations
- Ethics and compliance investigations

- Whistleblower complaint investigation
- Regulatory investigation support

### Licensing and Regulation

**State Licensing Requirements**:
- Private investigator license application
- Background check and fingerprinting
- Training and experience requirements
- Continuing education
- License renewal
- Insurance requirements
- Bonding requirements

**Exemptions**:

- Law enforcement (retired or active)
- Insurance company investigators

**Business Registration**:
- Business entity registration
- Professional liability insurance
- General liability insurance
- Workers' compensation insurance
- Errors and omissions coverage

### Legal Boundaries

**Permissible Activities**:
- Public records research
- Observation in public spaces
- Consensual interviews
- Lawful surveillance
- Database research (authorized)
- Social media monitoring (public profiles)

**Prohibited Activities**:
- Trespassing on private property
- Wiretapping or electronic eavesdropping
- Impersonating law enforcement
- Hacking or unauthorized computer access
- Pretexting for protected information
- Stalking or harassment
- Breaking and entering
- Illegal GPS tracking

**Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)**:
- Permissible purposes for background checks
- Consumer disclosure requirements
- Adverse action procedures
- Accuracy and dispute resolution
- Record retention requirements

**Privacy Laws**:
- State privacy laws compliance
- GDPR (for international investigations)
- CCPA/CPRA (California)
- Telephone recording laws (one-party vs. two-party consent)
- Video surveillance laws
