# .org.ai Domain Hierarchy

This document describes the complete hierarchy of all .org.ai ontology domains.

## Overview

The .org.ai ecosystem consists of **109** interconnected ontology domains organized in a hierarchical structure with `graph.org.ai` at the root.

## Visual Hierarchy

```
graph.org.ai (root)
├── schema.org.ai (core types)
│   ├── things.org.ai
│   ├── nouns.org.ai
│   ├── verbs.org.ai
│   ├── types.org.ai
│   └── properties.org.ai
├── knowledge.org.ai (taxonomies & classifications)
│   ├── onet.org.ai
│   ├── naics.org.ai
│   ├── apqc.org.ai
│   └── ...
├── business.org.ai (commercial entities)
│   ├── industries.org.ai
│   ├── companies.org.ai
│   └── ...
└── ...
```

## Relationships

Each domain:
- **Extends** its parent domain's types and properties
- **Imports** types from related domains
- **Exports** its types for use by child domains
