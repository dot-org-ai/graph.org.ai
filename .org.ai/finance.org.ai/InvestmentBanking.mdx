# Investment Banking

Securities intermediation, brokerage, asset management, and investment advisory services facilitating capital formation, trading, and portfolio management in global financial markets.

## Overview

Investment banking and securities services (NAICS 523) encompass the full spectrum of capital markets activities, from underwriting and distributing new securities to trading, research, and portfolio management. These institutions connect issuers with investors, provide liquidity to markets, and manage assets on behalf of clients.

### Core Functions

- **Capital Raising**: Underwriting equity (IPOs, follow-ons) and debt (bonds, notes) offerings
- **Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A)**: Advisory services for corporate transactions
- **Sales & Trading**: Market-making, proprietary trading, and client execution
- **Research**: Equity research, fixed income research, economic analysis
- **Asset Management**: Portfolio management for institutional and retail investors
- **Prime Brokerage**: Services for hedge funds and sophisticated investors
- **Custody Services**: Safekeeping and administration of securities

### Market Size and Structure

- **Global Investment Banking Fees**: $150+ billion annually
- **Global AUM (Assets Under Management)**: $100+ trillion
- **Daily Trading Volume (U.S. Equities)**: $500+ billion
- **IPO Proceeds (U.S.)**: $200+ billion annually (varies by market conditions)
- **M&A Transaction Volume**: $3-5 trillion globally

## APQC Investment Management Processes

### Portfolio Management Process

**Investment Policy and Strategy**
1. Define investment objectives and constraints
2. Develop asset allocation strategy (strategic, tactical)
3. Establish risk tolerance and risk budget
4. Set performance benchmarks
5. Document investment policy statement (IPS)

**Security Selection and Analysis**
1. Fundamental analysis (top-down, bottom-up)
2. Technical analysis
3. Quantitative modeling
4. ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) integration
5. Valuation analysis (DCF, relative valuation, precedent transactions)

**Portfolio Construction**
1. Asset allocation optimization (mean-variance, Black-Litterman)
2. Security selection
3. Position sizing
4. Risk factor exposure management
5. Diversification analysis

**Trade Execution**
1. Order generation and routing
2. Best execution practices
3. Transaction cost analysis (TCA)
4. Market impact mitigation
5. Settlement and clearing

**Performance Monitoring and Reporting**
1. Performance measurement (time-weighted, money-weighted returns)
2. Attribution analysis (security selection, asset allocation, timing)
3. Risk analytics (VaR, tracking error, Sharpe ratio, information ratio)
4. Compliance monitoring
5. Client reporting

### M&A Advisory Process

**1. Origination and Mandate**
- Client relationship development
- Pitch presentation and proposal
- Engagement letter and fee negotiation
- Conflict checks and approvals

**2. Preparation and Planning**
- Management interviews
- Business and industry analysis
- Financial analysis and projections
- Valuation analysis (DCF, comparable companies, precedent transactions)
- Marketing materials preparation (CIM, teaser, management presentation)

**3. Marketing and Buyer Identification**
- Strategic buyer list development
- Financial sponsor (PE) outreach
- Confidentiality agreements (NDAs)
- Management presentations
- Data room preparation (virtual data room / VDR)

**4. Due Diligence Management**
- Buy-side or sell-side due diligence coordination
- Q&A management
- Expert calls and site visits
- Issues list tracking
- Deal team coordination

**5. Negotiation and Structuring**
- Indication of interest (IOI) review
- Letter of intent (LOI) negotiation
- Purchase agreement negotiation
- Deal structure optimization (stock vs. asset deal, tax considerations)
- Financing arrangement

**6. Closing and Post-Closing**
- Regulatory approvals (HSR, foreign investment review)
- Shareholder approvals
- Financing commitment
- Closing conditions satisfaction
- Post-closing integration support

### Underwriting Process

**Pre-Marketing Phase**
1. Issuer evaluation and due diligence
2. Financial statement review and analysis
3. Industry and competitive analysis
4. Regulatory review (SEC, FINRA)
5. Underwriting syndicate formation
6. Pricing and terms discussion

**Registration and Filing**
1. Draft Registration Statement (S-1 for IPOs, S-3 for seasoned issuers)
2. Financial statement audit and comfort letters
3. Legal opinion preparation
4. SEC comment letter response
5. FINRA review
6. Exchange listing application

**Marketing Phase (Roadshow)**
1. Investor presentations
2. One-on-one meetings
3. Pilot fishing (gauging demand)
4. Book building
5. Pricing discussions

**Pricing and Allocation**
1. Final pricing determination
2. Greenshoe/overallotment option decision
3. Book allocation to investors
4. Agreement Among Underwriters (AAU) execution
5. Underwriting agreement execution

**Closing and Aftermarket**
1. Securities delivery and settlement
2. Gross spread payment
3. Stabilization activities
4. Lock-up period monitoring
5. Research coverage initiation

## Performance Metrics

### Investment Banking Metrics

- **League Table Rankings**: Market share in ECM, DCM, M&A
- **Deal Volume and Value**: Number and dollar amount of transactions
- **Underwriting Fees**: Revenue from securities offerings
- **Advisory Fees**: M&A and restructuring advisory revenue

### Asset Management Metrics

- **Assets Under Management (AUM)**: Total client assets
- **Net Flows**: Gross inflows minus gross outflows
- **Management Fee Rate**: Basis points charged on AUM
- **Performance Fee / Carried Interest**: Percentage of profits (typically 20%)
- **Performance Metrics**:
  - Absolute return
  - Benchmark-relative return (alpha)
  - Sharpe ratio (risk-adjusted return)
  - Information ratio (active return / tracking error)
  - Maximum drawdown
  - Win/loss ratio
  - Up/down capture ratios

### Trading Metrics

- **Spread Revenue**: Bid-ask spread capture
- **Trading Volume**: Notional traded
- **Market Share**: Percentage of market volume
- **Electronic vs. Voice Trading**: Channel mix
- **Price Improvement**: Client trades executed at better prices than NBBO
