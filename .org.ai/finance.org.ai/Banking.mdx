# Banking

Credit intermediation and depository institutions providing deposit accounts, lending services, payment processing, and financial advisory to consumers, businesses, and institutions.

## Overview

Banking services (NAICS 522) form the core of the financial system, facilitating the flow of capital between savers and borrowers. Banks perform critical functions including:

- **Deposit-taking**: Accepting customer deposits in checking, savings, and time deposit accounts
- **Lending**: Providing credit to consumers and businesses through loans, lines of credit, and mortgages
- **Payment services**: Processing transactions via checks, ACH, wire transfers, and cards
- **Financial intermediation**: Transforming short-term deposits into long-term loans
- **Liquidity provision**: Maintaining reserves to meet withdrawal demands
- **Trust and custody**: Safeguarding assets and providing fiduciary services

### Market Size and Structure

- **U.S. Commercial Banks**: 4,100+ FDIC-insured institutions
- **Total Banking Assets**: $24+ trillion (U.S.)
- **Credit Unions**: 4,900+ institutions serving 130+ million members
- **Branch Network**: 70,000+ bank branches nationwide
- **Employment**: 1.8+ million banking professionals

#### 52213 - Credit Unions

Member-owned cooperative financial institutions:

**Characteristics:**
- Not-for-profit structure
- Common bond membership (employer, association, community)
- Tax-exempt status
- Member-focused services
- Democratic governance (one member, one vote)

**Services:**
- Share accounts (savings)
- Share draft accounts (checking)
- Consumer loans
- Mortgage lending
- Business services (for eligible credit unions)

**Regulatory Framework:**
- National Credit Union Administration (NCUA)
- National Credit Union Share Insurance Fund (NCUSIF)
- Federal Credit Union Act

**Examples:**
- Navy Federal Credit Union
- State Employees' Credit Union
- Pentagon Federal Credit Union

**Management Practices:**
- Credit policy and underwriting standards
- Loan review and quality assurance
- Portfolio concentration limits
- Risk-based pricing
- Allowance for Loan and Lease Losses (ALLL) / Current Expected Credit Loss (CECL)
- Stress testing and scenario analysis

**Key Metrics:**
- Non-performing loan (NPL) ratio
- Net charge-off rate
- Loan loss coverage ratio
- Criticized and classified assets

### Market Risk

**Interest Rate Risk Management:**
- Asset-liability management (ALM)
- Duration gap analysis
- Repricing gap analysis
- Net interest margin (NIM) sensitivity
- Hedging strategies (interest rate swaps, caps, floors)

**Liquidity Risk:**
- Liquidity coverage ratio (LCR)
- Net stable funding ratio (NSFR)
- Contingency funding plan
- Access to Federal Reserve discount window

### Operational Risk

**Key Areas:**
- Fraud prevention and detection
- Cybersecurity and information security
- Business continuity and disaster recovery
- Third-party risk management
- Model risk management

**Controls:**
- Segregation of duties
- Dual control procedures
- Transaction monitoring
- Audit trails and logging
- Incident response plans

## Regulatory Compliance

### Federal Banking Regulations

**Prudential Regulation:**
- Capital adequacy requirements (Basel III)
  - Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1): minimum 4.5%
  - Tier 1 Capital: minimum 6%
  - Total Capital: minimum 8%
  - Capital Conservation Buffer: 2.5%
  - Countercyclical Capital Buffer (varies)
- Leverage ratio requirements
- Liquidity requirements (LCR, NSFR)
- Stress testing (CCAR, DFAST for large banks)

**Consumer Protection:**
- Truth in Lending Act (TILA) - Regulation Z
- Truth in Savings Act (TISA) - Regulation DD
- Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA) - Regulation X
- Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)
- Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA)
- Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) - Regulation B
- Fair Housing Act

## Performance Metrics

### Profitability Metrics

- **Net Interest Margin (NIM)**: (Interest Income - Interest Expense) / Average Earning Assets
- **Return on Assets (ROA)**: Net Income / Average Total Assets
- **Return on Equity (ROE)**: Net Income / Average Shareholders' Equity
- **Efficiency Ratio**: Non-Interest Expense / (Net Interest Income + Non-Interest Income)

### Asset Quality Metrics

- **Non-Performing Loan (NPL) Ratio**: Non-Performing Loans / Total Loans
- **Net Charge-Off Rate**: Net Charge-Offs / Average Loans
- **Loan Loss Reserve Ratio**: Allowance for Loan Losses / Total Loans
- **Texas Ratio**: (Non-Performing Assets) / (Tangible Common Equity + Loan Loss Reserves)

### Liquidity Metrics

- **Loan-to-Deposit Ratio (LTD)**: Total Loans / Total Deposits
- **Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)**: High-Quality Liquid Assets / Net Cash Outflows (30-day stress)
- **Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)**: Available Stable Funding / Required Stable Funding

### Capital Metrics

- **Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) Ratio**: CET1 Capital / Risk-Weighted Assets
- **Tier 1 Leverage Ratio**: Tier 1 Capital / Average Total Consolidated Assets
- **Tangible Common Equity (TCE) Ratio**: Tangible Common Equity / Tangible Assets
