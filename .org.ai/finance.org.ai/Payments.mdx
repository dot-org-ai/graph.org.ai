# Payments

Electronic and traditional payment processing infrastructure enabling funds transfer between parties through card networks, ACH, wire transfers, real-time payment systems, and emerging fintech solutions.

## Overview

The payments industry forms the circulatory system of the global economy, facilitating trillions of dollars in transactions annually. Modern payment systems combine infrastructure (networks, rails, protocols), participants (issuers, acquirers, processors), and innovations (mobile wallets, instant payments, blockchain) to enable seamless value transfer.

### Core Functions

- **Authorization**: Verifying funds availability and approving transactions
- **Clearing**: Exchanging transaction details between financial institutions
- **Settlement**: Transferring funds to complete transactions
- **Reconciliation**: Matching and confirming transaction records
- **Dispute Resolution**: Managing chargebacks and payment disputes
- **Fraud Prevention**: Detecting and preventing unauthorized transactions
- **Compliance**: AML/KYC, sanctions screening, regulatory reporting

### Market Size and Structure

- **Global Payment Transaction Volume**: 1+ trillion transactions annually
- **Global Card Payment Volume**: $40+ trillion
- **U.S. ACH Transaction Volume**: 30+ billion transactions, $75+ trillion
- **Wire Transfer Volume**: $150+ trillion annually
- **Payment Processing Revenue**: $250+ billion globally
- **Fintech Investment**: $200+ billion annually

## Payment Types and Instruments

### Card Payments

#### Credit Cards

Cards that extend revolving credit to cardholders:

**Features:**
- Grace period (typically 21-25 days)
- Credit limit based on creditworthiness
- Interest charges on carried balances (APR)
- Rewards programs (cashback, points, miles)
- Purchase protections and extended warranties

**Card Networks:**
- **Visa**: 3.8+ billion cards, 200+ countries
- **Mastercard**: 3.0+ billion cards, 210+ countries
- **American Express**: 140+ million cards (closed-loop network)
- **Discover**: 70+ million cards (U.S.-focused)
- **China UnionPay**: 9+ billion cards (Asia-Pacific focus)

**Transaction Flow:**
1. Cardholder presents card at merchant
2. Merchant sends authorization request via acquirer
3. Card network routes to issuing bank
4. Issuer approves or declines transaction
5. Authorization response returned to merchant
6. Clearing and settlement occur in batch (T+1 to T+3)

**Interchange Fees:**
- Paid by merchant acquirer to card issuer
- Typically 1.5%-3.5% of transaction value
- Varies by card type, merchant category, transaction type
- Regulated in some jurisdictions (EU Interchange Fee Regulation, Durbin Amendment)

#### Debit Cards

Cards that draw directly from cardholder's bank account:

**Types:**
- **Signature Debit**: Processed over credit card networks (Visa, Mastercard)
- **PIN Debit**: Processed over EFT networks (STAR, NYCE, Pulse, Accel)
- **Prepaid Debit**: Loaded with funds, not linked to bank account

**Regulation:**
- Durbin Amendment (Regulation II): Interchange fee caps for large issuers
- Debit interchange typically $0.21 + 0.05% for regulated debit
- PIN debit generally has lower interchange than signature debit

#### Charge Cards

Similar to credit cards but require full balance payment each month:
- No preset spending limit
- No interest charges (balance must be paid in full)
- Annual fees typically higher
- Example: American Express Charge Cards

#### Commercial Cards

Cards issued to businesses and organizations:

**Types:**
- **Corporate Cards**: For employee business expenses
- **Purchasing Cards (P-Cards)**: For procurement and supplies
- **Travel and Entertainment (T&E) Cards**: For business travel
- **Fleet Cards**: For fuel and vehicle maintenance

**Features:**
- Higher credit limits
- Detailed expense reporting
- Integration with ERP systems
- Rebate programs (Level 2/Level 3 data)

### Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT)

#### ACH (Automated Clearing House)

Batch-processed electronic payments for recurring and one-time transfers:

**ACH Network Operators:**
- **Federal Reserve (FedACH)**: Government-operated ACH
- **The Clearing House (EPN - Electronic Payments Network)**: Private sector ACH

**ACH Transaction Types:**

**ACH Credit (Push)**
- Direct deposit of payroll
- Government benefits (Social Security, tax refunds)
- Business-to-business payments
- Person-to-person payments
- Same-Day ACH (initiated by sender)

**ACH Debit (Pull)**
- Bill payments (utilities, mortgages, subscriptions)
- Business-to-business collections
- Consumer-authorized payments
- Same-Day ACH debit

**ACH Processing:**
- Batch processing (multiple cut-off times daily)
- Same-Day ACH: Settlement in hours
- Next-Day ACH: Settlement next business day
- Return window: 2 business days (unauthorized), 60 days (consumer disputes)

**ACH Rules and Governance:**
- NACHA (National Automated Clearing House Association) Operating Rules
- Originating Depository Financial Institution (ODFI) responsibilities
- Receiving Depository Financial Institution (RDFI) responsibilities
- Third-Party Service Provider (TPSP) oversight

#### Wire Transfers

Real-time, irrevocable electronic funds transfers:

**Domestic Wires (Fedwire):**
- Operated by Federal Reserve Banks
- Real-time gross settlement (RTGS)
- Irrevocable once sent
- Individual transaction processing
- Typical fees: $15-$50 per wire
- High-value transactions ($5+ million average)
- Same-day settlement

**International Wires (SWIFT):**
- SWIFT (Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication)
- Global messaging network for international payments
- Correspondent banking relationships
- Multi-day settlement (2-5 business days)
- Higher fees ($25-$75+)
- Currency conversion costs
- SWIFT gpi (Global Payments Innovation): Tracking and faster settlement

**Wire Transfer Use Cases:**
- Real estate transactions
- Large business payments
- International remittances
- Securities transactions
- Emergency payments

#### Real-Time Payments (RTP)

Instant, 24/7/365 payment settlement:

**U.S. Real-Time Payment Systems:**

**RTP Network (The Clearing House):**
- Launched 2017
- Real-time settlement (seconds)
- Irrevocable payments
- ISO 20022 messaging standard
- Request for Payment (RfP) feature
- 400+ participating financial institutions
- Transaction limit: $1 million

**FedNow Service (Federal Reserve):**
- Launched July 2023
- Instant payment settlement
- Interoperable with RTP Network
- 24/7/365 availability
- Transaction limit: $500,000
- Universal bank access

**Use Cases:**
- Emergency disbursements (disaster relief, insurance claims)
- Gig economy payments
- Instant account-to-account transfers
- Just-in-time B2B payments
- Instant invoice payments
- Real-time bill payments

**International Real-Time Payment Systems:**
- UPI (Unified Payments Interface) - India
- PIX - Brazil
- Faster Payments Service (FPS) - UK
- SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) - Europe
- PayNow - Singapore
- PromptPay - Thailand

### Mobile Payments and Digital Wallets

#### Mobile Wallets

Digital applications storing payment credentials:

**NFC-Based Mobile Wallets (Proximity Payments):**
- **Apple Pay**: Tokenized card credentials, biometric authentication
- **Google Pay**: Android devices, tokenized payments
- **Samsung Pay**: MST and NFC technology

**Features:**
- Tokenization (replacing card numbers with tokens)
- Biometric authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint)
- In-app and online payments
- Transit payments
- Loyalty program integration

**QR Code Wallets:**
- **Alipay**: 1.3+ billion users (China)
- **WeChat Pay**: 1.2+ billion users (China)
- **Paytm**: 450+ million users (India)
- **Cash App**: 50+ million users (U.S.)
- **Venmo**: 90+ million users (U.S.)

#### Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Payments

Person-to-person funds transfer apps:

**U.S. P2P Platforms:**
- **Zelle**: Bank-owned, integrated into bank apps, 2,000+ FIs, 120+ million users
- **Venmo** (PayPal): Social payments, $244 billion TPV (2023)
- **Cash App** (Block/Square): $13+ billion revenue (2023)
- **PayPal**: 430+ million accounts globally

**Features:**
- Instant or near-instant transfers
- Social feeds and payment notes
- Merchant acceptance
- Debit card issuance
- Stock and crypto trading integration

**International P2P:**
- M-Pesa (Kenya, mobile money)
- PhonePe (India, UPI-based)
- GCash (Philippines)
- bKash (Bangladesh)

### Check Payments

Traditional paper-based payment instruments:

**Check 21 (Check Clearing for the 21st Century Act):**
- Substitute checks (Image Replacement Documents)
- Remote deposit capture (RDC)
- Check truncation
- Electronic check presentment

**Check Volume Decline:**
- Peak: 42+ billion checks (1995)
- Current: 3-4 billion checks annually
- Continued use for B2B, rent, and government payments

**Services:**

#### Payment Service Providers (PSPs)

Aggregated merchant services for small and medium businesses:

**Examples:**
- Stripe
- Square
- PayPal
- Adyen
- Worldpay

**Model:**
- Aggregate multiple merchants under master merchant account
- Simplified onboarding (less underwriting)
- All-in-one pricing
- API-based integration

#### Independent Sales Organizations (ISOs)

Sales agents that resell merchant acquiring services:

**Role:**
- Merchant sales and relationship management
- Equipment placement
- Customer support

Sub-merchant model for onboarding and managing merchants:

**Structure:**
- Master merchant account with acquirer

- Underwriting responsibility
- Compliance and risk management

**Examples:**
- Stripe Connect
- Square
- PayPal
- Shopify Payments

**Benefits:**
- Faster merchant onboarding (minutes vs. days)
- Simplified underwriting
- Seamless integration into platforms

### Payment Security

#### PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)

Security standards for handling cardholder data:

**Compliance Levels:**
- **Level 1**: 6+ million transactions annually
- **Level 2**: 1-6 million transactions
- **Level 3**: 20,000-1 million e-commerce transactions
- **Level 4**: <20,000 e-commerce transactions or <1 million total

**12 Requirements:**
1. Install and maintain firewall configuration
2. Don't use vendor-supplied defaults
3. Protect stored cardholder data
4. Encrypt transmission of cardholder data
5. Use and regularly update anti-virus software
6. Develop and maintain secure systems
7. Restrict access to cardholder data (need-to-know)
8. Assign unique ID to each person with computer access
9. Restrict physical access to cardholder data
10. Track and monitor all access to network and cardholder data
11. Regularly test security systems and processes
12. Maintain information security policy

**Validation:**
- Self-Assessment Questionnaires (SAQs)
- Attestation of Compliance (AOC)
- Quarterly network scans (Approved Scanning Vendor)
- Annual on-site assessments (Level 1)

#### EMV (Europay, Mastercard, Visa)

Chip card technology for card-present transactions:

**Features:**
- Embedded microprocessor chip
- Dynamic authentication codes (different per transaction)
- Reduced counterfeit fraud
- Liability shift to non-compliant parties (2015 in U.S.)

**Transaction Types:**
- Chip-and-PIN (Europe, rest of world)
- Chip-and-Signature (U.S., declining)
- Contactless EMV (NFC, tap-to-pay)

#### Tokenization

Replacing sensitive payment data with surrogate values (tokens):

**Types:**
- **Network Tokens** (Visa Token Service, Mastercard Digital Enablement Service)
- **Gateway Tokens** (Stripe, Braintree)
- **Acquirer Tokens**

**Benefits:**
- Reduced PCI DSS scope
- Fraud reduction
- Improved authorization rates
- Enhanced recurring payment security

#### 3D Secure (3DS)

Additional authentication layer for online card transactions:

**3D Secure 2.0 (3DS2):**
- Risk-based authentication
- Frictionless flow for low-risk transactions
- Biometric authentication support
- Mobile-optimized
- Reduced cart abandonment
- Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) compliance (PSD2 in Europe)

**Liability Shift:**
- Issuer liable for fraud on authenticated transactions
- Merchant liable for fraud on unauthenticated transactions

#### Fraud Detection and Prevention

**Techniques:**
- Device fingerprinting
- Behavioral biometrics
- Machine learning models
- Velocity checks
- Address Verification Service (AVS)
- Card Verification Value (CVV/CVC)
- Geolocation verification
- 3D Secure authentication

**Fraud Types:**
- Card-not-present (CNP) fraud
- Account takeover (ATO)
- Identity theft
- Friendly fraud (chargeback abuse)
- Synthetic identity fraud
- BIN attacks

## Regulatory and Compliance Framework

### U.S. Payment Regulations

**Electronic Fund Transfer Act (EFTA) - Regulation E:**
- Consumer protections for electronic payments
- Error resolution procedures
- Liability limits for unauthorized transactions
- Disclosure requirements

**Durbin Amendment (Dodd-Frank Section 1075):**
- Debit card interchange fee caps (regulated issuers)
- Network exclusivity and routing requirements
- Fraud prevention adjustment

**NACHA Operating Rules:**
- ACH network rules and standards
- Risk management requirements
- Same-Day ACH obligations
- Third-party sender registration

**Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) / Anti-Money Laundering (AML):**
- Customer Identification Program (CIP)
- Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) filing
- Currency Transaction Report (CTR) for $10,000+ cash
- Travel Rule for funds transfers $3,000+

**OFAC (Office of Foreign Assets Control):**
- Sanctions screening
- Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) List
- Blocked transactions and asset freezing

**State Money Transmitter Licenses (MTL):**
- Required for non-bank payment providers
- State-by-state licensing (50+ jurisdictions)
- Surety bond requirements
- Permissible investment requirements
- Net worth requirements

### International Payment Regulations

**PSD2 (Payment Services Directive 2) - Europe:**
- Strong Customer Authentication (SCA)
- Open Banking (API access to account data)
- Third-Party Provider (TPP) licensing
- Payment Initiation Services (PIS)
- Account Information Services (AIS)

**GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation):**
- Data protection and privacy
- Right to be forgotten
- Data breach notification
- Consent requirements

**AML Directives (Europe):**
- 5AMLD (Fifth Anti-Money Laundering Directive)
- 6AMLD (Sixth Anti-Money Laundering Directive)
- Travel Rule for crypto assets

## FinTech Innovation and Emerging Trends

### Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL)

Point-of-sale installment lending:

**Leading Providers:**
- **Affirm**: Interest-based and 0% APR options
- **Klarna**: 4-interest-free payments, shopping app
- **Afterpay** (Block): 4 installments, merchant-funded
- **PayPal Pay in 4**: Integrated into PayPal checkout
- **Apple Pay Later**: Built into Apple Wallet

**Characteristics:**
- Short-term installment loans (typically 4-12 payments)
- Soft credit checks (alternative underwriting)
- Merchant-subsidized (interchange-like economics)
- No late fees (in some cases)
- Integration at online checkout

**Regulatory Scrutiny:**
- CFPB oversight and inquiry
- Truth in Lending Act (TILA) applicability
- Credit reporting requirements
- Responsible lending concerns

### Cryptocurrency and Blockchain Payments

**Payment Use Cases:**
- Cross-border remittances (lower fees, faster settlement)
- Merchant acceptance (BitPay, Coinbase Commerce)
- Stablecoin payments (USDC, USDT)
- Lightning Network (Bitcoin Layer 2 for micropayments)
- Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs)

**Challenges:**
- Volatility (for non-stablecoins)

Digital fiat currencies issued by central banks:

**Motivations:**
- Financial inclusion
- Payment system efficiency
- Reduce cash usage
- Cross-border payment improvement
- Competitive response to private crypto

**Design Choices:**
- Retail vs. wholesale CBDC
- Account-based vs. token-based
- Direct central bank accounts vs. two-tier (bank intermediaries)
- Offline capability
- Programmability

**Global CBDC Projects:**
- **China**: Digital yuan (e-CNY) pilot programs
- **European Central Bank**: Digital euro investigation
- **Bank of England**: Britcoin consultation
- **Federal Reserve**: Project Hamilton research
- **Sand Dollar** (Bahamas): Launched 2020

**Open Banking:**

## Cross-Border Payments

### Correspondent Banking

Traditional model for international payments:

**Structure:**
- Nostro accounts (correspondent bank accounts held abroad)
- Vostro accounts (foreign bank accounts held domestically)
- Multi-hop routing through intermediary banks

**Challenges:**
- High costs (3-7% of transaction value)
- Slow settlement (2-5 business days)
- Lack of transparency
- Currency conversion spreads
- Compliance costs (AML, sanctions screening)

### Alternative Cross-Border Solutions

**Fintech Platforms:**
- **Wise (formerly TransferWise)**: Peer-to-peer currency exchange model
- **Remitly**: Remittance-focused, cash pickup options
- **WorldRemit**: Mobile money integration
- **Payoneer**: Cross-border B2B payments
- **Western Union**: Traditional remittance leader, digital expansion
- **MoneyGram**: Remittances with cash and digital options

**Blockchain-Based:**
- Ripple (XRP Ledger, On-Demand Liquidity)
- Stellar (cross-border payments for financial institutions)
- Circle (USDC stablecoin for remittances)

**Bank Collaborations:**
- SWIFT gpi (Global Payments Innovation): Faster, traceable correspondent banking
- Visa B2B Connect: Blockchain-based B2B payments
- Mastercard Cross-Border Services

## Performance Metrics

### Transaction Metrics

- **Transaction Volume**: Number of transactions processed
- **Transaction Value (TPV - Total Payment Volume)**: Dollar amount processed
- **Authorization Rate**: Approved transactions / total authorization requests
- **Settlement Time**: Time from authorization to funds availability
- **Transaction Success Rate**: Completed transactions / initiated transactions

### Revenue Metrics

- **Take Rate**: Revenue as percentage of TPV
- **Interchange Income** (issuers): Interchange fees earned
- **Discount Rate** (acquirers): Merchant service charge rate
- **Net Revenue Retention**: Revenue from existing customers year-over-year

### Operational Metrics

- **Uptime / Availability**: System availability (target: 99.99%+)
- **Fraud Rate**: Fraudulent transactions / total transactions (basis points)
- **Chargeback Rate**: Chargebacks / total transactions (threshold: typically <1%)
- **False Positive Rate**: Legitimate transactions declined as fraud
- **Time to Settlement**: Days or hours to settle funds

### Customer Metrics

- **Merchant Attrition Rate**: Merchants churned / total merchants
- **Payment Abandonment Rate**: Incomplete checkouts / initiated checkouts
- **Cost per Transaction (CPT)**: Processing costs / transaction volume
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Marketing and sales costs / new customers
