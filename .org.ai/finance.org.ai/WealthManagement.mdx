# Wealth Management

Comprehensive financial services for high-net-worth individuals (HNWIs), ultra-high-net-worth individuals (UHNWIs), families, and institutions, including investment management, financial planning, trust services, and concierge banking.

## Overview

Wealth management represents the convergence of investment management, financial planning, banking, and specialized services tailored to affluent clients. Wealth managers provide holistic advice addressing multiple dimensions of clients' financial lives, from investment portfolios to estate planning, tax optimization, philanthropic giving, and generational wealth transfer.

### Core Functions

- **Investment Management**: Portfolio construction, asset allocation, manager selection
- **Financial Planning**: Comprehensive planning, goal-based strategies
- **Trust and Estate Services**: Estate planning, trust administration, legacy planning
- **Tax Planning**: Tax-efficient investing, tax mitigation strategies
- **Risk Management**: Insurance planning, hedging strategies
- **Banking and Lending**: Private banking, securities-based lending, mortgages
- **Philanthropy**: Charitable giving strategies, donor-advised funds, foundations
- **Family Office Services**: Multi-generational wealth management, family governance

### Market Size and Client Segments

**Client Segmentation:**

**Mass Affluent:**
- Investable assets: $100,000 - $1 million
- Services: Automated advice (robo-advisors), hybrid advisory

**High-Net-Worth (HNW):**
- Investable assets: $1 million - $5 million
- Services: Dedicated advisor, financial planning

**Very High-Net-Worth (VHNW):**
- Investable assets: $5 million - $30 million
- Services: Wealth management team, specialized services

**Ultra-High-Net-Worth (UHNW):**
- Investable assets: $30 million+
- Services: Family office, bespoke solutions, concierge services

**Market Statistics:**
- Global UHNW population: 250,000+ individuals
- Global UHNW wealth: $35+ trillion
- Global wealth management AUM: $100+ trillion
- Private banking AUM: $25+ trillion
- Family office AUM: $6+ trillion

## Wealth Management Services

### Investment Management

#### Portfolio Construction

**Strategic Asset Allocation:**
- Policy portfolio based on client objectives, time horizon, risk tolerance
- Capital market assumptions (expected returns, volatilities, correlations)
- Mean-variance optimization (Modern Portfolio Theory)
- Risk parity approaches
- Liability-driven investing (LDI) for institutions
- Goal-based allocation

**Tactical Asset Allocation:**
- Short-term deviations from strategic policy
- Market timing and valuation-driven adjustments
- Risk-on / risk-off positioning
- Sector rotation
- Geographic allocation shifts

**Asset Class Selection:**

**Public Equities:**
- U.S. large-cap (S&P 500)
- U.S. mid-cap and small-cap
- International developed markets (MSCI EAFE)
- Emerging markets (MSCI EM)
- Sector-specific (technology, healthcare, financials)
- Factor-based (value, growth, momentum, quality, low volatility)

**Fixed Income:**
- U.S. Treasuries (bills, notes, bonds, TIPS)
- Investment-grade corporate bonds
- High-yield bonds
- Municipal bonds (tax-advantaged)
- International bonds (developed and emerging)
- Floating-rate loans
- Convertible bonds

**Alternative Investments:**
- Hedge funds (long/short equity, event-driven, global macro, relative value)
- Private equity (buyouts, growth equity, venture capital)
- Real estate (core, value-add, opportunistic, REITs)
- Infrastructure (utilities, transportation, communication)
- Commodities (gold, energy, agriculture)
- Collectibles (art, wine, classic cars)
- Digital assets (Bitcoin, Ethereum, crypto funds)

**Cash and Equivalents:**
- Money market funds
- Treasury bills
- Bank deposits
- Stable value funds

#### Manager Selection and Due Diligence

**Quantitative Analysis:**
- Performance attribution (alpha, beta)
- Risk-adjusted returns (Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, information ratio)
- Tracking error and active share
- Factor exposures (Fama-French, Carhart)
- Drawdown analysis
- Up/down capture ratios

**Qualitative Analysis:**
- Investment philosophy and process
- Team stability and experience
- Organizational structure and resources
- Risk management framework
- Alignment of interests (fee structure, co-investment)
- Operational due diligence (trade execution, valuation, controls)

**Manager Monitoring:**
- Quarterly performance reviews
- Style drift analysis
- Capacity constraints
- Personnel changes
- Strategy deviations
- Risk limit breaches

#### Direct Indexing and Tax Optimization

**Direct Indexing (Separately Managed Accounts):**
- Ownership of individual securities (vs. mutual fund/ETF)
- Customization (ESG screens, restrictions, tilts)
- Tax-loss harvesting (daily or opportunistic)
- Tracking error management
- Minimum investment: $100,000-$250,000+

**Tax-Loss Harvesting:**
- Realize losses to offset capital gains
- Wash sale rule compliance (30-day substantially identical security)
- Long-term vs. short-term gain/loss management
- $3,000 annual ordinary income offset
- Carryforward of excess losses

**Tax-Efficient Portfolio Construction:**
- Asset location (tax-advantaged vs. taxable accounts)
- Municipal bonds for high-tax states
- Qualified dividend income (QDI) treatment
- Long-term capital gains preferential rates (0%, 15%, 20%)
- Minimizing distributions and turnover

**Education Planning:**

- Coverdell Education Savings Accounts (ESA)
- UGMA/UTMA custodial accounts
- Private school funding strategies
- Financial aid optimization (FAFSA, CSS Profile)

**Cash Flow and Budgeting:**
- Income and expense analysis
- Discretionary vs. non-discretionary spending
- Emergency fund sizing (3-6 months expenses)
- Debt management (mortgage, consumer debt)

**Risk Management and Insurance:**
- Life insurance (term, whole life, universal life, variable life)
- Disability insurance (own-occupation, any-occupation)
- Long-term care insurance
- Liability insurance (umbrella policies)
- Property and casualty insurance
- Key person insurance (business owners)

**Wills and Trusts:**
- Last will and testament
- Revocable living trusts (RLTs)
- Irrevocable life insurance trusts (ILITs)
- Grantor retained annuity trusts (GRATs)
- Charitable remainder trusts (CRTs)
- Qualified personal residence trusts (QPRTs)
- Spousal lifetime access trusts (SLATs)

**Beneficiary Designations:**
- Retirement accounts (IRA, 401(k))
- Life insurance policies
- Transfer-on-death (TOD) accounts
- Payable-on-death (POD) accounts

**Estate Tax Planning:**
- Federal estate tax exemption: $13.61 million (2024, individual), $27.22 million (married couple)
- Portability of unused exemption (DSUE - Deceased Spousal Unused Exclusion)
- Annual gift tax exclusion: $18,000 per recipient (2024)
- Lifetime gift tax exemption (unified with estate tax)
- Generation-skipping transfer (GST) tax
- Step-up in basis at death
- State estate and inheritance taxes

**Business Succession Planning:**
- Buy-sell agreements (cross-purchase, redemption, hybrid)
- Family limited partnerships (FLPs)
- Business valuations (409A, estate/gift tax)
- Key employee retention
- Exit strategies (sale, IPO, family transfer)

**Trust Administration:**
- Trustee selection (individual, corporate, co-trustee)
- Trust accounting and tax returns (Form 1041)
- Distributions to beneficiaries
- Investment management
- Fiduciary duties (prudent investor rule, duty of loyalty, duty of impartiality)

#### Tax Planning and Optimization

**Income Tax Strategies:**
- Tax bracket management (marginal vs. effective rates)
- Capital gains harvesting (filling 0% bracket)
- Roth IRA conversions (optimizing tax rates in retirement)
- Qualified Business Income (QBI) deduction (Section 199A)
- Net investment income tax (NIIT) - 3.8% surtax
- Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT) planning

**Charitable Giving Strategies:**
- Donor-advised funds (DAFs) - immediate deduction, distribute over time
- Charitable remainder trusts (CRTs) - income stream + charitable deduction
- Charitable lead trusts (CLTs) - charity receives income, remainder to heirs
- Qualified charitable distributions (QCDs) from IRAs (age 70.5+, up to $105,000)
- Appreciated securities donations (avoid capital gains tax)
- Private foundations (control, multi-generational giving)

**Retirement Account Strategies:**
- Traditional IRA vs. Roth IRA contributions
- Backdoor Roth IRA (for high earners above income limits)
- Mega backdoor Roth (after-tax 401(k) contributions)
- Roth conversions (strategic timing in low-income years)
- Stretch IRA (repealed by SECURE Act for most beneficiaries)
- SECURE Act 2.0 provisions (RMD age increase, catch-up contributions)

**State and Local Tax (SALT) Planning:**
- Domicile planning (residency in low-tax states)
- State income tax considerations (Florida, Texas, Nevada, Wyoming - no income tax)
- Property tax planning
- Incomplete gift non-grantor trusts (ING trusts) for state tax planning

### Private Banking Services

#### Deposit and Cash Management

**High-Yield Savings and Money Market Accounts:**
- FDIC insurance ($250,000 per depositor, per bank)
- Sweep accounts (automatically invest excess cash)
- Multi-bank deposit programs (extended FDIC coverage via reciprocal deposits)

**Concierge Banking:**
- Dedicated relationship manager
- Priority service and access
- Preferential pricing (fee waivers, rate discounts)
- Lifestyle services (travel, event access, personal shopping)

#### Lending Services

**Securities-Based Lending (SBL) / Margin Loans:**
- Loans collateralized by investment portfolio
- Advance rates: 50-95% (depending on asset class and liquidity)
- Floating interest rates (SOFR + spread)
- No loan repayment schedule (interest-only, revolving)
- Use of funds: Real estate, business investments, liquidity (not for securities purchases on margin)
- Risk: Margin calls if portfolio value declines

**Residential Mortgages:**
- Jumbo mortgages (>$766,550)
- Portfolio loans (non-conforming)
- Interest-only mortgages
- Relationship-based pricing

**Lombard Loans (European equivalent of SBL):**
- Similar structure to SBL
- Used in Europe and Asia
- Multi-currency lending

**Pledged Asset Lines of Credit:**
- Credit line secured by brokerage account
- Lower interest rates vs. unsecured credit
- Flexible draws and repayments

#### Trust Services

**Institutional Trust Services:**

**Custody Services:**

### Alternative Investment Access

#### Private Equity

**Access Methods:**
- Direct co-investments (alongside PE funds)
- Fund of funds (diversified PE exposure)
- Primary fund commitments
- Secondary market purchases (LP interests)

**Investment Minimums:**
- Institutional funds: $5-25 million+
- Wealth management platforms: $250,000-$1 million

**Lock-Up Periods:**
- Typical fund life: 10-12 years
- J-curve (negative returns initially, distributions later)
- Limited liquidity (secondaries provide some exit)

#### Hedge Funds

**Access Requirements:**
- Accredited investor: $1 million net worth (excluding primary residence) or $200,000+ annual income ($300,000 joint)
- Qualified purchaser: $5 million+ investable assets (for 3(c)(7) funds)

**Fee Structures:**
- Management fee: 1-2% of AUM
- Performance fee (incentive fee): 15-20% of profits
- Hurdle rate: Minimum return before performance fee (sometimes)
- High-water mark: Performance fee only on gains above previous peak

**Liquidity Terms:**
- Lock-up periods: 1-3 years (initial investment)
- Redemption frequency: Quarterly, semi-annual, annual
- Redemption notice: 30-90 days
- Gates (limits on redemptions during stress)

**Fund of Hedge Funds:**
- Diversified hedge fund exposure
- Lower minimums ($100,000-$1 million)
- Additional layer of fees (0.50-1.50% management, 5-10% performance)
- Due diligence and manager selection

#### Real Estate Investments

**Direct Ownership:**
- Residential real estate (primary, vacation homes, rental properties)
- Commercial real estate (office, retail, industrial, multifamily)
- Farmland and timberland
- Real estate development

**Indirect Ownership:**
- REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts) - publicly traded, liquid
- Private REITs - non-traded, less liquid
- Real estate funds (core, value-add, opportunistic)
- Real estate crowdfunding platforms (RealtyMogul, Fundrise)
- 1031 exchanges (tax-deferred like-kind exchanges)
- Delaware Statutory Trusts (DSTs) for 1031 exchanges

**Sources:**

**Initial Steps:**
- Wealth preservation (safety, liquidity)
- Assemble advisory team (wealth manager, CPA, estate attorney)
- Defer major decisions (3-6 months)

#### Divorcee Wealth Management

**Unique Needs:**
- Asset division and settlement analysis
- Alimony and child support management

#### Executive Compensation and Equity Planning

**Stock Options:**
- Incentive Stock Options (ISOs) - favorable tax treatment, AMT considerations
- Non-Qualified Stock Options (NSOs) - ordinary income on exercise
- Exercise strategies (early exercise, cashless exercise, sell-to-cover)

**Restricted Stock Units (RSUs):**
- Vest over time (typically 3-4 years)
- Taxed as ordinary income upon vesting
- Tax withholding considerations (sell-to-cover)

**Performance Share Units (PSUs):**
- Vest based on company performance metrics
- Potential for above-target payouts

**Employee Stock Purchase Plans (ESPPs):**
- Purchase company stock at discount (typically 15%)
- Lookback provisions
- Qualifying vs. disqualifying dispositions (tax treatment)

**Diversification Strategies:**
- Rule of thumb: No more than 10-20% in single company stock
- Systematic selling programs (10b5-1 trading plans)
- Hedging strategies (cashless collars, prepaid variable forwards)
- Tax-aware liquidation

## Investment Strategies for Wealth Management

### Conservative / Capital Preservation

**Asset Allocation:**
- 20-40% equities
- 50-70% fixed income
- 5-15% alternatives

**Objectives:**
- Capital preservation
- Income generation
- Low volatility
- Liquidity

**Suitable For:**
- Retirees drawing income
- Risk-averse investors
- Short time horizons (0-5 years)

### Balanced / Moderate Growth

**Asset Allocation:**
- 50-70% equities
- 25-40% fixed income
- 5-15% alternatives

**Objectives:**
- Balanced growth and income
- Moderate risk tolerance
- Inflation protection

**Suitable For:**
- Pre-retirees (5-10 years to retirement)
- Long-term investors with moderate risk tolerance
- Diversified growth and income needs

### Growth / Aggressive Growth

**Asset Allocation:**
- 75-90% equities
- 5-15% fixed income
- 10-25% alternatives

**Objectives:**
- Long-term capital appreciation
- High risk tolerance
- Inflation outpacing growth

**Suitable For:**
- Young investors with long time horizons (15+ years)
- High-income earners with long savings horizon
- Institutional endowments and foundations

### Alternative-Heavy / Absolute Return

**Asset Allocation:**
- 30-50% alternatives (hedge funds, PE, real estate)
- 30-50% equities
- 20-30% fixed income

**Objectives:**
- Absolute returns (positive returns in all environments)
- Low correlation to traditional markets
- Alpha generation

**Suitable For:**
- Ultra-high-net-worth investors
- Family offices
- Sophisticated investors with long time horizons and high liquidity

## Regulatory and Compliance

### Investment Advisers Act of 1940

**Registration Requirements:**
- SEC registration (>$110 million AUM, or if state-registered and in 15+ states)
- State registration (<$110 million AUM)

**Form ADV:**
- Part 1: Firm information, business practices, disciplinary history
- Part 2A: Brochure (services, fees, conflicts of interest, disciplinary)
- Part 2B: Brochure supplement (advisor background and qualifications)

**Fiduciary Duty:**
- Duty of care: Act in client's best interest, provide suitable advice
- Duty of loyalty: Disclose conflicts of interest, avoid self-dealing
- Best execution: Seek best execution of client transactions

**Custody Rule (Rule 206(4)-2):**
- Surprise annual examinations (if custody of assets)
- Qualified custodian requirement
- Account statements delivered to clients

### CFP (Certified Financial Planner) Code of Ethics

**Fiduciary Standard:**
- Act in client's best interest at all times
- Provide financial planning services as fiduciary
- Conflict of interest disclosure

**Standards of Conduct:**
- Duties to clients
- Duties to firms and subordinates
- Duties to CFP Board

### Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Oversight

**Examinations:**
- Periodic examinations (every 3-5 years on average)
- Risk-based examination priorities
- Compliance program review

**Focus Areas:**
- Fiduciary duty and conflicts of interest
- Fees and expenses (billing practices)
- Portfolio management (strategy adherence, suitability)
- Marketing and advertising (testimonials, performance)
- Custody and safeguarding of assets

### Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC)

**Customer Identification Program (CIP):**
- Verify identity (name, address, date of birth, SSN/TIN)
- Documentary verification (driver's license, passport)
- Non-documentary verification (credit bureau, public records)

**Customer Due Diligence (CDD):**
- Identify and verify beneficial owners (25%+ ownership)
- Understand nature and purpose of customer relationships
- Ongoing monitoring for suspicious activity

**Suspicious Activity Reporting (SAR):**
- File SARs for transactions >$5,000 with suspicious activity indicators
- Insider trading, market manipulation, terrorist financing, money laundering

**OFAC Sanctions Screening:**
- Screen against Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) List
- Blocked persons and entities
- Blocked assets and transaction prohibition

## Performance Measurement and Benchmarking

### Return Calculation

**Time-Weighted Return (TWR):**
- Eliminates impact of cash flows
- Appropriate for manager performance evaluation
- Benchmark comparison

**Money-Weighted Return (MWR) / Internal Rate of Return (IRR):**
- Includes impact of cash flows (contributions, withdrawals)
- Appropriate for client return (actual experience)
- Personalized return

**After-Tax Returns:**
- Pre-liquidation after-tax return
- Post-liquidation after-tax return
- Tax-cost ratio

### Benchmarks

**Public Equity:**
- S&P 500 (U.S. large-cap)
- Russell 2000 (U.S. small-cap)
- MSCI EAFE (International developed)
- MSCI Emerging Markets (Emerging markets)

**Fixed Income:**
- Bloomberg U.S. Aggregate Bond Index (Core bonds)
- Bloomberg U.S. Corporate High Yield (High-yield bonds)
- Bloomberg Municipal Bond Index (Tax-exempt)

**Balanced:**
- 60/40 stock/bond portfolio
- Target-date fund benchmarks

**Alternatives:**
- HFRI Fund Weighted Composite Index (Hedge funds)
- Cambridge Associates Private Equity Index
- NCREIF Property Index (Real estate)

### Risk Metrics

**Volatility:**
- Standard deviation (annualized)
- Downside deviation (semi-deviation)

**Risk-Adjusted Returns:**
- Sharpe Ratio: (Return - Risk-Free Rate) / Standard Deviation
- Sortino Ratio: (Return - Risk-Free Rate) / Downside Deviation
- Information Ratio: Active Return / Tracking Error

**Drawdown Metrics:**
- Maximum drawdown (peak-to-trough decline)
- Drawdown duration (time to recover)
- Ulcer Index (depth and duration of drawdowns)

**Tail Risk:**
- Value-at-Risk (VaR): Maximum loss at given confidence level (e.g., 95%, 99%)
- Conditional VaR (CVaR): Expected loss beyond VaR threshold
- Skewness and kurtosis
