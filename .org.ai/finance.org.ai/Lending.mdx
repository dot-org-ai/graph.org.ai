# Lending

Consumer, mortgage, and commercial credit products enabling individuals and businesses to access capital for purchases, investments, and working capital needs through depository and non-depository financial institutions.

## Overview

Lending is the core business of banking and financial intermediation, connecting savers (depositors) with borrowers through credit products. Lenders assess creditworthiness, structure loan terms, originate credit facilities, service outstanding balances, and manage portfolio risk. The lending lifecycle encompasses origination, underwriting, closing, servicing, and collections.

### Core Functions

- **Credit Origination**: Loan application, qualification, and approval process
- **Underwriting**: Credit analysis, risk assessment, and pricing
- **Loan Servicing**: Payment processing, escrow management, customer service
- **Portfolio Management**: Risk monitoring, loss mitigation, collections
- **Secondary Market**: Loan sales, securitization, and investor relations
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Consumer protection, fair lending, capital requirements

### Market Size and Structure

- **U.S. Total Loan Portfolio**: $17+ trillion (commercial banks)
- **Mortgage Debt Outstanding**: $12+ trillion
- **Consumer Credit Outstanding**: $4.9+ trillion
- **Commercial & Industrial Loans**: $2.7+ trillion
- **Commercial Real Estate Loans**: $2.9+ trillion
- **Auto Loans Outstanding**: $1.6+ trillion
- **Student Loans Outstanding**: $1.6+ trillion

## Lending Product Categories

### Consumer Lending

Credit products for individuals and households:

#### Personal Loans

Unsecured installment loans for general purposes:

**Characteristics:**
- Loan amounts: $1,000 - $100,000
- Terms: 1-7 years
- Fixed interest rates (typically)
- No collateral required
- Origination fees: 1-8%
- APRs: 6-36% (depending on credit quality)

**Use Cases:**
- Debt consolidation
- Home improvements
- Major purchases
- Medical expenses
- Wedding expenses
- Emergency expenses

**Lenders:**
- Traditional banks (Wells Fargo, Chase, Discover)
- Credit unions
- Online lenders (SoFi, LendingClub, Prosper, Upstart)

#### Auto Loans

Secured loans for vehicle purchases:

**Structure:**
- New vehicle loans: 2-7 years, 4-8% APR
- Used vehicle loans: 3-6 years, 5-12% APR
- Loan-to-Value (LTV): Up to 120% (including negative equity)
- Down payment: 0-20%
- Collateral: Vehicle with lien

**Lenders:**
- Captive finance companies (Toyota Financial, Ford Credit, GM Financial)
- Banks and credit unions
- Online lenders (Capital One Auto Navigator, Bank of America)

**Special Programs:**
- 0% APR promotional financing (manufacturer incentives)
- Subprime auto lending (deep subprime: FICO <580)
- Lease buyout financing

#### Credit Cards

Revolving unsecured credit lines:

**Key Terms:**
- Credit limits: $500 - $50,000+
- APRs: 15-30% (variable)
- Grace period: 21-25 days
- Minimum payment: 1-3% of balance or $25-$35 minimum

**Card Types:**
- Rewards cards (cashback, points, miles)
- Balance transfer cards (0% intro APR)
- Secured credit cards (deposit-backed)
- Student cards
- Business credit cards

**Revenue Sources (Issuers):**
- Interest income (revolving balances)
- Interchange fees (1.5-3.5%)
- Annual fees ($0-$695+)
- Late fees (up to $40)
- Foreign transaction fees (0-3%)
- Balance transfer fees (3-5%)
- Cash advance fees (3-5%)

**Underwriting:**
- FICO scores (primary factor)
- Income verification
- Debt-to-income (DTI) ratio
- Credit utilization
- Payment history
- Automated decisioning engines

#### Student Loans

Education financing for tuition and living expenses:

**Federal Student Loans:**
- Direct Subsidized Loans (undergraduate, need-based)
- Direct Unsubsidized Loans (graduate and undergraduate)
- Direct PLUS Loans (parents and graduate students)
- Fixed interest rates set by Congress
- Income-Driven Repayment (IDR) plans
- Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF)
- Deferment and forbearance options

**Private Student Loans:**
- Variable or fixed rates
- Credit-based underwriting
- Cosigner typically required (for students)
- Refinancing options post-graduation
- Fewer borrower protections than federal loans

**Lenders:**
- Federal government (Department of Education)
- Banks (Citizens Bank, Wells Fargo, Discover)
- Credit unions
- Online lenders (SoFi, Earnest, CommonBond)

#### Home Equity Loans and HELOCs

Secured loans using home equity as collateral:

**Home Equity Loan:**
- Lump sum disbursement
- Fixed interest rate
- Fixed repayment term (5-30 years)
- Second mortgage lien position

**Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC):**
- Revolving credit line
- Variable interest rate (typically)
- Draw period (5-10 years) + repayment period (10-20 years)
- Interest-only payments during draw period (often)

**Key Metrics:**
- Combined Loan-to-Value (CLTV): Up to 85-90%
- Credit score requirements: 620-680+
- Debt-to-income: Max 43-50%
- Minimum equity: 15-20%

**Use Cases:**
- Home renovations and improvements
- Debt consolidation
- Education expenses
- Investment property down payments
- Emergency reserves

#### Other Consumer Credit

**Personal Lines of Credit:**
- Revolving credit (non-card)
- Overdraft protection
- Checking account lines of credit

**Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL):**
- Point-of-sale installment financing
- 0% APR (typically)
- 4-12 installments
- Soft credit checks
- Providers: Affirm, Klarna, Afterpay, PayPal Pay in 4

**Payday Alternative Loans (PALs):**
- Credit union small-dollar loans
- $200-$1,000 amounts
- 1-6 month terms
- 28% APR cap
- Alternative to predatory payday lending

### Mortgage Lending

Real estate secured loans for home purchases and refinancing:

#### Residential Mortgage Products

**Conventional Mortgages:**

**Conforming Loans:**
- Meet Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac guidelines
- Loan limits: $766,550 (2024, standard); up to $1,149,825 (high-cost areas)
- Down payment: 3-20%
- PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance) required if LTV >80%
- Credit score minimums: 620-640
- DTI limits: 36-43% (front-end / back-end)

**Jumbo Loans:**
- Exceed conforming loan limits
- Higher credit score requirements (700+)
- Larger down payments (10-20%)
- Lower DTI requirements (max 43%)
- Higher interest rates (typically)
- Not eligible for GSE purchase

**Government-Backed Loans:**

**FHA (Federal Housing Administration) Loans:**
- Low down payment: 3.5% (FICO 580+); 10% (FICO 500-579)
- Mortgage Insurance Premium (MIP): Upfront (1.75%) + annual (0.55-1.05%)
- Loan limits: $498,257 (2024, standard)
- Lenient credit requirements
- Higher DTI allowances (up to 50% with compensating factors)

**VA (Department of Veterans Affairs) Loans:**
- 0% down payment
- No mortgage insurance
- VA funding fee: 1.4-3.6% (waived for disabled veterans)
- Eligible for veterans, active duty, and surviving spouses
- Loan limits: None (with full entitlement)
- Lenient credit and DTI requirements

**USDA (U.S. Department of Agriculture) Loans:**
- 0% down payment
- For rural and suburban properties
- Income limits apply (115% of area median income)
- Upfront guarantee fee (1%) + annual fee (0.35%)
- Property must be in eligible rural area

**Fixed-Rate Mortgages (FRM):**
- 30-year fixed (most common)
- 20-year fixed
- 15-year fixed (lower rates, higher payments)
- 10-year fixed
- Stable monthly payments
- Protection against interest rate increases

**Adjustable-Rate Mortgages (ARM):**
- Initial fixed period: 3, 5, 7, 10 years (3/1, 5/1, 7/1, 10/1 ARMs)
- Adjustment period: Typically annual after initial period
- Index: SOFR (Secured Overnight Financing Rate), replacing LIBOR
- Margin: 2-3% added to index
- Caps: Initial, periodic, lifetime (e.g., 5/2/5 cap structure)
- Lower initial rates vs. fixed-rate mortgages

**Interest-Only Mortgages:**
- Interest-only payment period (5-10 years)
- Principal and interest payments thereafter
- Higher-risk product
- Used by high-income borrowers with variable income

**Reverse Mortgages (HECM - Home Equity Conversion Mortgage):**
- For seniors 62+
- Convert home equity to income or lump sum
- No monthly mortgage payments required
- Loan repaid when homeowner moves or passes away
- FHA-insured (HECM)
- Non-borrowing spouse protections

#### Mortgage Refinancing

**Rate-and-Term Refinance:**
- Lower interest rate
- Change loan term (e.g., 30-year to 15-year)
- Switch from ARM to fixed-rate
- Break-even analysis (closing costs / monthly savings)

**Cash-Out Refinance:**
- Refinance for more than existing loan balance
- Extract equity in cash
- Higher interest rates than rate-and-term
- LTV limits: 80% (conventional), 85% (FHA), 90% (VA)

**Streamline Refinance:**
- FHA Streamline Refinance (no appraisal, limited documentation)
- VA Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan (IRRRL)
- Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac High LTV Refinance

#### Commercial Real Estate (CRE) Lending

Loans for income-producing properties and business real estate:

**Property Types:**
- Office buildings
- Retail centers and shopping malls
- Multifamily (apartment buildings, 5+ units)
- Industrial (warehouses, distribution centers)
- Hospitality (hotels, motels)
- Self-storage
- Healthcare facilities (medical offices, senior living)
- Special purpose (gas stations, car washes, churches)

**Loan Structure:**
- Loan-to-Value (LTV): 65-80%
- Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR): Minimum 1.20-1.35x
- Amortization: 20-30 years
- Term: 5-10 years (balloon payment)
- Recourse vs. non-recourse
- Interest rates: Fixed or floating (SOFR + margin)

**Underwriting Criteria:**
- Property cash flow and net operating income (NOI)
- Property appraisal and valuation
- Sponsor experience and net worth
- Market and location analysis
- Tenant creditworthiness and lease terms
- Environmental assessment (Phase I/II)
- Property condition assessment (PCA)

**CRE Loan Types:**

**Permanent Loans:**
- Long-term financing (5-30 years)
- Stabilized, income-producing properties
- Conventional lenders: banks, life insurance companies, CMBS

**Bridge Loans:**
- Short-term financing (1-3 years)
- Transitional properties or lease-up
- Higher interest rates
- Exit strategy required

**Construction Loans:**
- Financing for new construction or major renovation
- Interest-only during construction
- Draws based on construction progress
- Convert to permanent financing or refinance upon completion

**Mezzanine Loans:**
- Subordinated debt (junior to senior mortgage)
- Higher interest rates (8-15%+)
- Used to increase leverage beyond senior LTV limits
- Often includes equity participation (warrants, profit share)

**Small Business Administration (SBA) Loans:**

**SBA 7(a) Loans:**
- General purpose small business loans
- Maximum: $5 million
- SBA guarantee: Up to 85% (<$150K), 75% (>$150K)
- Real estate, equipment, working capital
- Terms: Up to 25 years (real estate), 10 years (equipment), 10 years (working capital)

**SBA 504 Loans:**
- Commercial real estate and equipment financing
- Two-loan structure: 50% bank loan, 40% CDC loan (SBA-backed), 10% borrower equity
- Fixed-rate CDC loan (20-year term)
- Maximum: $5-5.5 million

### Commercial and Industrial (C&I) Lending

Business loans for operating expenses, equipment, and growth:

#### Working Capital Loans

Short-term financing for operational needs:

**Structures:**
- Revolving lines of credit
- Term loans (1-5 years)
- Seasonal lines of credit
- Inventory financing
- Accounts receivable financing

**Key Metrics:**
- Advance rates: 75-85% of eligible A/R, 50% of inventory
- Borrowing base certificates (periodic reporting)
- Financial covenants (leverage, DSCR, liquidity)

#### Equipment Financing

Loans or leases for machinery, vehicles, and equipment:

**Equipment Loans:**
- Secured by equipment
- Terms: 3-7 years
- LTV: 80-100%
- Fixed or variable rates

**Equipment Leases:**
- Capital leases (ownership transfer)
- Operating leases (return equipment at end)
- $1 buyout leases
- Fair market value (FMV) leases

#### Term Loans

Medium to long-term business financing:

**Uses:**
- Business expansion
- Acquisition financing
- Real estate purchase
- Debt refinancing
- Equipment purchases

**Structure:**
- Loan amounts: $50,000 - $10 million+
- Terms: 5-25 years
- Amortization: Level payments
- Collateral: Business assets, real estate, personal guarantee

#### Leveraged Loans and Syndicated Lending

Large loans for leveraged buyouts (LBOs) and M&A:

**Leveraged Loans:**
- High leverage multiples (5-7x EBITDA)
- Below investment-grade credit quality
- SOFR + 300-600 bps
- Financial maintenance covenants or covenant-lite
- Institutional investors (CLOs, loan funds)

**Syndicated Loans:**
- Multiple lenders participate
- Lead arranger(s) structure and syndicate
- Agent bank administers loan
- Revolving credit facility + term loans
- Used for large corporate borrowers

**Loan Tranches:**
- Revolver (revolving credit facility)
- Term Loan A (TLA): Amortizing, bank-held
- Term Loan B (TLB): Institutional, minimal amortization
- Second Lien Loans: Subordinated security interest

#### Asset-Based Lending (ABL)

Revolving credit secured by current assets:

**Collateral:**
- Accounts receivable
- Inventory
- Machinery and equipment
- Real estate

**Advance Rates:**
- A/R: 75-85% of eligible receivables
- Inventory: 50-65% of eligible inventory
- Equipment: 50-75% of orderly liquidation value (OLV)

**Borrowing Base:**
- Periodic reporting (weekly, monthly)
- Field examinations and audits
- Ineligible accounts (over 90 days, concentrations)

**Uses:**
- Turnaround situations
- High-growth companies
- Seasonal businesses
- Leveraged transactions

## Lending Lifecycle and APQC Processes

### 4.2.1 Originate Loans

**Application Intake:**
- Loan application submission (online, branch, phone, broker)
- Initial screening and pre-qualification
- Document collection (pay stubs, tax returns, bank statements)
- Credit authorization

**Loan Officer Assignment:**
- Relationship manager or loan officer
- Product selection and recommendations
- Pricing and rate lock (mortgages)

**Pipeline Management:**
- Application tracking
- Milestone management
- Communication with borrowers
- Status updates and follow-up

### 4.2.2 Underwrite and Approve Loans

**Credit Analysis:**

**Consumer Credit:**
- FICO score evaluation (300-850 scale)
- Payment history (35% of FICO score)
- Credit utilization (30%)
- Length of credit history (15%)
- New credit inquiries (10%)
- Credit mix (10%)

**Income and Employment Verification:**
- Pay stubs (recent 30 days)
- W-2 forms (past 2 years)
- Tax returns (self-employed, 2 years)
- Verification of Employment (VOE)
- Bank statements (asset verification)

**Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio:**
- Front-end ratio (housing / gross income): Max 28-31%
- Back-end ratio (total debt / gross income): Max 36-43%
- Qualified Mortgage (QM) limit: 43% (with exceptions)

**Collateral Evaluation:**

**Real Estate Appraisal:**
- Licensed appraiser
- Uniform Residential Appraisal Report (URAR / Form 1004)
- Sales comparison approach
- Cost approach
- Income approach (investment properties)
- Appraisal Management Company (AMC) coordination

**Property Inspection:**
- Home inspection (structural, systems, safety)
- Pest inspection (termites)
- Survey (boundary verification)
- Title search and title insurance

**Commercial Credit Analysis:**
- Financial statement analysis (3-5 years)
- Cash flow analysis and projections
- Industry and market analysis
- Management assessment
- Credit bureau reports (D&B, Experian Business)
- Trade references

**Loan Pricing:**
- Risk-based pricing
- Credit score tiers
- Loan-to-value (LTV) adjustments
- Loan purpose adjustments (purchase vs. cash-out refinance)
- Property type adjustments (single-family, condo, 2-4 unit)
- Occupancy adjustments (owner-occupied, second home, investment)

**Approval Decision:**
- Automated underwriting systems (DU - Desktop Underwriter, LP - Loan Prospector)
- Manual underwriting (complex cases)
- Credit committee approval (large loans)
- Conditional approval (subject to conditions)
- Final approval and clear to close

### 4.2.3 Close and Fund Loans

**Mortgage Closing Process:**

**Clear to Close:**
- All underwriting conditions satisfied
- Final verification of employment (day before closing)
- Updated credit report (soft pull)
- Final walk-through (buyer inspection)

**Closing Disclosure (CD):**
- Provided 3 business days before closing
- Final loan terms and closing costs
- Comparison to Loan Estimate (LE)

**Closing Meeting:**
- Signing of loan documents
  - Promissory note
  - Deed of trust / mortgage
  - Truth in Lending (TIL) disclosure
  - Initial escrow disclosure
  - Closing disclosure (CD)
  - Other state-specific documents
- Notarization
- Funding and disbursement
- Recording of lien with county recorder

**Consumer Loan Funding:**
- Electronic signature (often)
- Funds disbursement (ACH, check)
- Lien perfection (auto loans - DMV lien notation)

**Commercial Loan Closing:**
- Loan agreement execution
- Security agreement and UCC filings
- Guaranty agreements
- Legal opinions
- Insurance requirements (property, liability, key person life)
- Flood certification and insurance
- Environmental indemnity agreements

### 4.2.4 Service and Administer Loans

**Payment Processing:**
- Monthly payment collection (principal, interest, escrow)
- Payment application (interest first, then principal)
- Late fee assessment (typically after 15-day grace period)
- NSF / returned payment fees

**Escrow Administration:**
- Property tax payments
- Homeowners insurance premiums
- Escrow analysis (annual review)
- Escrow shortage or surplus adjustments

**Customer Service:**
- Payment inquiries
- Payoff quotes
- Statement requests
- Online account access (loan portal)
- Modification requests

**Loan Modifications:**
- Rate modifications
- Term extensions
- Principal forbearance
- Payment deferrals

**Investor Reporting:**
- Loan performance data
- Delinquency reports
- REO (Real Estate Owned) reporting
- Remittance reporting

### 4.2.5 Manage Loan Portfolio and Collections

**Portfolio Monitoring:**
- Delinquency tracking (30, 60, 90 days past due)
- Risk rating and credit quality migration
- Concentration risk (geography, industry, product)
- Stress testing and scenario analysis

**Early-Stage Collections (1-30 days delinquent):**
- Payment reminders (email, SMS, phone)
- Payment arrangements
- Automatic payment enrollment

**Mid-Stage Collections (31-90 days delinquent):**
- Loss mitigation outreach
- Financial hardship assessment
- Modification options
- Repayment plans

**Late-Stage Collections (90+ days delinquent):**
- Default notice
- Demand letters
- Pre-foreclosure activities
- Foreclosure or repossession proceedings

**Loss Mitigation:**

**Mortgage Workouts:**
- Loan modification (HAMP, proprietary programs)
- Forbearance agreements
- Repayment plans
- Short sale (sell for less than loan balance)
- Deed-in-lieu of foreclosure

**Foreclosure Process:**
- Judicial foreclosure (court process, 6-24 months)
- Non-judicial foreclosure (trustee sale, 3-6 months)
- Notice of Default (NOD)
- Notice of Trustee Sale (NTS)
- Trustee sale / auction
- REO (Real Estate Owned) management

**Auto Loan Collections:**
- Repossession (after 60-90 days typically)
- Auction sale
- Deficiency balance collection

**Charge-Off and Recovery:**
- Charge-off timing (120-180 days, depending on product)
- Sale to debt buyers
- Collection agency placement
- Legal action and judgment
- Bankruptcy proceedings (Chapter 7, 11, 13)

## Credit Risk Management

### Risk Assessment and Monitoring

**Key Risk Metrics:**

**Asset Quality:**
- Non-Performing Loan (NPL) Ratio: NPL / Total Loans
- Net Charge-Off Rate: Net Charge-Offs / Average Loans
- Delinquency Rates (30, 60, 90 days past due)
- Criticized and Classified Assets (Special Mention, Substandard, Doubtful, Loss)
- Loan Loss Reserve Ratio: Allowance / Total Loans

**Credit Risk Indicators:**
- Weighted Average FICO Score
- Weighted Average LTV / CLTV
- Weighted Average DTI
- Origination credit quality trends

**Portfolio Composition:**
- Product mix
- Geographic concentration
- Industry concentration (C&I)
- Vintage analysis

### Allowance for Loan and Lease Losses (ALLL) / Current Expected Credit Loss (CECL)

**CECL (Current Expected Credit Loss) Model:**
- Lifetime expected loss at origination
- Forward-looking assumptions (economic forecasts)
- Reasonable and supportable forecast period
- Reversion to historical loss rates
- Qualitative adjustments

**Loss Estimation Methods:**
- Vintage analysis
- Roll rate analysis
- Probability of Default (PD) x Loss Given Default (LGD) x Exposure at Default (EAD)
- Discounted cash flow (DCF) method

**Factors Considered:**
- Historical loss experience
- Current economic conditions
- Reasonable and supportable forecasts
- Collateral values
- Credit quality indicators

### Credit Portfolio Management

**Concentration Risk Management:**
- Geographic limits
- Industry limits
- Product limits
- Single borrower / obligor limits
- Loan-to-one-borrower (legal lending limits)

**Stress Testing:**
- Baseline, adverse, and severely adverse scenarios
- Credit loss projections
- Pre-provision net revenue (PPNR) modeling
- Capital adequacy assessment

**Risk-Based Pricing:**
- Credit score-based pricing tiers
- LTV-based adjustments
- Loan purpose adjustments
- Market-competitive rates

## Regulatory and Compliance

### Consumer Lending Regulations

**Truth in Lending Act (TILA) - Regulation Z:**
- Annual Percentage Rate (APR) disclosure
- Finance charge calculation
- Right of rescission (3-day cooling-off period for home-secured loans)
- Ability-to-Repay (ATR) rule
- Qualified Mortgage (QM) standards

**Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA) - Regulation X:**
- Good Faith Estimate (replaced by Loan Estimate)
- HUD-1 Settlement Statement (replaced by Closing Disclosure)
- Loan servicing standards
- Loss mitigation procedures
- Force-placed insurance restrictions

**TILA-RESPA Integrated Disclosure (TRID) Rule:**
- Loan Estimate (LE): Within 3 business days of application
- Closing Disclosure (CD): At least 3 business days before closing
- Fee tolerance limits (zero, 10%, unlimited)

**Home Mortgage Disclosure Act (HMDA) - Regulation C:**
- Loan application register (LAR)
- Annual reporting of lending data
- Demographic information collection
- Loan pricing data (rate spread reporting)

**Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) - Regulation B:**
- Prohibition of discrimination (race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age, public assistance)
- Adverse action notices
- Appraisal disclosure requirements
- Record retention (25 months, 38 months for HMDA reporters)

**Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA):**
- Permissible purposes for credit reports
- Adverse action notices (credit-based)
- Credit score disclosure
- Prescreened credit offer opt-out
- Free annual credit report (AnnualCreditReport.com)

**Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA):**
- Third-party debt collector conduct rules
- Prohibition of abusive practices
- Communication restrictions
- Validation of debt
- Dispute rights

**Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA):**
- 6% interest rate cap for pre-service obligations
- Foreclosure protections
- Early lease termination rights
- Eviction protections

**Military Lending Act (MLA):**
- 36% Military Annual Percentage Rate (MAPR) cap
- Prohibition on mandatory arbitration
- Applies to covered borrowers (active duty military and dependents)

### Fair Lending and UDAAP

**Fair Lending:**
- Disparate treatment (overt discrimination)
- Disparate impact (neutral policy with discriminatory effect)
- Redlining (refusal to lend in certain areas)
- Reverse redlining (predatory lending targeting protected classes)
- Fair lending testing and monitoring

**UDAAP (Unfair, Deceptive, or Abusive Acts or Practices):**
- Unfair: Substantial injury, not reasonably avoidable, not outweighed by benefits
- Deceptive: Misleading representation or omission
- Abusive: Material interference with understanding or taking unreasonable advantage

### Capital and Liquidity

**Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA):**
- Standardized approach
- Advanced internal ratings-based (IRB) approach
- Risk weights: 0% (cash), 20% (GSE MBS), 50% (residential mortgages), 100% (most C&I, CRE)
- Credit risk mitigation (collateral, guarantees)

**Capital Ratios:**
- Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1): Minimum 4.5%
- Tier 1 Capital: Minimum 6%
- Total Capital: Minimum 8%
- Capital Conservation Buffer: 2.5%
- Countercyclical Capital Buffer (varies)

**Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR):**
- High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA) / Net Cash Outflows (30-day stress)
- Minimum 100%

**Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR):**
- Available Stable Funding / Required Stable Funding
- Minimum 100%

## Secondary Market and Securitization

### Mortgage Secondary Market

**Government-Sponsored Enterprises (GSEs):**
- **Fannie Mae** (Federal National Mortgage Association): Conventional mortgages
- **Freddie Mac** (Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation): Conventional mortgages
- **Ginnie Mae** (Government National Mortgage Association): FHA, VA, USDA loans

**GSE Functions:**
- Purchase loans from originators
- Guarantee mortgage-backed securities (MBS)
- Provide liquidity to mortgage market
- Set underwriting standards (conforming loan guidelines)

**Mortgage-Backed Securities (MBS):**
- Pass-through securities (principal and interest pass through)
- Tranched securities (CMOs - Collateralized Mortgage Obligations)
- Prepayment risk (borrowers refinance or pay off early)
- Extension risk (borrowers pay slowly in high-rate environment)

**Whole Loan Sales:**
- Portfolio sales to other lenders or investors
- Servicing released or servicing retained
- Premium or discount to par
- Due diligence and representations & warranties

### Consumer Loan Securitization

**Asset-Backed Securities (ABS):**
- Auto loan ABS
- Credit card ABS (master trusts)
- Student loan ABS (SLABS)
- Personal loan ABS

**Structure:**
- Special purpose vehicle (SPV) / trust
- Tranching (senior, mezzanine, subordinated)
- Credit enhancement (overcollateralization, reserve accounts, subordination)
- Ratings (AAA, AA, A, BBB, below investment-grade)

### Commercial Loan Secondary Market

**Collateralized Loan Obligations (CLOs):**
- Leveraged loan portfolios
- Actively managed (CLO manager)
- Tranched structures
- $1+ trillion CLO market

**Loan Syndication:**
- Primary syndication (at origination)
- Secondary trading (loan assignments, participations)
- Loan trading desks
- Par and distressed loan trading

## Performance Metrics

### Origination Metrics

- **Pull-Through Rate**: Closed loans / applications or approvals
- **Turn Time**: Days from application to closing
- **Fallout Rate**: Applications or locks that don't close
- **Lock Recapture Rate**: Rate locks that close
- **Cost per Loan**: Operating expenses / loans originated

### Portfolio Metrics

- **Yield**: Interest income / average outstanding balance
- **Net Interest Margin (NIM)**: Interest income - interest expense / earning assets
- **Prepayment Rate**: CPR (Constant Prepayment Rate), PSA (Public Securities Association) speed
- **Vintage Performance**: Credit performance by origination period

### Servicing Metrics

- **Cost to Service**: Servicing expenses / number of loans serviced
- **Delinquency Rate**: Delinquent loans / total loans (30, 60, 90 DPD)
- **Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)**: Survey scores
- **First Call Resolution**: Issues resolved on first contact

### Alternative Credit Models

- Expanded credit access (thin-file borrowers, new immigrants)

- Point-of-sale financing (BNPL)
- Buy-online-pick-up-in-store (BOPIS) financing
- Auto dealer financing integration
- Real estate brokerage mortgage integration

### Regulatory Evolution

- Qualified Mortgage (QM) rule changes (removal of 43% DTI limit)
- CECL adoption and refinement
- LIBOR to SOFR transition
- Appraisal modernization (desktop appraisals, hybrid appraisals)
- Fair lending and algorithmic bias concerns
