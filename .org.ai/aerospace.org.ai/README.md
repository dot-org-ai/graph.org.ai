# aerospace.org.ai

[![License: CC BY-SA 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY--SA%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

Ontology domain for aerospace product and parts manufacturing.

## Overview

This repository contains MDX documentation for aerospace.org.ai, part of the .org.ai ontology ecosystem.

The aerospace industry encompasses the research, design, manufacture, operation, and maintenance of aircraft, spacecraft, satellites, missiles, and related systems and equipment. As one of the most technologically advanced manufacturing sectors, aerospace combines cutting-edge engineering, precision manufacturing, complex supply chains, and rigorous quality and safety standards to enable commercial aviation, defense systems, and space exploration.

## Industry Scope

The aerospace industry is characterized by high barriers to entry, long product development cycles (5-10+ years), extensive regulatory oversight, and global supply chains involving thousands of specialized suppliers.

### NAICS 3364: Aerospace Product and Parts Manufacturing

The aerospace industry is primarily classified under **NAICS 3364 - Aerospace Product and Parts Manufacturing**, which encompasses establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following:

- Manufacturing complete aircraft, missiles, or spacecraft
- Manufacturing aerospace engines, propulsion units, and related parts
- Developing and manufacturing airframe structural components
- Researching and developing aerospace products and systems
- Providing aerospace prototype manufacturing
- Performing aerospace systems integration
- Manufacturing space vehicles and launch vehicles
- Producing guided missiles and space vehicle propulsion units

### Primary Subsectors

#### Commercial Aviation (NAICS 336411-336413)
Design, manufacture, and assembly of civil aircraft for passenger and cargo transport, including narrow-body and wide-body jets, regional aircraft, business jets, and general aviation aircraft.

**Key Players**: Boeing, Airbus, Embraer, Bombardier, Gulfstream, Cessna, Cirrus Aircraft

**Major Products**: Commercial airliners (737, A320 families), wide-body aircraft (787, A350), regional jets (E-Jets, CRJ series), business jets, turboprops, helicopters

#### Defense & Military Aerospace (NAICS 336414-336419)
Development and production of military aircraft, rotorcraft, unmanned aerial systems (UAS), guided missiles, space systems, and related defense technologies for government and military customers.

**Key Players**: Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, Raytheon, BAE Systems, General Dynamics, Saab, Dassault Aviation

**Major Products**: Fighter jets (F-35, F/A-18, Eurofighter), bombers (B-21), military transport, attack helicopters, missiles, missile defense systems

#### Space Industry (NAICS 336414-336415)
Manufacturing of satellites, launch vehicles, spacecraft, space station components, and ground support equipment for civil, commercial, and military space applications.

**Key Players**: SpaceX, Blue Origin, United Launch Alliance, Northrop Grumman, Arianespace, Rocket Lab

**Major Products**: Launch vehicles (Falcon 9, Vulcan, Ariane 6), satellites (communications, Earth observation, navigation), crew capsules (Crew Dragon, Starliner), space station modules

#### Aircraft Engines & Propulsion (NAICS 336412)
Design and manufacture of aircraft engines, including turbofan, turbojet, turboprop, turboshaft engines, and advanced propulsion systems for commercial, military, and space applications.

**Key Players**: General Electric Aviation, Pratt & Whitney, Rolls-Royce, Safran, Honeywell Aerospace

**Major Products**: High-bypass turbofans (LEAP, Trent XWB, PW1000G), military engines (F135, F414), APUs, small engines

#### Aerospace Parts & Components (NAICS 336413)
Manufacturing of airframe structural components, avionics, landing gear, flight control systems, hydraulics, environmental systems, and other specialized aerospace components.

**Key Players**: Collins Aerospace, Safran Landing Systems, Spirit AeroSystems, Triumph Group, AAR Corp

**Major Products**: Fuselage sections, wing assemblies, landing gear, flight controls, cabin systems, nacelles

### Supporting Sectors

#### Aircraft Maintenance, Repair & Overhaul (MRO) (NAICS 488190, 811310)
Comprehensive maintenance, repair, overhaul, and modification services for aircraft, engines, and components to ensure airworthiness and extend service life.

#### Aerospace Supply Chain (Multi-tier Suppliers)
Complex network of thousands of specialized suppliers providing raw materials, components, assemblies, and services across multiple tiers:
- **Tier 1**: Major subsystem integrators (Collins Aerospace, Safran, Spirit AeroSystems)
- **Tier 2**: Component manufacturers (fasteners, bearings, actuators, electronics)
- **Tier 3+**: Raw materials and specialty materials (titanium, composites, superalloys)

#### Urban Air Mobility (Emerging Sector)
Development of electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft, air taxis, autonomous aerial vehicles, and supporting infrastructure for urban and regional transportation.

**Key Players**: Joby Aviation, Archer Aviation, Lilium, Volocopter, EHang, Wisk Aero

## APQC Process Classification Framework

The aerospace industry leverages the **APQC Aerospace & Defense Industry Extension**, which provides industry-specific process frameworks aligned with the unique characteristics of aerospace operations.

### Key APQC Process Areas for Aerospace

#### 1.0 Develop Vision and Strategy
- **1.1.4** Develop aerospace market intelligence and competitive analysis
- **1.2.3** Define aerospace product portfolio strategy (commercial, defense, space)
- **1.3.2** Establish aerospace technology roadmaps and R&D priorities

#### 2.0 Develop and Manage Products and Services
- **2.1** Govern and manage aerospace product development portfolio
- **2.2** Develop aerospace products and services (5-10 year development cycles)
  - **2.2.1** Design aerospace products (conceptual, preliminary, detailed design)
  - **2.2.2** Develop aerospace prototypes and conduct flight testing
  - **2.2.3** Obtain aerospace regulatory certifications (FAA, EASA, military certification)
  - **2.2.4** Prepare aerospace products for production
- **2.3** Commercialize aerospace products (product launch, market introduction)
- **2.4** Manage aerospace product lifecycle
  - Configuration management
  - Obsolescence management
  - Product support and sustainment

#### 3.0 Market and Sell Products and Services
- **3.1** Understand aerospace markets and customers (airlines, military, space agencies)
- **3.2** Develop aerospace marketing strategy (airshows, demonstrations, campaigns)
- **3.3** Develop aerospace sales strategy (long sales cycles, customer financing)
- **3.4** Develop and manage aerospace marketing plans
- **3.5** Develop and manage aerospace sales plans (major account management)

#### 4.0 Deliver Products and Services
- **4.1** Plan for and align aerospace supply chain resources
  - Multi-tier supplier management
  - Long-lead item procurement
  - Critical material sourcing (titanium, composites, superalloys)
- **4.2** Procure aerospace materials and services
  - Supplier qualification and AS9100 certification
  - Supplier performance management
  - Strategic supplier partnerships
- **4.3** Produce/Manufacture aerospace products
  - **4.3.1** Schedule aerospace production (rate planning, flow management)
  - **4.3.2** Execute aerospace manufacturing (assembly, integration, testing)
  - **4.3.3** Manage aerospace production quality (AS9100, NADCAP, military standards)
  - **4.3.4** Manage aerospace production inventory (work-in-process, serialized tracking)
- **4.4** Deliver aerospace products
  - Flight testing and customer acceptance
  - Aircraft delivery and customer training
  - Documentation and certification packages
- **4.5** Manage aerospace orders and customer service
  - Configuration management
  - Retrofit and modification programs
  - Technical support and field service

#### 5.0 Manage Customer Service (Aerospace Aftermarket)
- **5.1** Develop aerospace customer care strategy
- **5.2** Plan and manage aerospace customer service operations
  - Parts provisioning and distribution
  - Technical publications and manuals
  - Service bulletins and airworthiness directives
- **5.3** Perform aerospace maintenance, repair, and overhaul (MRO)
  - Heavy maintenance checks (C-checks, D-checks)
  - Engine overhaul and testing
  - Component repair and exchange programs
- **5.4** Manage aerospace product warranties and recalls
- **5.5** Resolve aerospace customer complaints and inquiries

#### 8.0 Manage Information Technology
- **8.1** Manage aerospace IT strategy and governance
- **8.2** Manage aerospace enterprise architecture
  - Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) systems
  - Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES)
  - Supply chain visibility platforms
- **8.3** Manage aerospace IT infrastructure
- **8.4** Deploy aerospace IT solutions
  - Digital twin technology
  - Predictive maintenance analytics
  - Factory automation and Industry 4.0

#### 10.0 Manage Environmental Health and Safety (EHS)
- **10.1** Determine aerospace EHS strategy and regulatory compliance
  - Aviation safety management systems (SMS)
  - Aerospace chemical and materials regulations
  - Export control and ITAR compliance
- **10.2** Design and construct/acquire aerospace facilities
- **10.3** Develop and manage aerospace EHS programs
  - Occupational safety in aerospace manufacturing
  - Hazardous materials handling
  - Environmental compliance (emissions, waste)
- **10.4** Manage aerospace safety incidents and emergencies
- **10.5** Manage aerospace regulatory compliance

#### 11.0 Manage External Relationships (Aerospace Specific)
- **11.1** Build aerospace investor relationships
- **11.2** Manage aerospace government and regulatory relationships
  - FAA, EASA, CAA regulatory engagement
  - Defense contracting and acquisition programs
  - Space agency partnerships (NASA, ESA)
- **11.3** Manage aerospace board and stakeholder relations
- **11.4** Manage aerospace legal and ethical issues
  - Export control (ITAR, EAR)
  - Trade compliance
  - Intellectual property protection
- **11.5** Manage aerospace public relations

#### 12.0 Manage Knowledge, Improvement, and Change
- **12.1** Manage aerospace organizational change
- **12.2** Manage aerospace continuous improvement (Lean, Six Sigma)
- **12.3** Measure and benchmark aerospace performance
- **12.4** Manage aerospace knowledge and intellectual assets
  - Engineering knowledge management
  - Lessons learned databases
  - Configuration management
- **12.5** Implement aerospace quality management systems
  - AS9100 quality management
  - NADCAP special process certifications
  - Military quality standards (MIL-STD)

## Key Industry Trends

### Sustainable Aviation
Commitment to net-zero carbon emissions by 2050 through sustainable aviation fuels (SAF), hydrogen propulsion, electric aircraft, and operational efficiency improvements.

### Advanced Manufacturing
Adoption of additive manufacturing (3D printing), automation, robotics, digital twins, and Industry 4.0 technologies to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and enable complex geometries.

### Digitalization
Integration of artificial intelligence, machine learning, IoT sensors, and big data analytics across design, manufacturing, operations, and maintenance to optimize performance and reduce costs.

### Space Commercialization
Rapid growth of commercial space economy including satellite constellations, space tourism, in-space manufacturing, and cislunar infrastructure development.

### Urban Air Mobility
Emergence of eVTOL aircraft and autonomous aerial systems for urban and regional transportation, creating new markets and regulatory frameworks.

### Supply Chain Resilience
Focus on supply chain visibility, risk management, diversification, and nearshoring in response to global disruptions and geopolitical tensions.

### Advanced Materials
Development and deployment of lightweight composites, advanced alloys, additive materials, and smart materials to improve performance and reduce weight.
