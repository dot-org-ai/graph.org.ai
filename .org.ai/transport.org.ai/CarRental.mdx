# Car Rental

The car rental industry provides temporary vehicle access for personal and business use, ranging from hourly car sharing to long-term leases. This sector has evolved from traditional airport-focused rental to include diverse mobility solutions including peer-to-peer sharing and subscription services.

## Overview

Car rental services, classified under **NAICS 5321 (Automotive Equipment Rental and Leasing)**, provide short-term and long-term vehicle access without ownership. The industry encompasses traditional rental companies, car sharing platforms, and emerging subscription models.

### Industry Segments

**By Service Model:**
- **Traditional Rental**: Daily/weekly rentals, airport locations (NAICS 532111)
- **Car Sharing**: Hourly/by-the-minute usage, distributed vehicles (Zipcar, Car2Go)
- **Peer-to-Peer (P2P)**: Individual car owners renting to others (Turo, Getaround)
- **Vehicle Subscription**: Monthly all-inclusive vehicle access
- **Long-Term Leasing**: Multi-month rentals, lease alternatives (NAICS 532112)

**By Location:**
- **Airport Rental**: On-airport and near-airport facilities
- **Neighborhood**: Local branches and urban locations
- **Free-Floating**: Park-anywhere car sharing
- **Station-Based**: Dedicated pickup/return locations

**By Customer Segment:**
- **Leisure/Tourism**: Vacation and personal travel
- **Business Travel**: Corporate and business rentals
- **Insurance Replacement**: Accident and repair rentals
- **Relocation**: Moving and temporary relocation

**Enterprise Holdings:**
- Market Position: Largest car rental company globally
- Brands: Enterprise Rent-A-Car, National Car Rental, Alamo Rent A Car
- Fleet Size: 2+ million vehicles
- Locations: 10,000+ worldwide
- Business Model: Neighborhood and airport locations

**Key Differentiators:**
- Home-city market focus (Enterprise)
- "We'll pick you up" service
- Insurance replacement market leadership
- Extensive suburban network
- Local area marketing

**Hertz Global Holdings:**
- Brands: Hertz, Dollar, Thrifty
- Fleet Size: 500,000+ vehicles
- Locations: 10,000+ worldwide
- History: Founded 1918, bankruptcy 2020, emerged 2021
- Focus: Airport locations, business travelers

**Services:**
- Hertz Gold Plus Rewards
- EV rental expansion
- Uber/Lyft driver rentals
- Premium and luxury vehicle options

**Avis Budget Group:**
- Brands: Avis, Budget, Zipcar
- Fleet Size: 500,000+ vehicles
- Locations: 10,000+ worldwide
- Stock Symbol: CAR (NASDAQ)

**Brand Positioning:**
- Avis: Premium business travel ("We Try Harder")

### Car Sharing Platforms

**Zipcar (Avis Budget Group):**
- Founded: 2000
- Model: Station-based car sharing
- Coverage: 500+ cities globally
- Members: 1+ million
- Pricing: Hourly or daily, includes gas and insurance
- Vehicle Access: Mobile app or RFID card
- Target: Urban residents without personal vehicles

**Car2Go / ShareNow:**
- Founded: 2008 (Daimler), merged with DriveNow (BMW) 2019
- Model: Free-floating car sharing
- Cities: Major European and North American cities
- Fleet: Smart cars, BMW, Mercedes
- Status: Exited North America 2019, focus on Europe

**Getaround:**
- Founded: 2009
- Model: Peer-to-peer car sharing with Connect technology
- Coverage: U.S. cities and Europe
- Technology: Keyless entry via smartphone
- Insurance: $1M liability coverage included

### Peer-to-Peer Platforms

**Turo:**
- Founded: 2009 (as RelayRides)
- Model: "Airbnb for cars" - peer-to-peer car rental
- Inventory: 300,000+ vehicles from individual owners
- Coverage: U.S., UK, Canada, France, Australia
- Age Range: Vehicles from vintage to new
- Insurance: Marketplace insurance programs
- Host Earnings: 65-90% of trip price

**Business Model:**
- Hosts list vehicles with pricing and availability
- Renters book through app/website
- Insurance packages for protection
- Airport and delivery options
- Range from economy to exotic vehicles

**HyreCar:**
- Founded: 2015
- Model: P2P rentals for rideshare/delivery drivers
- Target: Uber, Lyft, DoorDash drivers
- Insurance: Commercial coverage options
- Rental Periods: Weekly/monthly focus

### Subscription Services

- Model: Monthly car subscriptions

- Digital-first experience

**Current Subscription Providers:**
- **Canvas (by Ford/GM)**: Multi-brand access

## Business Model

### Traditional Rental Economics

**Revenue Model:**
- Base rental rates (daily, weekly)
- Mileage charges (unlimited vs. per-mile)
- Fuel charges (prepaid, refueling service)
- Insurance/damage waivers (LDW/CDW)
- Equipment rentals (GPS, child seats, ski racks)
- Additional driver fees
- Young/senior driver surcharges
- Airport concession fees (passed to customer)

**Cost Structure:**
- Vehicle depreciation (largest cost, 30-40%)
- Vehicle acquisition and financing
- Facility lease costs
- Labor (counter staff, shuttle drivers, maintenance)
- Insurance and claims
- Maintenance and cleaning
- Marketing and distribution
- Airport concession fees (10-20% of revenue)

**Fleet Management:**
- Vehicle lifecycle: 12-18 months typical
- Residual value risk management
- Remarketing through used car channels
- Manufacturer fleet programs
- Mix optimization (economy to luxury)

### Car Sharing Economics

**Pricing Models:**
- **Hourly**: $8-15/hour depending on vehicle
- **Daily**: $60-100/day
- **Membership**: Monthly fees or pay-per-use
- **Discounts**: Off-peak, weekend, long-term

**Revenue Streams:**
- Usage fees (time and mileage)
- Membership fees
- Late fees and penalties
- Fuel surcharges
- Cleaning fees
- Damage charges

**Cost Drivers:**
- Vehicle acquisition and depreciation
- Parking (station-based model)
- Fuel and charging (EVs)
- Maintenance and cleaning
- Insurance
- Technology platform costs
- Vehicle relocation/rebalancing

### P2P Platform Economics

**Turo Revenue Model:**
- 10-35% commission on bookings
- Varies by insurance/protection plan
- Additional services (delivery, airport)
- Young driver fees

**Host Economics:**
- Revenue: $500-$2,000/month typical
- Costs: Insurance, cleaning, maintenance, depreciation
- Occupancy rates vary by location and vehicle
- High-end/unique vehicles command premium rates

**Benefits to Hosts:**
- Offset vehicle ownership costs
- Monetize idle vehicle time
- Flexible listing (block dates)
- Platform handles payment/insurance

## Regulatory Environment

### Insurance Requirements

**Liability Coverage:**
- Minimum state requirements
- Supplemental Liability Insurance (SLI)
- Personal effects coverage
- Roadside assistance

**Damage Coverage:**
- Loss Damage Waiver (LDW) / Collision Damage Waiver (CDW)
- Not insurance, waives renter liability
- Pricing: $15-40/day
- Alternatives: Personal auto, credit card coverage

**P2P Platform Insurance:**
- Marketplace insurance programs
- $1M liability standard
- Physical damage coverage tiers
- Host protection plans

### Airport Regulations

**Concession Agreements:**
- Customer facility charges (CFC)
- Concession fees (10-20% of revenue)
- Facility requirements
- Operating standards
- Environmental requirements

**Consolidated Rental Car Facilities:**
- Shared facilities for all operators
- Reduced on-airport footprint
- Shuttle bus requirements
- Cost sharing among operators

### Age Restrictions

**Minimum Age:**
- Typically 21 years (25 for some vehicle classes)
- Young driver surcharges ($15-35/day)
- Varies by location and company
- New York state: Cannot charge under-25 fee

**Maximum Age:**
- Generally no maximum (discrimination concerns)
- International: Some countries have age limits
- Senior discounts (AARP, AAA)
