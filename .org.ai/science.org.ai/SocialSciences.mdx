# Social Sciences

## Overview

Social sciences comprise the academic disciplines that study human society and social relationships. These sciences employ rigorous methods to understand human behavior, culture, institutions, economics, and social structures. Social science research combines quantitative analysis with qualitative understanding to address questions about how people live, work, and interact.

## Definition

Social sciences are academic disciplines concerned with the study of society and human behavior. They apply scientific methods to investigate institutions, relationships, behaviors, and systems that structure human life, seeking to understand and improve social phenomena.
