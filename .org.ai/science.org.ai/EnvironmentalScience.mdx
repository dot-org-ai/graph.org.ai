# Environmental Science

## Overview

Environmental science is an integrative field studying natural systems and human-environment interactions. It encompasses research on climate, weather, oceans, ecosystems, biodiversity, and environmental challenges ranging from global climate change to local pollution management. Environmental scientists work to understand environmental processes and develop sustainable solutions.

## Definition

Environmental science is the study of the environment and the solution of environmental problems through integrating physical, biological, and social sciences. It addresses how humans interact with the natural world and seeks sustainable approaches to managing environmental resources and challenges.

## Core Disciplines

### Atmospheric Science and Meteorology

Study of Earth's atmosphere and weather processes.

**Subdomains:**
- **Meteorology** - Weather and atmospheric dynamics
  - Thermodynamics of atmosphere
  - Atmospheric circulation patterns
  - Weather prediction and forecasting
  - Extreme weather events
- **Climatology** - Long-term atmospheric patterns
  - Climate classification systems
  - Climate variability and oscillations
  - Paleoclimate reconstruction
  - Regional climate analysis
- **Air Quality Science** - Atmospheric pollution
  - Air pollutant chemistry
  - Ozone formation and depletion
  - Particulate matter dynamics
  - Health effects of air pollution
  - Emission sources and control
- **Atmospheric Physics** - Physical processes in atmosphere
  - Radiation and energy balance
  - Cloud formation and precipitation
  - Lightning and electrical processes
  - Aerosol physics
- **Climate Modeling** - Computational climate systems
  - General circulation models (GCMs)
  - Climate projection methods
  - Ensemble forecasting
  - Earth system modeling

### Oceanography

Study of oceans and marine systems.

**Subdomains:**
- **Physical Oceanography** - Ocean circulation and dynamics
  - Ocean currents and gyres
  - Thermohaline circulation
  - Wave mechanics
  - Coastal oceanography
- **Chemical Oceanography** - Ocean chemistry
  - Salt and nutrient cycles
  - Ocean acidification
  - Redox reactions
  - Carbon cycling in oceans
- **Biological Oceanography** - Marine life and ecosystems
  - Phytoplankton ecology
  - Marine food webs
  - Deep-sea biology
  - Coral reef ecosystems
- **Marine Geology** - Seafloor and sediments
  - Plate tectonics
  - Seafloor spreading
  - Sediment transport
  - Paleoceanography
- **Marine Pollution** - Ocean contaminants
  - Plastic pollution
  - Oil spills
  - Mercury and heavy metals
  - Nutrient overload and dead zones

  - Plate tectonics
  - Faulting and folding

- **Paleontology** - Fossil organisms and evolution

  - Extinction events
  - Stratigraphic dating
- **Geochemistry** - Chemical composition of Earth
  - Elemental cycles
  - Radiometric dating
