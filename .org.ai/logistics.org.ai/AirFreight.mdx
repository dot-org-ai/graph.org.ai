# Air Freight and Cargo

Air cargo transportation services providing rapid, long-distance freight movement via scheduled airlines, cargo charter operations, and integrated express carriers under NAICS 481.

## Overview

Air freight represents the fastest mode of commercial transportation, moving high-value, time-sensitive, and perishable goods across global supply chains. While accounting for less than 1% of global freight tonnage, air cargo represents approximately 35% of international trade value, moving over $6.8 trillion in goods annually.

The air cargo industry generates over $175 billion in annual revenue, with integrated express carriers (FedEx, UPS, DHL) and combination passenger/cargo airlines serving diverse market segments from overnight documents to outsized industrial equipment.

- Government and military contracts

### Air Cargo Handling

**Ground Handling Operations:**
- Ramp services: aircraft loading/unloading
- Cargo buildup: palletizing and containerization
- Break-down: deconsolidation of inbound cargo
- Screening and security inspection
- Customs and import/export documentation
- Warehouse storage and cross-dock operations

**Unit Load Devices (ULD):**
- **Pallets**: 88" × 125" (LD3, PMC, PGA configurations)
- **Containers**: LD3 (60" × 61" × 64"), LD7, LD9, LD11
- Standardized to fit aircraft cargo holds
- Quick loading/unloading for fast turnarounds
- Temperature-controlled ULDs for pharma and perishables

**Dangerous Goods Handling:**
- IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR)
- Certified dangerous goods handlers
- Lithium battery restrictions and packaging
- Proper labeling and documentation
- Segregation and compatibility requirements

## Air Cargo Pricing and Economics

### Pricing Structure

**Chargeable Weight Calculation:**
- **Actual Weight**: Gross weight in kilograms
- **Volumetric Weight**: (Length × Width × Height cm) / 6,000
- **Chargeable Weight**: Greater of actual or volumetric weight
- Example: 50 kg box at 100 × 80 × 60 cm = 80 kg volumetric (80 kg charged)

**Rate Components:**
- **Freight Charge**: Per-kg rate based on weight break (e.g., +45 kg, +100 kg, +300 kg)
- **Fuel Surcharge**: Variable based on jet fuel prices
- **Security Surcharge**: Post-9/11 screening costs
- **Handling Charges**: Pickup, delivery, documentation
- **Accessorial Charges**: Dangerous goods, temperature control, oversized

**Typical Rate Structure:**
- 0-45 kg: $5.00/kg (small shipment premium)
- 45-100 kg: $3.50/kg
- 100-300 kg: $2.80/kg
- 300-500 kg: $2.40/kg
- 500-1000 kg: $2.10/kg
- 1000+ kg: $1.80/kg (volume discount)

### Capacity and Demand Dynamics

**Peak Season (Q4):**
- October-December e-commerce and holiday surge
- Capacity constraints and premium pricing
- Charter aircraft supplementing scheduled service
- Rates increase 30-50% vs. off-peak

**Belly Cargo Impact:**
- Passenger aircraft provide 50% of global air cargo capacity
- COVID-19 pandemic reduced passenger flights, reducing cargo capacity
- Freighter aircraft rates surged during pandemic
- Gradual normalization as passenger travel recovers

**Spot vs. Contract Rates:**
- Contract rates: negotiated annual or quarterly agreements
- Spot rates: market-driven, variable pricing
- Spot premium during capacity crunches
- Long-term contracts provide cost stability

### Cargo Terminal Operations

**Import Processing:**
1. Aircraft arrival and unloading
2. ULD breakdown and deconsolidation
3. Customs clearance and documentation
4. Security screening and inspection
5. Storage or immediate delivery
6. Pickup by freight forwarder or trucker

**Export Processing:**
1. Shipper drop-off or forwarder delivery
2. Acceptance and documentation review
3. Security screening (100% screening mandate)
4. ULD buildup and palletization
5. Transfer to aircraft loading area
6. Loading and departure

**Customs and Border Protection:**
- Automated Commercial Environment (ACE) in US
- Electronic filing of import/export declarations
- Risk-based targeting and inspection
- Bonded warehouses for duty deferral
- Foreign Trade Zones (FTZ) at airports

## Regulations and Compliance

### Safety and Security

**TSA Cargo Screening:**
- 100% screening of cargo on passenger aircraft (US)
- Certified Cargo Screening Program (CCSP)
- Known Shipper Program
- Indirect Air Carrier (IAC) regulations
- X-ray, ETD (explosives trace detection), canine inspection

**IATA Regulations:**
- Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR) updated annually
- Live Animals Regulations (LAR)
- Perishable Cargo Regulations (PCR)
- Temperature Control Regulations (TCR)
- Lithium Battery Shipping Guidelines

**FAA and ICAO Standards:**
- Aircraft airworthiness and maintenance
- Crew duty time limitations
- Air traffic control coordination
- International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) standards

### Customs and Trade

**Import/Export Documentation:**
- Air Waybill (AWB): contract of carriage
- Commercial invoice
- Packing list
- Certificate of origin
- Import licenses and permits
- Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) classification

**Trade Programs:**
- **C-TPAT (Customs-Trade Partnership Against Terrorism)**: Trusted shipper program
- **AEO (Authorized Economic Operator)**: EU trusted trader
- **FAST (Free and Secure Trade)**: Expedited border clearance

### Environmental Regulations

**Carbon Emissions:**
- IATA Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation (CORSIA)
- EU Emissions Trading System (ETS)
- Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) adoption
- Fleet modernization to fuel-efficient aircraft

## Industry Trends

### E-Commerce Growth

**Drivers:**
- Cross-border e-commerce exceeding $4 trillion globally
- Direct-to-consumer shipments from Asia to US/Europe
- Faster delivery expectations (2-3 days international)
- Small package volumes growing 8-10% annually

**Network Adaptations:**
- Dedicated e-commerce freighter routes
- Smaller aircraft for regional distribution
- Customs pre-clearance programs
- Last-mile delivery integration

### Cold Chain and Pharma

**Pharmaceutical Logistics:**
- COVID-19 vaccine distribution highlighting cold chain criticality
- GDP (Good Distribution Practice) compliance
- Temperature-controlled ULDs and active containers
- Real-time temperature monitoring (IoT sensors)
- Qualified passive packaging solutions

**Cold Chain Infrastructure:**
- Airport pharma-certified facilities (IATA CEIV Pharma)
- Dedicated temperature-controlled zones
- Backup power and refrigeration systems
- Validation and qualification protocols

### Digitalization and Technology

**Cargo Management Systems:**
- CargoWise for freight forwarding
- Cargo.one for instant booking and pricing
- WebCargo (Freightos) for digital marketplace
- Descartes MacroPoint for tracking
- CHAMP Cargosystems for airline cargo IT

**E-AWB (Electronic Air Waybill):**
- Paperless documentation
- 80%+ e-AWB adoption globally
- IATA ONE Record data sharing standard
- Blockchain for transparency and traceability

**Autonomous and Drone Cargo:**
- Autonomous cargo aircraft development
- Drone delivery for last-mile and remote areas
- Zipline medical drone deliveries in Africa
- Regulatory frameworks evolving

### Sustainability

**Fuel Efficiency Initiatives:**
- New-generation aircraft (A350F, 777X freighter)
- Fuel-efficient flight planning and routing
- Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) from renewable sources
- Electric and hybrid cargo aircraft for short-haul

**Packaging and Waste Reduction:**
- Reusable ULDs and packaging materials
- Right-sized packaging to reduce volumetric weight
- Recycling programs for cargo materials
- Carbon-neutral shipping options

### Consolidation and Partnerships

**Airline Alliances and Joint Ventures:**
- SkyTeam Cargo, WOW Alliance
- Code-sharing and interline agreements
- Coordinated scheduling and capacity
- Shared handling and infrastructure

**Forwarder Consolidation:**
- M&A activity creating larger global players
- DSV acquisition of Panalpina
- Technology investment to compete with integrators
- Control tower and visibility platforms
