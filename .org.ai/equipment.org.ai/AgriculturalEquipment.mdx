# Agricultural Equipment

Agricultural equipment encompasses the machinery and implements used in modern farming operations, from soil preparation and planting to harvesting and post-harvest processing. This category is classified under NAICS 33311 (Agricultural, Construction, and Mining Machinery Manufacturing).

## Overview

Agricultural equipment has revolutionized farming, enabling mechanization of labor-intensive tasks, increasing productivity, and supporting precision agriculture practices. Modern farm equipment integrates advanced technology including GPS guidance, data analytics, and automation.

### Major Equipment Categories

#### Tractors

The workhorse of modern agriculture, providing power and versatility:

- **Utility Tractors**
  - Power range: 40-100 HP
  - Category I and II 3-point hitch
  - PTO (Power Take-Off): 540 and 1000 RPM
  - Applications: Small to medium farms, livestock operations
  - Loader compatibility and mid-mount mowers
  - Models: John Deere 5E Series, Kubota M7, Massey Ferguson 4700

- **Row Crop Tractors**
  - Power range: 100-400+ HP
  - High-clearance design for crop access
  - Advanced transmissions (CVT, powershift)
  - GPS guidance and autosteer
  - Large fuel capacity for long days
  - Models: John Deere 8R Series, Case IH Magnum, New Holland T7

- **Specialty Tractors**
  - Vineyard and orchard tractors: Narrow design, ROPS
  - High-clearance tractors: For tall crops
  - 4WD articulated tractors: 400-600+ HP for heavy tillage
  - Track tractors: Superior traction and reduced compaction
  - Models: John Deere 9RX, Case IH Steiger Quadtrac

#### Harvesting Equipment

Specialized machinery for crop collection:

- **Combine Harvesters**
  - Grain harvesting for wheat, corn, soybeans, rice
  - Engine power: 300-600+ HP
  - Grain tank capacity: 200-450 bushels
  - Header width: 20-50 feet
  - Cleaning system: Sieves, fans, rotary separators
  - Yield monitoring and mapping
  - Models: John Deere S700 Series, Case IH Axial-Flow, Claas Lexion

- **Corn Heads and Flex Headers**
  - Corn heads: 6-18 rows, row-unit spacing 30"
  - Flex headers: 20-50 foot cutting width for soybeans
  - Draper headers: Belt-driven cutting platforms
  - Stripper headers: For rice and other grain crops

- **Cotton Harvesters**
  - Picker and stripper designs
  - 4-8 row capacity
  - Module building capability
  - GPS guidance for precise row following
  - Models: John Deere CP690, Case IH Module Express

- **Forage Harvesters**
  - Self-propelled: 400-900+ HP
  - Pull-type: PTO-driven, 100-200 HP
  - Kernel processing for corn silage
  - Cut length adjustment
  - Models: John Deere 8000 Series, Claas Jaguar

#### Planting Equipment

Precision equipment for seed placement:

- **Planters**
  - Row spacing: 15", 20", 30", 36", 38"
  - 6-48 row configurations
  - Vacuum or finger pickup seed meters
  - Variable rate seeding capability
  - Precision depth control
  - Down-force monitoring
  - Liquid fertilizer application
  - Models: John Deere DB60, Kinze 4900, Case IH Early Riser

- **Seed Drills**
  - Small grain seeding (wheat, oats, barley)
  - Width: 10-60 feet
  - No-till and minimum-till capability
  - Air seeder systems for large-scale operations
  - Models: John Deere 1890, Case IH SDX Air Cart

- **Transplanters**
  - Vegetable and tobacco transplanting
  - 2-8 row designs
  - Water injection systems
  - Operator stations for manual feeding
  - Automated plant spacing

#### Tillage Equipment

Soil preparation and management implements:

- **Primary Tillage**
  - Moldboard plows: 3-12 bottom, 16"-20" bottoms
  - Chisel plows: 9-47 shanks, working depths 6-12"
  - Disk rippers: Deep tillage with leveling action
  - Subsoilers: Break hardpan, 18-30" depths
  - Working width: 10-60 feet

- **Secondary Tillage**
  - Tandem disk harrows: 10-40 feet wide
  - Field cultivators: S-tine and C-shank designs
  - Harrows: Spring tooth, spike tooth, chain harrows
  - Rotary tillers: PTO-driven, 4-10 feet wide
  - Finishing speeds: 5-8 mph

- **Vertical Tillage**
  - Vertical tillage tools: Low-disturbance soil mixing
  - Gang angles: 0-15 degrees
  - Working depths: 1-4 inches
  - Residue sizing and incorporation
  - Models: Salford RTS, Kuhn Krause Excelerator

#### Application Equipment

Machinery for crop inputs and amendments:

- **Sprayers**
  - Self-propelled: 90-120 foot booms, 1000-1600 gallon tanks
  - Engine power: 250-350 HP
  - GPS guidance and section control
  - Variable rate application
  - Pulse width modulation (PWM) nozzles
  - Pull-type: 60-90 foot booms, 800-1200 gallons
  - Models: John Deere R4045, Case IH Patriot, AGCO RoGator

- **Fertilizer Spreaders**
  - Dry spreaders: Spinner and pneumatic types
  - Capacity: 200-1000 cubic feet
  - Variable rate capability
  - Wet kits for liquid application
  - Strip-till bars with in-row fertilizer

- **Manure Applicators**
  - Tank spreaders: 2000-8000 gallon capacity
  - Injection systems: Reduce odor and nutrient loss
  - Drag hose and toolbar injectors
  - Solid manure spreaders: 200-700 bushel capacity

#### Haying Equipment

Equipment for forage production:

- **Mowers**
  - Disc mowers: 7-16 foot cutting width
  - Mower conditioners: Crimping and crushing rollers
  - Sickle bar mowers: Traditional design
  - Self-propelled windrowers: 12-25 foot headers
  - Models: Kuhn GMD, New Holland Discbine

- **Balers**
  - Round balers: 4x4, 4x5, 5x6 foot bales, 800-2000 lbs
  - Large square balers: 3x3x8, 3x4x8, 4x4x8 feet, 1000-2000 lbs
  - Small square balers: 14"x18" bales, 40-80 lbs
  - Variable chamber and fixed chamber designs
  - Net wrap and twine tying systems
  - Models: John Deere 9 Series, New Holland BigBaler

- **Rakes and Tedders**
  - Wheel rakes: 10-20 foot working width
  - Rotary rakes: V-rake and parallel bar designs
  - Tedders: Aerate and dry hay, 12-25 foot width

#### Livestock Equipment

Specialized machinery for animal agriculture:

- **Feed Mixers**
  - Vertical mixers: 150-1500 cubic foot capacity
  - Horizontal mixers: Ribbon and auger designs
  - TMR (Total Mixed Ration) preparation
  - Load cells for precise mixing
  - Truck-mounted and stationary designs

- **Milking Equipment**
  - Parlor systems: Herringbone, parallel, rotary
  - Milking units and automation
  - Bulk tanks: 300-10,000 gallon capacity
  - Milk cooling and quality monitoring

- **Livestock Handling**
  - Squeeze chutes and head gates
  - Sorting gates and alleyways
  - Loading chutes
  - Scales and identification systems

#### Specialty Crop Equipment

Equipment for fruits, vegetables, and specialty crops:

- **Potato Equipment**
  - Planters: 2-12 row, cup and pick systems
  - Harvesters: Self-propelled and pull-type
  - Grading and storage equipment

- **Vegetable Harvesters**
  - Carrot harvesters: Top removal and lifting
  - Onion harvesters: Undercutting and windrow
  - Green bean and pea viners
  - Sweet corn pickers

- **Orchard and Vineyard Equipment**
  - Tree shakers and catching frames
  - Grape harvesters: Over-the-row designs
  - Pruning equipment
  - Sprayers: Air blast and tower designs

## Precision Agriculture Technology

### GPS and Guidance Systems

Modern farming integrates positioning technology:

- **Auto-steer Systems**: RTK GPS for sub-inch accuracy
- **Section Control**: Automatic implement shut-off
- **Variable Rate Application**: Prescription maps for inputs
- **Field Mapping**: Yield monitoring and as-applied records
- **Automatic Headland Turns**: Reduced operator fatigue

### Sensors and Monitoring

Data-driven decision making:

- **Yield Monitors**: Real-time harvest data collection
- **Soil Sensors**: EC mapping, compaction, moisture
- **Crop Sensors**: NDVI and plant health monitoring
- **Equipment Monitors**: Performance and diagnostics
- **Weather Stations**: On-farm microclimate data

### Data Management Platforms

- **John Deere Operations Center**
- **Climate FieldView (Bayer)**
- **CNH AFS Connect**
- **AgLeader SMS Software**
- **Trimble Ag Software**

## Technical Specifications

### Power Requirements

Agricultural equipment power delivery:

- **PTO Standards**: 540 RPM (1-3/8" shaft), 1000 RPM (1-3/4" shaft)
- **Hydraulics**: Open center and closed center systems
- **Electrical**: 12V and 24V systems, ISO 11783 (ISOBUS)
- **Tire Sizes**: From compact (25x8.5-14) to large ag (710/70R42)

### Implement Attachment Systems

Standardized connections:

- **3-Point Hitch**: Category 0, I, II, III, IV
- **Quick Hitch**: Allows fast implement changes
- **Drawbar**: Fixed and swinging designs
- **Front Loaders**: SSQA (Skid Steer Quick Attach)

## Maintenance Programs

### Scheduled Maintenance

Regular service intervals:

- **Daily**: Fluid checks, visual inspections, lubrication points
- **50-100 hours**: Oil and filter changes
- **200-500 hours**: Hydraulic filters, transmission service
- **Annual**: Safety inspections, storage preparation
- **Seasonal**: Pre-planting and pre-harvest checks

### Seasonal Storage

Proper equipment storage extends life:

- Cleaning and washing
- Lubrication of exposed metal
- Tire pressure adjustment
- Battery maintenance
- Indoor storage when possible
- Fuel stabilization

## Economic Considerations

### Equipment Sizing

Matching capacity to operation:

- **Acres per hour**: Planter = width × speed × efficiency / 8.25
- **Harvest capacity**: Bushels per hour based on yield
- **Total hours available**: Planting/harvest windows
- **Field efficiency**: Turns, overlap, obstacles (70-85%)

### Ownership vs. Custom Hire

Decision factors:

- **Equipment costs**: Purchase price, depreciation, interest
- **Operating costs**: Fuel, maintenance, repairs, storage
- **Utilization**: Hours per year, acres covered
- **Custom rates**: Local availability and pricing
- **Timeliness**: Control over scheduling

### Depreciation and Replacement

Farm equipment economics:

- **Depreciation**: 10-15% first year, 8-10% thereafter
- **Trade-in cycles**: Typically 3-7 years for major equipment
- **Residual value**: Brand, condition, hours, technology
- **Tax considerations**: Section 179, bonus depreciation
