# Space Defense

Space defense encompasses the military systems, satellites, and operations that enable modern warfare. This includes reconnaissance satellites, communications constellations, missile defense systems, and emerging space force capabilities that provide decisive military advantages through space-based superiority.

## Overview

Space has become a critical warfighting domain with systems enabling:

- **Satellite reconnaissance** - Global surveillance capability
- **Communications networks** - Global connectivity for command and control
- **Navigation systems** - GPS and military positioning
- **Missile defense** - Ballistic missile detection and intercept
- **Early warning systems** - Ballistic missile launch detection
- **Space operations** - Military activities in orbital and suborbital space
- **Anti-satellite systems** - Weapons targeting orbital systems

## Military Satellite Systems

### Reconnaissance Satellites

<ReconnaissanceSatellites />

**Optical Reconnaissance Satellites:**
- **KH-11/12 (Keyhole) Satellites** - High-resolution panchromatic imaging
- **KH-11 Extended Duration** - Long-mission high-resolution imaging
- **Advanced KH Series** - Multi-spectral and hyperspectral capabilities
- **Characteristics:**
  - Resolution: 10-30 cm (publicly acknowledged is coarser)
  - Orbit: Sun-synchronous for consistent lighting
  - Lifespan: 8-10 years on-orbit
  - Coverage: Global, including polar regions
  - Sensors: Visible light, infrared, multi-spectral bands

**Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) Satellites:**
- **Lacrosse/Onyx satellites** - All-weather radar reconnaissance
- **Capabilities:**
  - Penetrates clouds, rain, and darkness
  - High resolution imaging in all weather
  - 1-3 meter resolution capability
  - Active illumination (radar)
  - Global coverage without weather limitations
- **Uses:**
  - Ship monitoring and tracking
  - Armor and vehicle identification
  - Surface deformation and change detection
  - Urban mapping and demolition assessment

**Ocean Surveillance Satellites:**
- **Mercury (Vortex) satellites** - Ship detection and tracking
- **Capabilities:**
  - Wide-area ocean coverage
  - Ship classification and identification
  - Fleet movement tracking
  - Naval warfare support
  - Maritime domain awareness

**Signals Intelligence (SIGINT) Satellites:**
- **VORTEX/Magnum/Orion** - Communications intelligence collection
- **Characteristics:**
  - Geostationary orbit for persistent collection
  - Global coverage from stationary position
  - Communications interception capability
  - Multi-spectral signal detection
  - Strategic-level intelligence gathering

**Emerging Satellite Systems:**
- **Next Generation Overhead Persistent Infrared (NGOS)** - Improved missile warning
- **Military Space Plane (X-37B)** - Reusable orbital platform
- **Space Mobility and Logistics (SML)** - Cargo delivery to orbit
- **Hypersonic Glide Vehicle tracking** - Advanced threat monitoring

### Communications Satellites

<CommunicationsSatellites />

**Military SATCOM Systems:**
- **Milstar satellites** - Secure military communications
- **Advanced Extremely High Frequency (AEHF)** - Next-generation secure SATCOM
- **Ultra High Frequency Follow-On (UFO)** - Tactical communications satellite
- **Defense Satellite Communications System (DSCS)** - Strategic communications

**Characteristics of Military SATCOM:**
- **Secure encryption** - Unbreakable communications
- **Anti-jamming** - Resistance to electronic attack
- **Worldwide coverage** - Global communication capability
- **Low probability of intercept (LPI)** - Hard to detect or locate
- **Multiple access** - Many users per satellite
- **High reliability** - Critical command communications

**Wideband Communications:**
- **Wideband Global SATCOM (WGS)** - High-capacity broadband communications
- **Capacity:** Multiple gigabits per second
- **Coverage:** Global and regional beams
- **Flexibility:** Reconfigurable coverage areas
- **Applications:** Full-motion video, imagery transmission, data distribution

**Commercial Space Communications:**
- **Competitive procurement** - Using commercial satellite capacity
- **Cost savings** - Commercial service less expensive
- **Frequency sharing** - Leasing commercial transponders
- **Advantages:** Flexibility, rapid deployment, cost-effective
- **Disadvantages:** Less security control, shared resources

### Navigation and Timing Satellites

<NavigationSatellites />

**Global Positioning System (GPS):**
- **Space segment:** 24+ operational satellites in medium Earth orbit
- **Accuracy:** 5-15 meters (civilian), <1 meter (military)
- **Application:** Targeting, navigation, timing synchronization
- **Military advantage:** Precision-guided munitions accuracy
- **Resilience:** Multiple satellite redundancy for coverage

**GPS Modernization:**
- **GPS III** - Improved signal strength and accuracy
- **GPS IIIF** - Flex variant for expanded service
- **Enhanced accuracy:** <1 meter civilian accuracy target
- **Multi-frequency transmission** - Improved robustness
- **Cross-link capability** - Satellite-to-satellite communication
- **Resistant to jamming** - Advanced signal encoding

**Alternative Navigation Systems:**
- **Russian GLONASS** - Global Navigation Satellite System
- **European Galileo** - Satellite navigation system
- **Chinese BeiDou** - Regional/global navigation constellation
- **Cooperative use** - Multiple system integration
- **Resilience:** Using multiple GNSS reduces jamming vulnerability

## Missile Defense Systems

### Ballistic Missile Defense

<BallisticMissileDefense />

**Strategic Defense:**
- **Ground-Based Midcourse Defense (GMD)** - Intercontinental ballistic missile defense
- **System components:**
  - Ground-based interceptors (GBIs) at Fort Greely, Alaska and Vandenberg AFB
  - AN/SPY-1 radar for target detection and tracking
  - Battle management system coordinating intercept
  - Command center for launch authorization
- **Capability:** Defending continental US from ICBM attack
- **Interceptor:** Kinetic kill vehicle with divert thrusters
- **Success rate:** Improving with successive tests

**Regional Ballistic Missile Defense:**
- **THAAD (Terminal High Altitude Area Defense)**
  - Mobile system for regional air defense
  - Engages aircraft, cruise missiles, ballistic missiles
  - Radar integrated with C4I system
  - Rapid deployment capability
  - Coverage: ~60km radius

- **Patriot Air Defense System**
  - PAC-3 variant for ballistic missile defense
  - Phased array radar for tracking
  - Command center coordination
  - Multiple platform deployment
  - Coverage: ~60-100km range

- **Aegis Ballistic Missile Defense**
  - Ship-based air and missile defense
  - Phased array radar for 3D tracking
  - SM-3 missiles for intercept
  - Naval fleet protection
  - Mobile, deployable worldwide

**Lower-Tier Defense:**
- **Phalanx CIWS (Close-In Weapon System)** - Last-resort air defense
- **ESSMs (Evolved Sea Sparrow Missiles)** - Short-range air defense
- **Stinger missiles** - Portable air defense
- **20mm/30mm guns** - Kinetic air defense

### Sensor Systems for Missile Defense

<MissileDefenseSensors />

**Radar Systems:**
- **AN/SPY-1 Phased Array** - Aegis system radar
- **AN/TPY-2 Radar** - THAAD system radar
- **AESA Radar** - Air defense active electronically scanned array
- **Capabilities:**
  - Multi-target simultaneous tracking
  - Long-range detection (200+ km)
  - Ballistic trajectory computation
  - Continuous beam steering
  - Weather-independent operation

**Infrared Sensors:**
- **Overhead Persistent Infrared (OPIR)** - Satellite-based missile detection
- **Forward-Based X-Band Radar** - Mobile deployed sensors
- **Sea-Based X-Band Radar** - Naval-deployed radar platform
- **Early warning distribution** - Real-time targeting data

**Battle Management:**
- **Command and control system** - Coordinating defense elements
- **Target track data fusion** - Integrating multiple sensor inputs
- **Engagement sequencing** - Prioritizing threats
- **Interceptor guidance** - Real-time trajectory updates

## Anti-Satellite Systems

### Ground-Based ASAT

<GroundBasedASAT />

**Air-Launched ASAT:**
- **ASAT missile** - Air-launched anti-satellite weapon
- **Characteristics:**
  - Launched from F-15 fighter aircraft
  - Kinetic kill vehicle for direct impact
  - High-altitude interception capability
  - Single use, consumable weapon
- **Status:** Retired in 1994, not currently operational
- **Successor programs:** Developing new ASAT capabilities

**Ground-Based ASAT Development:**
- **Directed energy weapons** - Lasers and high-power microwave
- **Kinetic energy weapons** - Direct impact projectiles
- **Debris concerns** - Orbital debris from satellite destruction
- **Testing controversies** - International concerns over debris

### Space-Based ASAT

<SpaceBasedASAT />

**Emerging Threat Systems:**
- **Satellite rendezvous and inspection spacecraft** - Proximity operations
- **Robotic arm systems** - Grappling and manipulation
- **Directed energy systems** - Space-based lasers and HPM
- **Fragmentation weapons** - Exploding payloads creating debris
- **Electromagnetic pulse (EMP) weapons** - Electronic system disruption

**Strategic Concerns:**
- **Proliferation** - Multiple nations developing ASAT capability
- **Orbital debris** - Weaponization creating collision hazard
- **Space arms race** - Accelerating military space competition
- **Strategic stability** - Implications for nuclear deterrence

## Emerging Space Defense Technologies

### Hypersonic Systems

<HypersonicSystems />

**Hypersonic Glide Vehicle (HGV):**
- **AGM-183 ARRW** - Air-Launched Rapid Response Weapon
- **DF-ZF/WZ-8** - Chinese hypersonic glide vehicle
- **Avangard** - Russian hypersonic warhead system
- **Characteristics:**
  - Speeds: Mach 5+ (>3,800 mph)
  - Unpredictable flight paths - evasion of air defense
  - Limited boost-phase warning
  - Limited midcourse tracking capability
  - Emerging defense challenge

**Defense Implications:**
- **Shortened warning time** - Reduced decision timeline
- **Trajectory unpredictability** - Difficult interception geometry
- **Advanced sensors needed** - Rapid acquisition and tracking
- **Boost-phase intercept** - Most viable defense approach
