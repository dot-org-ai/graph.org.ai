# Defense Services

Defense services encompass the full range of support operations that enable military readiness and effectiveness. This includes training, maintenance and repairs, logistics, supply chain management, and operational support services that keep military personnel and equipment ready for deployment.

## Overview

Modern military operations depend on sophisticated support systems and trained personnel:

- **Training and Education** - Military occupational skill development
- **Maintenance and Repair** - Equipment and vehicle sustainment
- **Logistics and Supply Chain** - Material movement and inventory management
- **Facilities Management** - Base and installation operations
- **Medical Support Services** - Healthcare and casualty care
- **Security and Protection Services** - Force protection and facility security
- **Consulting and Advisory Services** - Strategic planning and operational support
