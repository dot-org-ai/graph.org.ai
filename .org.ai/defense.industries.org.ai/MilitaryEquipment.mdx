# Military Equipment

Military equipment encompasses the weapons systems, vehicles, aircraft, and naval vessels that form the backbone of modern military operations. This category includes everything from individual combat weapons to large-scale platforms worth billions of dollars.

## Overview

Military equipment ranges across multiple categories and complexity levels:

- **Tactical Equipment** - Individual and small unit weapons and gear
- **Combat Vehicles** - Tanks, armored personnel carriers, light tactical vehicles
- **Aircraft** - Fighter jets, transport, helicopters, unmanned systems
- **Naval Vessels** - Warships, submarines, support vessels
- **Weapons Systems** - Missiles, rockets, artillery, air defense systems
- **Support Equipment** - Logistics, communications, command and control
