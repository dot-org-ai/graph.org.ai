# gaming.org.ai

Comprehensive ontology documentation for the global gaming industry, covering game development, platforms, publishing, hardware, and business models.

## Overview

This repository contains comprehensive MDX documentation for gaming.org.ai, part of the .org.ai ontology ecosystem. The gaming industry represents one of the largest entertainment sectors globally, generating over $180 billion annually across console, PC, mobile, and emerging platforms.

## Categories

The gaming.org.ai domain is organized into six comprehensive subcategories:

### 1. [Video Games](./VideoGames.mdx)

Covers the core game development and publishing segment, including:
- **Console Gaming**: PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, Nintendo Switch platforms
- **PC Gaming**: Steam, Epic Games Store, specialized PC gaming ecosystems
- **Mobile Gaming**: iOS, Android, casual and hardcore mobile games
- **Cloud Gaming**: Game streaming services and serverless gaming
- **Game Genres**: Action, RPG, Strategy, Sports, Simulation, Puzzle, Horror
- **Development Models**: AAA, AA, Indie game development
- **NAICS**: 5112 - Software Publishers

**Key Metrics**:
- Market Size: $184B (2024)
- Console: $52B (28%)
- PC: $40B (22%)
- Mobile: $92B (50%)

### 2. [Online Gaming](./OnlineGaming.mdx)

Covers multiplayer experiences, competitive gaming, and streaming:
- **Massively Multiplayer Online (MMOs)**: World of Warcraft, Final Fantasy XIV
- **Competitive Multiplayer**: MOBAs, Battle Royale, FPS games
- **Esports Infrastructure**: Professional teams, tournaments, organizations
- **Game Streaming**: Twitch, YouTube Gaming, platform economies
- **Social Systems**: Guilds, voice chat, community management
- **NAICS**: 5112 - Software Publishers, 711219 - Other Spectator Sports

**Key Metrics**:
- MMO Market: $12B
- Competitive Multiplayer: $25B
- Esports Market: $1.9B
- Streaming Platforms: 150M+ users (Twitch)

### 3. [Casino Gaming](./CasinoGaming.mdx)

Covers regulated gambling, sports betting, and gaming operations:
- **Slot Machines**: Video/mechanical slots, progressive jackpots
- **Table Games**: Blackjack, roulette, poker, baccarat
- **Poker Operations**: Cash games, tournaments, professional poker
- **Sports Betting**: Legal sports wagering, mobile betting platforms
- **Online/Mobile Gambling**: Digital casino platforms, social gaming
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Licensing, responsible gaming, AML/KYC
- **NAICS**: 7132 - Gambling Industries

**Key Metrics**:
- Global Gaming Revenue: $260B
- US Sports Betting: $120B+ wagered annually
- Online Casino: $45B
- Macau: $23B (largest market)

### 4. [Game Engines](./GameEngines.mdx)

Covers game development tools and technology platforms:
- **Major Engines**: Unreal Engine, Unity, Godot, GameMaker
- **Development Tools**: 3D modeling, animation, audio production
- **Middleware Solutions**: Physics, networking, audio engines
- **Collaboration Platforms**: Version control, asset management
- **Engine Economics**: Free-to-play, subscription, royalty models
- **Emerging Technologies**: AI integration, real-time ray tracing, neural rendering
- **NAICS**: 5112 - Software Publishers

**Key Metrics**:
- Engine Market: $3-4B annually
- Unity Market Share: 60%+ of all games
- Unreal: 25-30% AAA market share
- Godot: Rapidly growing open-source adoption

### 5. [Gaming Hardware](./GamingHardware.mdx)

Covers gaming devices, peripherals, and physical platforms:
- **Gaming Consoles**: PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, Nintendo Switch
- **PC Gaming Components**: GPUs (NVIDIA, AMD), CPUs, RAM, storage
- **Mobile Gaming Devices**: Smartphones, tablets, gaming phones
- **VR/AR Hardware**: Meta Quest, PlayStation VR2, AR glasses
- **Input Peripherals**: Controllers, keyboards, mice, racing wheels, arcade sticks
- **Emerging Tech**: Haptic suits, eye tracking, neural interfaces
- **NAICS**: 3344 - Electronic Components, 3341 - Computer Manufacturing

**Key Metrics**:
- Global Hardware Market: $50B+
- Console Market: $15B
- PC Components: $20B
- VR/AR: $3B (fastest growing segment)

### 6. [Game Publishing](./GamePublishing.mdx)

Covers business operations, marketing, monetization, and live services:
- **Publisher Types**: AAA, mid-tier, indie publishers
- **Distribution Channels**: Steam, Epic Games Store, console stores, mobile stores
- **Marketing Strategies**: Influencer partnerships, advertising, community building
- **Monetization Models**: Premium, F2P, subscription, cosmetics, gambling
- **Live Operations**: Seasonal content, battle passes, live events
- **Community Management**: Support, moderation, engagement
- **Localization & Regional Strategy**: 30+ languages, cultural adaptation
- **NAICS**: 5112 - Software Publishers

**Key Metrics**:
- Publishing Market: $70-90B (40-50% of industry)
- AAA Game Budget: $100-500M
- Battle Pass Conversion: 15-25%
- Marketing Allocation: 25-35% of revenue
