# Casino Gaming

Casino gaming encompasses traditional casino operations, online gambling platforms, sports betting, and the technology infrastructure supporting the gambling industry. This heavily regulated sector combines entertainment, hospitality, mathematics, and sophisticated technology to deliver games of chance and skill to millions of players worldwide.

## Overview

The casino gaming industry has evolved from traditional brick-and-mortar casinos to a global ecosystem including online platforms, mobile betting apps, and integrated resort experiences. The industry generates over $260 billion in annual gross gaming revenue globally, with rapid growth in online/mobile segments and sports betting following regulatory changes in many jurisdictions.

### Table Games

**Blackjack (21)**:
- Most popular table game globally
- House edge: 0.5% (perfect strategy) to 2%+
- Multiple variations (Spanish 21, Double Exposure, etc.)
- Card counting possible (legal but discouraged)
- 6-8 deck shoes standard
- Automatic shufflers common

**Roulette**:
- European (single zero) - 2.7% house edge
- American (double zero) - 5.26% house edge
- French (La Partage rule)
- Electronic/automated versions
- Popular in Europe and Asia

**Craps**:
- Complex dice game
- Lowest house edge (Pass/Don't Pass: 1.41%)
- Social/communal experience
- Intimidating for beginners
- Declining in popularity (slower pace)

**Baccarat**:
- Extremely popular in Asia (Macau's dominant game)
- Simple gameplay (banker vs. player)
- Low house edge (1.06% banker, 1.24% player)
- VIP/high-roller game
- Mini-baccarat for lower stakes
- Commission-free variations

**Poker Variations**:

## Casino Economics

### House Edge

**Typical House Edges**:
- Slots: 2-15% (average 5-8%)
- Blackjack: 0.5-2% (skill-dependent)
- Roulette: 2.7% (European), 5.26% (American)
- Craps: 1.4% (best bets) to 16%+ (worst)
- Baccarat: 1.06% (banker), 1.24% (player)
- Sports betting: 4.5-5% (hold on balanced action)

**Hold Percentage**:
- Percentage of drop retained by casino
- Slots: 5-8% of coin-in
- Table games: 15-25% of drop
- Variance in short term, converges over time

### Revenue Models

**Land-Based Casinos**:
- Gaming revenue: 60-70% (slots, tables)
- Hotel: 15-20%
- Food & Beverage: 10-15%
- Entertainment: 3-5%
- Other (retail, spa): 2-5%

**Online Casinos**:
- Gaming revenue: 90-95%
- Payment processing fees: -2-4%
- Marketing costs: 30-50% (customer acquisition)
- Technology/platform: 10-15%
- Licensing fees: 5-20% (jurisdiction-dependent)

**Sports Betting**:
- Hold: 5-7% of handle
- Operating margin: 20-30% of revenue
- Marketing: 40-60% of revenue (competitive market)

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

**Casino Floor Metrics**:
- Win per unit (slot) per day: $100-300
- Table game drop per hour
- Table game hold percentage
- Slot machine coin-in
- Average bet size
- Player time on device

**Online Metrics**:
- Monthly active users (MAU)
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Lifetime value (LTV)
- LTV:CAC ratio (target: 3:1 minimum)
- First-time depositor (FTD) conversion
- Return to player (RTP) percentage
