## Industry Overview

The mining and extraction sector encompasses the discovery, extraction, and initial processing of mineral resources used in energy production, manufacturing, construction, and industrial applications. The sector spans multiple commodity types, operating environments, and regulatory frameworks:

### Primary Commodity Categories

**Energy Minerals**:
- Coal (thermal and metallurgical)
- Oil and natural gas
- Uranium and radium

**Metal Ores**:
- Iron ore
- Copper, nickel, lead, zinc
- Gold, silver, precious metals
- Rare earth elements (REEs)

**Construction Materials**:
- Crushed stone
- Sand and gravel
- Dimension stone

**Industrial Minerals**:
- Clay and ceramics
- Gypsum
- Talc, feldspar, mica
- Silica sand (glass, foundry, frac sand)

### Operating Environments

**Onshore**:
- Surface (open-pit) mining
- Underground mining (deep shafts, drifts, slopes)
- Small-scale artisanal mining (informal sector)

**Offshore**:
- Oil and gas extraction (shallow water, deepwater, ultra-deepwater)
- Sand and gravel dredging
- Methane hydrate research

**Specialized**:
- In-situ recovery (uranium, leaching operations)
- Solution mining
- Alluvial/placer mining

### Value Chain Integration

Mining supplies raw materials to downstream industries including:
- Refining and metallurgy
- Petrochemical processing
- Manufacturing and construction
- Power generation and utilities
- Technology and electronics

## Regulatory and Compliance Framework

### Federal Agencies

**Bureau of Land Management (BLM)**
- Federal onshore mining permits and leases
- Environmental review (NEPA)
- Mining claim administration

**Bureau of Ocean Energy Management (BOEM)**
- Offshore federal oil/gas leasing
- Environmental assessment
- Development operations oversight

**Mine Safety and Health Administration (MSHA)**
- Coal and metal/nonmetal mine safety standards
- Inspection and enforcement
- Training and certification requirements

**Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)**
- Clean Air Act (CAA) - Air quality, emissions
- Clean Water Act (CWA) - Water quality, discharge permits
- RCRA - Hazardous waste management
- CERCLA - Site remediation

**U.S. Geological Survey (USGS)**
- Resource assessments and commodity data
- Technical guidance and research

### State Agencies

**Mining Permits**:
- State-level permitting for onshore operations
- Environmental review and approval

**Reclamation**:
- Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act (SMCRA) - Coal
- Hardrock reclamation - Varies by state

**Water Quality**:
- NPDES permit administration
- Discharge standards and monitoring

### Industry Standards and Guidance

**ASTM International**
- Aggregate testing and specifications (ASTM C33)
- Drilling, blasting standards

**American Petroleum Institute (API)**
- Drilling, production, safety standards

**International Council on Mining and Metals (ICMM)**
- Mine closure guidance
- Sustainability principles

**Society for Mining, Metallurgy & Exploration (SME)**
- Technical standards and best practices
- Professional development

## Key Topics by Document

### CoalMining.mdx

Comprehensive coverage of coal extraction including:
- Geology and coal types (lignite, bituminous, anthracite)
- Geographic production regions (Powder River Basin, Appalachia, Illinois Basin)
- Surface mining methods (area mining, contour mining, mountaintop removal)

- Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act (SMCRA) requirements

**Employment**: ~40,000 coal miners
**Major States**: Wyoming, West Virginia, Pennsylvania, Illinois

### MetalOreMining.mdx

Detailed examination of metallic mineral extraction:
- **Iron Ore**: Taconite processing, Minnesota/Michigan production
- **Copper**: Porphyry deposits, Bingham Canyon mine, processing (flotation, SX-EW)
- **Gold & Silver**: Heap leaching, refractory ore processing, underground mining
- **Rare Earth Elements (REEs)**: Mountain Pass mine, critical minerals, separation
- **Lead-Zinc**: Mississippi Valley-type deposits

- **Mineral Processing**: Comminution (crushing, grinding), flotation, gravity/magnetic separation
- **Tailings Management**: Storage methods, dam safety
- **Commodity Markets**: Pricing, investment economics

**Production**: ~50,000 metal mining jobs, $30+ billion annually
**Major States**: Nevada, Arizona, Utah, Minnesota, Alaska

## Key Concepts

### Commodity Types

**Energy Minerals**: Coal, oil, natural gas, uranium - primary energy sources
**Metal Ores**: Iron, copper, gold, rare earths - manufacturing, technology, defense
**Construction Materials**: Aggregate, stone, sand, gravel - infrastructure
**Industrial Minerals**: Specialty minerals for chemicals, ceramics, glass

### Operating Methods

**Surface Mining**: Removal of overburden to access ore/mineral, 65-80% of total mining volume
**Underground Mining**: Deep shafts or slopes accessing ore at depth, primarily coal, metals
**In-Situ Extraction**: Leaching without excavation, uranium ISR, solution mining
**Dredging**: Aquatic operations for sand, gravel, rare earth extraction

### Value Chain

**Exploration** → **Development** → **Production** → **Processing** → **Transportation** → **Refining/Manufacturing**

Mining represents the first step, extracting raw materials for downstream industries.

### Environmental and Social Dimensions

**Environmental Impacts**: Land disturbance, water pollution, greenhouse gas emissions, biodiversity loss
**Regulatory Framework**: Federal, state, local oversight; SMCRA for coal; varying hardrock requirements
**Reclamation Obligation**: Financial assurance, restoration to productive use
**Community Relations**: Employment, tax revenue, infrastructure impacts, opposition in some areas
**Just Transition**: Supporting affected communities as mines close, industries decline
