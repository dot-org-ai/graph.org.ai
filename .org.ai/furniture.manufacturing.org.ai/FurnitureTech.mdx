# Furniture Technology and Innovation

## Overview

Furniture technology encompasses the digital transformation of furniture design, manufacturing, and use through CAD/CAM systems, 3D printing, Internet of Things (IoT) integration, smart furniture, artificial intelligence, and advanced production automation. This sector represents the intersection of traditional furniture manufacturing and cutting-edge technology, enabling mass customization, improved efficiency, sustainability, and user-centered design. The furniture technology market is valued at approximately $8 billion globally and growing 15-20% annually, representing the fastest-evolving segment of the furniture industry.
