# furniture.org.ai

[![License: CC BY-SA 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY--SA%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

Comprehensive furniture industry ontology covering residential, commercial, outdoor, and sustainable furniture manufacturing, along with emerging technologies and innovations in the furniture sector.

## Overview

This repository contains MDX documentation for furniture.org.ai, part of the .org.ai ontology ecosystem. It provides detailed coverage of the furniture industry aligned with NAICS 337 (Furniture and Related Product Manufacturing) classification system.

**Parent**: [industries.org.ai](https://industries.org.ai)

## Industry Classification

**NAICS Division 337 - Furniture and Related Product Manufacturing** is organized into three main subsectors:

- **NAICS 3371** - Household and Institutional Furniture and Kitchen Cabinet Manufacturing
- **NAICS 3372** - Office Furniture (except Wood) Manufacturing
- **NAICS 3379** - Other Furniture-Related Product Manufacturing

### NAICS Detailed Breakdown

#### NAICS 3371: Household and Institutional Furniture and Kitchen Cabinet Manufacturing
- **33711**: Wood kitchen cabinet and countertop manufacturing
- **33712**: Household appliance manufacturing (sub-segment)
- **33721**: Upholstered household furniture manufacturing
- **33722**: Nonupholstered wood household furniture manufacturing
- **33723**: Metal household furniture manufacturing
- **33729**: Other household and institutional furniture manufacturing
- **33731**: Household and institutional furniture (milling and related)
- **33741**: Mattress, foundation and boxspring manufacturing

#### NAICS 3372: Office Furniture (except Wood) Manufacturing
- Metal office furniture
- Plastic and composite office furniture
- Ergonomic seating systems
- Modular furniture systems
- Filing and storage systems

#### NAICS 3379: Other Furniture-Related Product Manufacturing
- Mattress and spring manufacturing
- Furniture frame and wood component manufacturing
- Furniture parts and accessories
