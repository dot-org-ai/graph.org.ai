# Haircare

## Overview

The haircare industry encompasses the manufacture, distribution, and retail of products designed to cleanse, condition, style, treat, and maintain hair health and appearance. This includes shampoos, conditioners, styling products, treatments, and professional salon products. The global haircare market exceeds $95 billion annually and is growing at 5-7% per year. The sector is characterized by product specialization for different hair types, textures, damage levels, and aesthetic goals, as well as increasing demand for professional-grade products at retail price points.

## Manufacturing & Raw Materials

### Key Ingredients
- **Cleansing Agents**: Sulfates, sulfate-free alternatives, natural surfactants
- **Conditioning Agents**: Silicones, silicone alternatives, proteins, oils
- **Actives**: Keratin, biotin, collagen, plant extracts, amino acids
- **Scalp Health**: Zinc pyrithione, tea tree oil, salicylic acid, prebiotic extracts
- **Styling Polymers**: Acrylates, natural gums, flexible hold resins
- **Botanical Extracts**: Coconut, argan, jojoba, shea butter, plant oils

### Sustainable & Clean Beauty
- **Plant-Based Surfactants**: Coconut-derived, sugarcane-derived alternatives
- **Natural Fragrances**: Essential oils replacing synthetic fragrance compounds
- **Biodegradable Polymers**: Eco-friendly styling agents, natural gum replacements
- **Upcycled Ingredients**: Coffee grounds, grape seed extracts, waste-derived actives
- **Sustainable Packaging**: Recyclable, refillable, plastic-free options

### Manufacturing Processes
- **Emulsification**: Oil-water blending for creams and conditioners
- **pH Adjustment**: Critical for hair cuticle health and ingredient efficacy
- **Protein Infusion**: Bonding technology for keratin and collagen treatments
- **Aerosol Production**: Pressurized systems for sprays and mousses
- **Viscosity Control**: Creating desired texture and spreadability
- **Thermal Stability**: Heat-activated treatment formulations
