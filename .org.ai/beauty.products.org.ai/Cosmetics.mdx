# Cosmetics

## Overview

The cosmetics industry encompasses the manufacture, distribution, and retail of color cosmetics and makeup products including foundations, powders, eyeshadows, lipsticks, mascaras, blushes, and other facial color products. This sector represents one of the largest segments of the global beauty and personal care market, with annual revenues exceeding $60 billion globally. The cosmetics market is characterized by rapid innovation, strong brand loyalty, diverse consumer preferences across demographics, and increasing digital and online commerce penetration.

## Manufacturing & Production

### Raw Materials
- **Pigments**: Iron oxides, mica, titanium dioxide, FD&C colorants, natural colorants
- **Bases**: Oils (jojoba, argan, rosehip), waxes, silicones, polymers
- **Actives**: Antioxidants, anti-inflammatory ingredients, skin-conditioning agents
- **Additives**: Preservatives, emulsifiers, stabilizers, fragrance compounds
- **Sustainability Trends**: Plant-based pigments, natural waxes, palm-oil alternatives

### Manufacturing Processes
- **Color Cosmetics Blending**: Mixing pigments with base oils and binding agents
- **Milling**: Creating uniform particle size in powders
- **Emulsification**: Creating stable cream and liquid formulations
- **Filling & Packaging**: Automatic equipment for compact, tube, jar, and bottle filling
- **Quality Control**: Color matching, stability testing, microbiological testing
- **Sustainability**: Reduced water usage, energy-efficient processes, waste reduction

### Regulatory Requirements (FDA & International)
- **FDA Compliance**: Color additives approval, manufacturing standards, labeling requirements
- **International Compliance**: EU regulations (banned 1,300+ chemicals), China regulations (cruelty-free testing)
- **GMP Compliance**: Good Manufacturing Practices certification
- **Testing Requirements**: Safety testing, stability testing, patch testing for allergens

## Regulatory & Compliance Framework

### US Regulations (FDA)
- **Color Additives**: FDA Color Certification List compliance
- **Labeling Requirements**: Ingredient disclosure, warning labels, language requirements
- **Manufacturing**: GMP compliance, facility registration, adverse event reporting
- **Banned/Restricted Substances**: 11 banned colorants, restricted heavy metals

### International Regulations
- **EU (REACH)**: 1,300+ banned chemicals, stricter ingredient restrictions
- **China**: Recent legalization of non-animal testing methods for imported cosmetics
- **Japan**: Category cosmetics vs. quasi-drugs classification system
- **Canada (Health Canada)**: Similar to FDA with additional requirements
