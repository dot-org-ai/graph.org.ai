# Fragrance

## Overview

The fragrance industry encompasses the design, manufacture, distribution, and retail of scent products including perfumes, colognes, eau de toilette, body sprays, and scented personal care products. The global fragrance market exceeds $54 billion annually and is one of the fastest-growing segments of the beauty and personal care industry with growth rates of 7-10% annually. The sector is characterized by high profit margins, strong brand loyalty, luxury positioning, and the intersection of art, chemistry, and commerce through master perfumers and niche scent creation.

## Fragrance Composition & Chemistry

### Fragrance Structure (Scent Notes)
- **Top Notes**: Initial scent impression (5-15 minutes)
  - Examples: Citrus, bergamot, lemon, pink pepper, fruity notes
  - Function: Attention-grabbing, first impression creation

- **Heart/Middle Notes**: The main character (1-8 hours)
  - Examples: Florals (rose, jasmine), spices, herbs, fruit
  - Function: Core fragrance identity, complexity

- **Base Notes**: Foundation, lasting impression (4+ hours)
  - Examples: Vanilla, musk, sandalwood, patchouli, ambroxan
  - Function: Longevity, depth, grounding scent

### Fragrance Raw Materials

#### Natural/Essential Oil Sources
- **Floral Extracts**: Rose, jasmine, tuberose, gardenia, orange blossom
- **Citrus Oils**: Bergamot, lemon, grapefruit, bitter orange, neroli
- **Woody Bases**: Sandalwood, cedarwood, vetiver, oud (agarwood)
- **Spice Ingredients**: Cinnamon, clove, nutmeg, cardamom, pepper
- **Herbal/Green**: Mint, basil, lavender, thyme, sage
- **Animalic Notes**: Musk, ambergris alternatives, castoreum (increasingly synthetic)

#### Synthetic & Amplified Ingredients
- **Aldehydes**: Synthetic molecules creating soapy, fruity notes
- **Polycyclic Musks**: Synthetic musk compounds (increasingly restricted)
- **Aromatic Hydrocarbons**: Lab-created aromatic compounds
- **Odorants**: Specific synthetic molecules targeting scent receptors
- **Amplifiers**: Molecules enhancing fragrance performance and longevity
- **Fixatives**: Ingredients extending fragrance wear time

### Fragrance Concentration Levels
- **Fragrance Oil Percentage Determines Category**:
  - Pure Fragrance/Parfum: 20-30% oils
  - Eau de Parfum (EDP): 15-20% oils
  - Eau de Toilette (EDT): 5-15% oils
  - Eau de Cologne: 2-5% oils
  - Body Spray: 0.5-2% oils

## Manufacturing & Production

### Fragrance Creation Process
- **Perfume Composition**: Master perfumers (Noses) formulate scent blends
- **Sourcing**: Ingredient procurement from global suppliers
- **Blending**: Mixing oils, alcohol, and other ingredients in precise ratios
- **Maceration**: Infusing and blending time for scent development
- **Dilution**: Adding alcohol (ethanol) to achieve desired concentration
- **Filtration**: Clarifying and removing impurities
- **Quality Control**: Stability testing, scent stability over time

### Manufacturing Standards
- **IFRA Compliance**: International Fragrance Association standards
- **ISO Standards**: Quality assurance and consistency measures
- **Allergen Documentation**: Tracking allergenic fragrance ingredients
- **Stability Testing**: Ensuring scent remains consistent over shelf life
- **Microbiological Testing**: Safety and contamination prevention
- **Batch Traceability**: Source material tracking and documentation
