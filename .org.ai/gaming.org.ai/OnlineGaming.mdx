# Online Gaming

Online gaming encompasses multiplayer experiences, competitive esports, live streaming, and the social infrastructure that connects billions of players worldwide. This segment has transformed gaming from a solitary activity into a global social phenomenon, creating new industries in competitive gaming, content creation, and community platforms.

## Overview

Online gaming has evolved from simple text-based MUDs (Multi-User Dungeons) in the 1970s to massive persistent worlds supporting millions of concurrent players. The rise of broadband internet, cloud infrastructure, and mobile connectivity has made online gaming accessible to over 3 billion players globally. The industry now generates over $60 billion annually across subscriptions, microtransactions, esports, and streaming.

## Game Streaming

### Streaming Platforms

**Twitch** (Amazon)
- 140M+ monthly active users
- 7M+ unique streamers monthly
- 31B+ hours watched annually
- 70/30 revenue split (standard)
- Subscriptions: $5, $10, $25 tiers
- Bits (virtual currency)
- Ads revenue share

**YouTube Gaming** (Google)
- 100M+ daily gaming viewers
- Integration with YouTube main
- Better discoverability than Twitch
- 70/30 revenue split
- Super Chat/Super Stickers
- Channel memberships

**Kick** (Stake-backed)
- Launched 2022
- 95/5 revenue split (creator-friendly)
- Looser content policies
- Guaranteed pay for partners
- Growing but smaller audience

**Regional Platforms**:
- Huya (China) - 150M+ MAU
- Douyu (China) - 140M+ MAU
- BIGO Live (Southeast Asia)
- Nimo TV (Southeast Asia)
- Trovo (Tencent)

### Streaming Economics

**Revenue Streams**:
- Subscriptions: $2.50-$12.50 per sub (after platform cut)
- Donations/Tips: 100% to creator (minus payment processing)
- Ad revenue: $2-5 CPM typically
- Sponsorships: $10K-$100K+ per stream for top creators
- Affiliate marketing: 5-10% commission

**Top Streamers (by avg viewers)**:
- xQc (Felix Lengyel) - 70K+ avg viewers
- Kai Cenat - 60K+ avg viewers
- NICKMERCS - 30K+ avg viewers
- Shroud - 25K+ avg viewers
- Pokimane - 20K+ avg viewers

**Content Types**:
- Gameplay streaming (majority)
- Esports tournament broadcasts
- Speedrunning
- Just Chatting/IRL (growing category)
- Creative (art, music for games)
- Game development

### Streaming Technology

**Software**:
- OBS Studio (Open Broadcaster Software) - Free, most popular
- Streamlabs Desktop - OBS fork with integrated features
- XSplit - Premium alternative
- Twitch Studio - Beginner-friendly

**Hardware Requirements**:
- CPU: Intel i7/AMD Ryzen 7+ (for encoding)
- GPU: NVIDIA RTX series (NVENC encoding)
- RAM: 16GB+ recommended
- Upload speed: 6+ Mbps minimum
- Capture card: For console streaming

**Stream Settings**:
- Resolution: 1080p60 or 720p60
- Bitrate: 6,000 Kbps (Twitch max for non-partners)
- Encoding: x264 (CPU) or NVENC (GPU)
- Audio: 160-320 Kbps

## Technical Infrastructure

### Game Servers

**Server Models**:
- Dedicated servers (authoritative)
- Listen servers (peer-to-peer with host)
- Hybrid (client-side prediction + server validation)
- Cloud gaming (server-side rendering)

**Server Providers**:
- Amazon GameLift (AWS)
- Google Cloud Game Servers
- Microsoft Azure PlayFab
- Photon Engine (indie-friendly)
- Custom dedicated servers

**Performance Metrics**:
- Tick rate: 64-128 Hz (competitive), 20-30 Hz (casual)
- Latency: <50ms ideal, <100ms acceptable
- Jitter: <10ms variation
- Packet loss: <1%

### Matchmaking

**Matchmaking Systems**:
- Skill-based matchmaking (SBMM)
- ELO/MMR rating systems
- TrueSkill algorithm (Microsoft)
- Team balancing
- Geographic proximity
- Ping limitations
- Party size considerations

**Queue Types**:
- Ranked/Competitive (strict matchmaking)
- Unranked/Casual (looser matching)
- Arcade/Fun modes (wide skill range)
- Custom games (player-hosted)

### Anti-Cheat

**Anti-Cheat Systems**:
- Easy Anti-Cheat (Epic)
- BattlEye
- Vanguard (Riot - kernel-level)
- VAC (Valve Anti-Cheat)
- FairFight (behavioral analysis)

**Cheat Detection Methods**:
- Signature detection (known cheats)
- Behavioral analysis (inhuman actions)
- Server-side validation
- Player reports
- Machine learning anomaly detection

**Consequences**:
- Temporary bans (first offense)
- Permanent bans (repeated/severe)
- Hardware ID bans (evasion prevention)
- Public ban lists (some games)

## Challenges & Issues

### Toxicity & Harassment
- Verbal abuse in voice/text chat
- Griefing (intentional team sabotage)
- Swatting (dangerous prank calls)
- Doxxing (releasing personal information)
- Moderation at scale challenges

### Cheating & Exploits
- Aimbots, wallhacks (FPS)
- Bots for farming (MMOs)
- Script/macro usage
- Exploiting game bugs
- Account boosting/sharing

### Addiction & Health
- Gaming disorder (WHO classification)
- Excessive playtime concerns
- Loot box gambling concerns
- Physical health (posture, eyestrain)
- Mental health impacts

### Infrastructure Challenges
- DDoS attacks on servers
- Server outages during launches
- Scaling for concurrent users
- Geographic latency issues
- Cost of global infrastructure
