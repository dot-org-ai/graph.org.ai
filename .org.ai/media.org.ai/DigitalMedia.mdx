# Digital Media

Digital media encompasses internet-based content creation, distribution, and consumption including streaming services, social media platforms, online video, podcasts, and the creator economy.

## Overview

Digital media has transformed from a complement to traditional media into the dominant form of media consumption. Characterized by on-demand access, user-generated content, algorithmic distribution, and direct creator-to-audience relationships, digital media has fundamentally changed how content is created, discovered, and monetized.

**NAICS Code**: 519130 - Internet Publishing and Broadcasting and Web Search Portals
