# Gaming

The gaming industry encompasses video game development, publishing, distribution, esports, game streaming, and the broader interactive entertainment ecosystem.

## Overview

Gaming has evolved from a niche hobby into the largest entertainment medium globally, surpassing film and music combined. The industry spans mobile games, console gaming, PC gaming, cloud gaming, and emerging platforms like VR/AR. With esports, streaming, and the creator economy, gaming has become a multifaceted ecosystem combining software development, media distribution, live events, and community engagement.

**NAICS Code**: 5112 - Software Publishers (Games specifically)

**Note**: Gaming overlaps multiple NAICS codes including software publishing, streaming services, and live events.

## Industry Structure

### Game Development Pipeline

1. **Pre-Production**
   - Concept development
   - Prototyping
   - Technical planning
   - Team assembly
   - Budget and timeline
   - Engine selection

2. **Production**
   - Level design
   - Programming
   - Art creation (2D, 3D)
   - Animation
   - Sound design and music
   - Writing and narrative
   - Quality assurance (QA)

3. **Testing**
   - Alpha testing (internal)
   - Beta testing (closed/open)
   - Bug fixing
   - Balance adjustments
   - Performance optimization
   - Platform certification

4. **Launch**
   - Marketing and PR
   - Community building
   - Distribution setup
   - Day-one patch
   - Launch monitoring

5. **Live Operations**
   - Content updates
   - Seasonal events
   - Bug fixes and patches
   - Community management
   - Analytics and optimization
   - Monetization tuning

## Business Models

### Premium / Pay-to-Play

**Pricing**:
- AAA games: $60-70 (standard), $70-100 (deluxe editions)
- Indie games: $5-30
- Mobile premium: $1-10

**Distribution**:
- Digital: 90%+ of PC, growing on console
- Physical: Declining, console still significant
- Platform cuts: 30% (standard), 12% (Epic)

### Free-to-Play (F2P)

**Monetization**:
- In-app purchases (IAP)
- Virtual currency
- Cosmetics (skins, emotes)
- Battle passes (seasonal $10-20)
- Loot boxes (controversial, regulated in some regions)
- Gacha systems (mobile)

**Conversion Rates**:
- Typical F2P: 2-5% of players spend
- Average Revenue Per Paying User (ARPPU): $10-50/month
- Whales (top spenders): <1% of users, 50%+ of revenue

**Top F2P Games**:
- Fortnite: $5-6B annually
- Genshin Impact: $4B+ annually
- PUBG Mobile: $2-3B annually
- Candy Crush: $1-2B annually

### Subscription Models

**Game Subscriptions**:
- **Xbox Game Pass** - 25M+ subscribers, $10-17/month
- **PlayStation Plus** - 47M subscribers, $10-18/month
- **EA Play** - 6M+ subscribers, $5-15/month
- **Nintendo Switch Online** - 36M+ subscribers, $4-8/month
- **Apple Arcade** - 50M+ estimated, $5/month
- **Humble Choice** - Monthly game bundles

**MMO Subscriptions**:
- World of Warcraft: $15/month

### Live Services
