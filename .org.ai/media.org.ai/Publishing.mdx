# Publishing

Publishing industries create and distribute information and content through print and digital media, including newspapers, magazines, books, databases, and software.

## Overview

The publishing industry has undergone dramatic transformation in the digital era. Traditional print publishing has been complemented and in many cases supplanted by digital publishing, while software publishing has emerged as a massive new category. Publishers now distribute content across multiple platforms and formats, with business models evolving from one-time purchases to subscriptions and advertising-supported models.

**NAICS Code**: 511 - Publishing Industries (except Internet)

## Subsectors (3-digit)

Publishing comprises several distinct subsectors:

### 5111 - Newspaper, Periodical, Book, and Directory Publishers

Traditional content publishing focused on:

- **Newspapers** - Daily news, investigative journalism, local coverage
- **Periodicals** - Magazines, journals, academic publications
- **Books** - Fiction, non-fiction, textbooks, reference materials
- **Directories** - Business directories, telephone books, mailing lists

**Business Models**:
- Subscriptions (print and digital)
- Advertising revenue
- Single-copy sales
- Licensing and syndication

**Key Trends**:
- Digital transformation and paywalls
- Decline in print circulation
- Rise of digital subscriptions
- Mobile-first reading experiences
- Audio editions (audiobooks, podcasts)

### 5112 - Software Publishers

Companies that publish software products:

- **Operating Systems** - Windows, macOS, Linux distributions
- **Productivity Software** - Microsoft Office, Google Workspace
- **Enterprise Software** - ERP, CRM, SCM systems
- **Consumer Applications** - Photo editing, tax software, games
- **Mobile Apps** - iOS and Android applications
- **SaaS Products** - Cloud-based software services

**Business Models**:
- Perpetual licenses
- Subscription (SaaS, subscription software)
- Freemium (free basic, paid premium)
- In-app purchases
- Advertising-supported
- Open source with commercial support

**Key Trends**:
- Shift from perpetual licenses to subscriptions
- Cloud-native and SaaS dominance
- API-first and platform approaches
- Low-code/no-code tools
- AI-powered features and copilots

## Economics

### Publishing Industry Metrics

**Print Publishing**:
- Average book advance: $5,000-$50,000+
- Typical author royalty: 8-15% of list price
- Print run economics: 3,000-5,000 copies break-even
- Return rates: 20-40% for trade books

**Software Publishing**:
- Average SaaS gross margin: 70-80%
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Lifetime value (LTV)
- LTV:CAC ratio (target: 3:1 or higher)
- Churn rate (target: <5% annual for enterprise)

### Revenue Models

**Publishing Revenue Streams**:
1. Content sales (40-60%)
2. Subscriptions (20-40%)
3. Advertising (10-30%)
4. Licensing and rights (5-15%)

**Software Revenue Streams**:
1. Subscriptions (60-80%)
2. Professional services (10-20%)
3. Maintenance and support (5-15%)
4. Add-ons and extensions (5-10%)
