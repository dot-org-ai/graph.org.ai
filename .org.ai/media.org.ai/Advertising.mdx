# Advertising

Advertising encompasses the creation, placement, and management of promotional messages across all media channels, from traditional broadcast and print to digital platforms and social media.

## Overview

The advertising industry connects brands with consumers through paid media placements and creative campaigns. It spans strategic planning, creative development, media buying, and performance measurement. The industry has undergone massive transformation with the rise of digital advertising, programmatic buying, and data-driven targeting.

**NAICS Code**: 5418 - Advertising, Public Relations, and Related Services

## Advertising Channels

### Television Advertising

**Linear TV**:
- 30-second spots (standard)
- 60-second spots (premium)
- Prime time pricing: $200K-$800K per spot
- Sports premium (Super Bowl: $7M per 30-second spot)

**Connected TV (CTV)**:
- Streaming service ads
- Programmatic CTV buying
- Addressable TV
- Interactive ads

**Metrics**:
- Gross Rating Points (GRPs)
- Target Rating Points (TRPs)
- Cost Per Point (CPP)
- Reach and frequency

### Digital Display Advertising

**Ad Formats**:
- Banner ads (728x90, 300x250, 160x600)
- Native ads (in-feed, content recommendation)
- Rich media (interactive, expandable)
- Video ads (pre-roll, mid-roll, out-stream)

**Pricing Models**:
- CPM (Cost Per Thousand Impressions): $1-$50+
- CPC (Cost Per Click): $0.10-$50+
- CPA (Cost Per Action): Variable by action
- CPV (Cost Per View): Video views

**Programmatic Buying**:
- Real-Time Bidding (RTB)
- Private Marketplaces (PMP)
- Programmatic Guaranteed
- Supply-Side Platforms (SSPs)
- Demand-Side Platforms (DSPs)

### Search Advertising

**Search Engine Marketing (SEM)**:

- Shopping ads (product listings)

**Bidding & Targeting**:

**Performance Metrics**:
- Click-Through Rate (CTR)

**Growth Trends**:
- Digital growing 10-15% annually
- Traditional declining or flat
- CTV/streaming rapid growth
- Social media maturation

**Spending by Medium** (US, 2023):

### Industry Challenges

**Privacy & Regulation**:
- GDPR compliance (EU)
- CCPA/CPRA (California)
- Cookie deprecation (Chrome 2024)
- iOS App Tracking Transparency
- Privacy-preserving measurement

**Ad Fraud**:
- Invalid traffic (IVT)
- Bot fraud
- Domain spoofing
- Click fraud
- Industry losses: $100B+ annually

**Brand Safety**:
- Content adjacency concerns
- Platform moderation failures
- Misinformation spread
- Reputational risk

**Ad Blocking**:
- 25-30% of users (desktop)
- 15-20% (mobile)
- Premium publisher impact
- Acceptable ads programs
