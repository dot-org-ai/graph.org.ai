# agriculture.org.ai

[![License: CC BY-SA 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY--SA%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

Comprehensive agricultural ontology covering crop production, animal agriculture, aquaculture, agricultural technology, organic farming, and support services. Part of the NAICS 11 (Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing, and Hunting) classification system.

## Overview

This repository contains MDX documentation for agriculture.org.ai, a detailed ontology of agricultural sectors and practices, integrated with the broader .org.ai ecosystem.

**Parent**: [industries.org.ai](https://industries.org.ai)

## Types and Resources

### Core Agricultural Domains

1. **[Crop Production](./CropProduction.mdx)** (NAICS 111)
   - Grain farming (corn, soybeans, wheat, rice)
   - Vegetable and melon farming
   - Fruit and tree nut farming
   - Greenhouse, nursery, and floriculture
   - Other crop farming (cotton, sugarcane, hay, tobacco)
   - Precision agriculture in field crops
   - Soil management and nutrient management
   - Water management and irrigation systems
   - Integrated pest management and crop protection
   - Sustainable crop practices

2. **[Animal Production](./AnimalProduction.mdx)** (NAICS 112)
   - Cattle ranching and beef production
   - Dairy farming and milk production
   - Hog and swine production
   - Poultry farming (broilers and eggs)
   - Sheep and goat farming
   - Horse and equine farming
   - Animal feedlots and finishing operations
   - Animal health and veterinary management
   - Feed production and management
   - Manure management and waste utilization
   - Precision livestock farming

3. **[Aquaculture](./Aquaculture.mdx)** (NAICS 1125)
   - Finfish farming (catfish, trout, salmon, tilapia)
   - Shellfish farming (shrimp, oysters, clams, mussels)
   - Mollusk farming and hatcheries
   - Crustacean production
   - Aquatic plant farming
   - Recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS)
   - Pond and raceway management
   - Sustainable aquaculture practices
   - Disease management and biosecurity
   - Feed and nutrition management

4. **[Agricultural Technology](./AgriTech.mdx)** (Cross-cutting)
   - Precision agriculture and variable rate technology
   - Remote sensing (satellite, drone, ground-based sensors)
   - Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and drones
   - IoT sensors and sensor networks
   - Autonomous and robotic systems
   - Data analytics and artificial intelligence
   - Farm management information systems (FMIS)
   - Controlled environment agriculture (CEA)
   - Vertical farming and hydroponics
   - Blockchain for traceability
   - Cellular agriculture and alternative proteins

5. **[Organic Farming](./OrganicFarming.mdx)** (Cross-cutting)
   - USDA National Organic Program (NOP) standards
   - Organic crop production systems
   - Organic livestock and dairy production
   - Organic aquaculture
   - Soil health and carbon sequestration
   - Biodiversity and ecosystem services
   - Organic certification and compliance
   - Regenerative agriculture practices
   - Carbon farming and offset programs
   - Marketing and premiums for organic products
   - Agroecological design principles

6. **[Agricultural Services](./AgriculturalServices.mdx)** (NAICS 115)
   - Agronomic consulting and crop advising
   - Soil testing and fertility management
   - Pest management and scouting services
   - Custom application services (herbicide, fungicide, fertilizer)
   - Custom harvesting and drying
   - Equipment rental and leasing
   - Farm business management services
   - Farm financial planning and accounting
   - Labor services and staffing
   - Precision agriculture data services
   - Organic certification services
   - Environmental program management

## Key Features

### Comprehensive Coverage

- **Detailed NAICS mapping**: Each document aligns with specific NAICS categories
- **Market data**: Global and U.S. market sizes, growth rates, employment
- **Technical depth**: Agronomic practices, species characteristics, equipment specifications
- **Economic analysis**: Pricing, profitability, cost structures, business models
- **Sustainability**: Environmental impact, conservation practices, carbon sequestration
- **Certification standards**: Organic, regulatory, quality assurance

- **Crop-specific details**: Varieties, planting dates, harvest timing, yield data
- **Species information**: Livestock breeds, aquaculture species, growth parameters
- **Management practices**: Best practices, standard protocols, decision frameworks
- **Technology implementations**: Equipment, sensors, software platforms
- **Supply chain insights**: Marketing channels, value-added products, pricing

## Relationships and Dependencies

### Upstream Industries (Inputs)

- **Equipment Manufacturing**: Tractors, combines, implements, sensors, drones
- **Chemical Industry**: Fertilizers, pesticides, herbicides, growth regulators
- **Seed Industry**: Breeding, genetics, seed production and distribution
- **Feed Industry**: Grain milling, supplement manufacturing
- **Energy**: Fuel, electricity, renewable energy

### Downstream Industries (Outputs)

- **Food Processing**: Primary processing, value-added products
- **Food Manufacturing**: Consumer products, food ingredients
- **Food Retail**: Grocery stores, farmers markets, restaurants
- **Beverage Industry**: Processing, production, distribution
- **Textiles**: Cotton, wool, fiber products
- **Pharmaceuticals**: Botanical ingredients, active compounds

### Supporting Services

- **Financial Services**: Farming loans, equipment financing, crop insurance
- **Transportation**: Farm-to-processor, export logistics
- **Warehousing**: Grain elevators, cold storage, distribution
- **Professional Services**: Accounting, legal, consulting
- **Education**: Universities, extension services, training programs

import {
  CropProduction,
  AnimalProduction,
  Aquaculture,
  AgriTech,
  OrganicFarming,
  AgriculturalServices
