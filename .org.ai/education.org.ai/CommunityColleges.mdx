# Community Colleges

Two-year institutions awarding associate degrees, certificates, and providing workforce training and university transfer preparation (NAICS 6112).

## Overview

Community colleges are public, open-access institutions offering affordable post-secondary education for diverse student populations. They provide academic transfer programs preparing students for bachelor's degree completion, career and technical education leading to employment, adult basic education, and workforce development training. Community colleges serve as critical access points for underrepresented students, adult learners, and communities seeking local economic development.

## NAICS 6112 Breakdown

| Code | Industry | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **611210** | Junior Colleges | 2-year institutions offering associate degrees and certificates |

### Institution Types

- **Comprehensive Community Colleges**: Transfer, career-tech, continuing education
- **Technical Colleges**: Workforce-focused with limited transfer programs
- **Tribal Colleges**: Serving Native American communities
- **Urban Community Colleges**: Large metro-area institutions with multiple campuses
- **Rural Community Colleges**: Serving geographically isolated populations

## Institutional Characteristics

### Mission and Purpose

Community colleges fulfill multiple missions simultaneously:

- **University Transfer**: Associate of Arts (AA) and Associate of Science (AS) for bachelor's degree pathways
- **Career and Technical Education**: Associate of Applied Science (AAS) and certificates for immediate employment
- **Workforce Development**: Non-credit training, continuing education, industry partnerships
- **Remedial Education**: Developmental coursework in reading, writing, mathematics
- **Community Education**: Personal enrichment, adult basic education, ESL

### Governance

- **State Systems**: Multi-college districts with system-level coordination
- **Local Districts**: Elected or appointed boards of trustees
- **State Oversight**: State community college boards, coordinating commissions
- **Accreditation**: Regional accreditors plus programmatic accreditation for technical programs

### Funding Model

- **State Appropriations**: Base funding plus performance-based components
- **Local Taxes**: Property tax levies in many states
- **Tuition and Fees**: Typically 30-50% lower than public 4-year institutions
- **Federal Grants**: Pell Grants, TRIO programs, workforce development
- **Workforce Grants**: WIOA, employer-sponsored training contracts

## Academic Programs

### Credential Types

| Credential | Credits | Purpose |
|------------|---------|---------|
| **Certificate (Short-term)** | 12-29 credits | Entry-level skills for specific occupation |
| **Certificate (Long-term)** | 30-59 credits | Advanced occupational skills |
| **Associate of Arts (AA)** | 60-64 credits | Transfer to 4-year, humanities/social sciences emphasis |
| **Associate of Science (AS)** | 60-64 credits | Transfer to 4-year, STEM emphasis |
| **Associate of Applied Science (AAS)** | 60-72 credits | Terminal career-technical degree |
| **Associate of Arts/Science (AAS)** | 60-64 credits | Transfer-focused applied degrees in some states |

### Transfer Programs

- **General Education**: English, math, natural sciences, social sciences, humanities
- **Articulation Agreements**: Guaranteed admission and credit transfer to 4-year partners
- **2+2 Programs**: Structured pathways to bachelor's completion
- **Reverse Transfer**: Awarding associate degree after students transfer without completing
- **Transfer Centers**: Advising, university visits, application assistance

### Career and Technical Education (CTE)

#### High-Demand Program Areas

- **Health Sciences**: Nursing (ADN, LPN), dental hygiene, radiologic technology, medical assisting
- **Information Technology**: Cybersecurity, networking, software development, database administration
- **Advanced Manufacturing**: CNC machining, welding, industrial maintenance
- **Business**: Accounting, business management, office administration
- **Public Safety**: Criminal justice, fire science, emergency medical services
- **Trades**: HVAC, electrical, plumbing, automotive technology
- **Creative Arts**: Graphic design, digital media, culinary arts

#### Program Characteristics

- Industry-aligned curriculum with employer input
- Hands-on labs and clinical/internship experiences
- Industry certifications embedded in programs
- Advisory committees with employer representation
- Equipment donations and partnerships with industry

### Developmental Education

Addressing college-readiness gaps for students:

- **Assessment**: Placement testing (Accuplacer, Compass) or multiple measures
- **Course Sequences**: Pre-algebra through college algebra, reading through composition
- **Reforms**: Co-requisite remediation, accelerated pathways, compression of sequences
- **Support Services**: Math and writing labs, embedded tutoring, supplemental instruction

### Non-Credit Workforce Training

- **Customized Training**: Employer-specific skill development
- **Incumbent Worker Training**: Upskilling current employees
- **Industry Certifications**: CompTIA, AWS, welding, CDL, CNA
- **Apprenticeships**: Registered apprenticeship programs with classroom instruction
- **Continuing Education Units (CEUs)**: Professional development credits

## Student Population

### Demographics

Community college students are notably diverse:

- **Age**: 40% are 25 or older, significant adult learner population
- **Enrollment Intensity**: 60% part-time, balancing work and family obligations
- **First-Generation**: 40-50% are first in family to attend college
- **Low-Income**: Majority Pell-eligible, high food and housing insecurity rates
- **Race/Ethnicity**: Disproportionately Black, Hispanic, Native American students
- **Dual Enrollment**: Growing high school student participation

### Student Needs

- **Financial**: Affordability, financial aid, emergency assistance
- **Academic**: Developmental education, tutoring, study skills
- **Logistical**: Childcare, transportation, flexible scheduling
- **Career**: Job placement, resume assistance, interview preparation
- **Personal**: Mental health counseling, food pantries, housing support

## Enrollment and Student Services

### Admissions

- **Open Access**: Minimal or no admissions requirements for most programs
- **Placement Testing**: Assessment of math and English readiness
- **Selective Programs**: Competitive entry for nursing, allied health, some technical programs
- **Dual Enrollment**: Concurrent high school and college credit
- **Non-Degree Seeking**: Community education and workforce training

### Student Support Services

#### Academic Support

- **Advising**: Academic planning, course selection, transfer guidance
- **Tutoring**: Math centers, writing centers, peer tutoring
- **Library Services**: Research assistance, online databases, study spaces
- **Disability Services**: Accommodations, assistive technology, support

#### Wraparound Services

- **Financial Aid**: Pell Grants, state aid, institutional scholarships, emergency grants
- **Career Services**: Resume writing, job search, employer connections, job fairs
- **Counseling**: Personal, mental health, crisis intervention
- **Basic Needs**: Food pantries, emergency housing, childcare referrals, transportation assistance

#### Student Success Initiatives

- **Guided Pathways**: Structured programs with clear maps, default course schedules
- **Early Alert Systems**: Identifying struggling students for intervention
- **Intrusive Advising**: Required advising checkpoints, case management
- **Learning Communities**: Cohort-based linked courses with peer support
- **Summer Bridge**: Pre-enrollment programs for skill development and orientation

## Educational Technology

### Learning Management Systems (LMS)

- **Platforms**: Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, D2L Brightspace
- **Usage**: Course content, discussion boards, assignment submission, grading
- **Accessibility**: ADA compliance, screen reader compatibility

### Student Information Systems (SIS)

- **Systems**: Ellucian Colleague, PeopleSoft, Jenzabar, Workday Student
- **Functions**: Registration, financial aid, academic records, degree audit
- **Student Portal**: Self-service registration, grades, financial account

### Online Education

- **Fully Online Programs**: Complete degrees and certificates available remotely
- **Hybrid Courses**: Blended in-person and online components
- **HyFlex**: Student choice to attend synchronously in-person, synchronously online, or asynchronously
- **OER Adoption**: Open educational resources to reduce textbook costs

### Specialized Systems

- **CRM**: Salesforce, Ellucian CRM Recruit for student recruitment and retention
- **Starfish/Navigate**: Early alert, advising case management
- **eLumen/Taskstream**: Outcomes assessment and accreditation management
- **Concourse**: Financial aid packaging and compliance

## Faculty and Staffing

### Faculty Types

| Type | Proportion | Characteristics |
|------|------------|-----------------|
| **Full-Time Faculty** | 25-35% | Tenure-track or continuing contract, higher teaching load than universities |
| **Adjunct Faculty** | 65-75% | Part-time, course-by-course contracts, often employed elsewhere |
| **Dual Enrollment Instructors** | Growing | High school teachers with graduate credentials |

### Teaching Load

- **Full-Time**: 15 contact hours (5 courses) per semester typical
- **Adjunct**: Variable, often 3-9 credits per term
- **Service Expectations**: Advising, committee work, professional development

### Professional Development

- **Pedagogy**: Active learning, culturally responsive teaching, equity-minded practices
- **Technology Training**: LMS, online teaching certification, accessibility
- **Discipline-Specific**: Industry certifications, technical skill updates
- **Completion Focus**: Guided Pathways implementation, early alert training

## Financial Operations

### Revenue Sources

- **State Appropriations**: 40-50% of operating budget
- **Tuition and Fees**: 20-30% of revenue
- **Local Taxes**: Property tax levies (state-dependent, 10-30%)
- **Federal Grants**: Financial aid administration, workforce programs
- **Contracts**: Workforce training, facility rentals, international students

### Cost Structure

- **Instruction**: 45-55% of expenditures
- **Student Services**: 10-15%
- **Institutional Support**: 15-20%
- **Facilities/Maintenance**: 10-15%
- **Financial Aid**: Institutional scholarships and emergency grants

### Financial Challenges

- **Enrollment Declines**: Post-COVID enrollment drops, especially in liberal arts
- **State Funding Cuts**: Recession-driven reductions, slow recovery
- **Tuition Constraints**: Political pressure to keep tuition low
- **Rising Costs**: Healthcare, technology, deferred maintenance
- **Performance Funding**: State models tying funding to completion metrics

## Accreditation and Compliance

### Institutional Accreditation

- Same regional accreditors as 4-year institutions
- Self-study every 10 years with interim reporting
- Focus on student learning outcomes, completion, mission fulfillment

### Programmatic Accreditation

- **Nursing**: ACEN, CCNE for ADN programs
- **Allied Health**: CAAHEP, CoARC for various health programs
- **Culinary**: ACF accreditation
- **Automotive**: ASE Education Foundation

### Federal Compliance

- **Title IV**: Federal financial aid eligibility and compliance
- **Gainful Employment**: Career program outcome reporting
- **Clery Act**: Campus safety reporting
- **Title IX**: Sex discrimination prevention and response

### Performance Accountability

- **State Performance Metrics**: Completion, transfer, workforce outcomes
- **Voluntary Framework of Accountability (VFA)**: Community college-specific metrics
- **IPEDS Reporting**: Enrollment, completions, finance, human resources

## Industry Trends

### Enrollment Challenges

- Pandemic-accelerated enrollment declines (15-20% drops in 2020-2022)
- Opportunity costs: tight labor market making work more attractive than school
- Online competition from OPM-backed programs and bootcamps
- Demographic shifts: declining high school graduates in many states

### Reform Movements

- **Guided Pathways**: Structured program maps, default schedules, meta-majors
- **Co-Requisite Remediation**: Eliminating developmental course sequences
- **Equity Initiatives**: Disaggregating data, eliminating opportunity gaps
- **Transfer Reforms**: Guaranteed admission agreements, reverse transfer
- **Competency-Based Education**: Credit for prior learning, accelerated progression

### Workforce Alignment

- Short-term Pell eligibility for non-degree workforce programs
- Apprenticeship expansion and integration
- Stackable credentials building toward degrees
- Industry partnerships for equipment, curriculum, hiring

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Student Success Metrics

- Fall-to-fall retention rate
- 3-year graduation rate (for full-time, first-time students)
- 6-year completion rate (transfer + completion)
- Transfer rate to 4-year institutions
- Post-enrollment earnings (via state wage records)

### Access and Equity Metrics

- Pell recipient enrollment share
- First-generation student enrollment
- Equity gaps in completion by race/ethnicity
- Adult learner (25+) enrollment

### Operational Metrics

- Cost per completion
- Student-to-advisor ratio
- Adjunct faculty proportion
- Course fill rates and section productivity
