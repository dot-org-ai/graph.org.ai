# Agricultural Services (NAICS 115)

Agricultural services encompass a wide range of support activities for agricultural production, including consultation, equipment services, specialized labor, and custom application services. These support industries enable farmers to optimize production while managing costs and risk.

## Overview

The agricultural services industry:
- **Market value**: $45+ billion annually (U.S.)
- **Employment**: 400,000+ workers
- **Service diversity**: 100+ distinct service types
- **Industry consolidation**: Regional and national service providers
- **Technology adoption**: Digital platforms, data analytics, automated equipment
- **Business models**: Fee-based, commission-based, subscription services
- **Integration**: Aligned with farm size, crop type, and management intensity
- **Economic importance**: Enables farm efficiency and productivity

Key service categories:
- Agronomic consultation and crop advising
- Soil testing and fertility management
- Pest scouting and monitoring
- Custom application services
- Farm business management
- Equipment rental and maintenance
- Specialized labor services
- Organic certification and consulting
- Precision agriculture data services
- Farm marketing and value-added processing support

## Agronomic Consulting and Crop Advising

### Traditional Agronomic Consulting

**Independent Agronomists**:
- Hired by individual farmers or groups
- Field scouting and monitoring
- Fertility recommendations based on soil tests
- Pest and disease identification and management
- Crop variety selection and hybrid recommendations
- Planting and harvest timing guidance
- Problem solving and crop troubleshooting
- Economic analysis and input optimization

**Service Delivery Models**:
1. **Per-Acre Fee**: Fixed rate per acre ($5-$20/acre depending on services)
2. **Hourly Rate**: $75-$150+ per hour consulting time
3. **Retainer Model**: Monthly fee for ongoing relationship
4. **Commission-Based**: Percentage of input savings or yield improvements
5. **Value-Sharing**: Split savings or increased profits from recommendations

**Consulting Expertise Areas**:
- **Crop Nutrition**: Soil testing interpretation, fertilizer recommendations, tissue testing
- **Crop Protection**: Pest and disease identification, IPM strategy development, application timing
- **Variety Selection**: Hybrid/variety performance, adaptation to farm conditions, market fit
- **Soil Health**: Organic matter improvement, physical structure, biological activity
- **Water Management**: Irrigation scheduling, drainage design, soil moisture monitoring
- **Sustainability**: Carbon farming, conservation practices, organic conversion planning

**Agronomist Credentials**:
- Bachelor's degree in agronomy or related field (typical)
- Certified Crop Advisor (CCA) credential (voluntary, exam-based)
- Pesticide applicator certification (required for recommendations)
- Continuing education requirements
- Professional liability insurance

**Consultant Market Opportunities**:
- Specialty crops (high-value vegetables, fruits, nuts)
- Organic farming transition and certification support
- Precision agriculture implementation and optimization
- Regenerative and carbon farming consulting
- Export market requirements (food safety, traceability)

### Digital Agronomic Platforms

**Web and Mobile Applications**:
- Scouting tools with GPS field mapping
- Weather integration and growth modeling
- Disease and pest forecasting
- Spray timing and advisory recommendations
- Field note and record keeping
- Integration with equipment data

**Examples and Platforms**:
- **AgWorld**: Agronomist collaboration and farm data platform
- **FarmLogs**: Record-keeping and decision support
- **Granular** (now Corteva): Comprehensive farm management
- **CERES** (formerly Climate FieldView): Data analytics and recommendations
- **Scout Mobile**: Scouting and field monitoring
- **Pix4D**: Drone imagery analysis and mapping

**AI-Driven Recommendations**:
- Machine learning models for pest/disease prediction
- Optimal input timing based on weather and growth stage
- Yield prediction and potential assessment
- Variable rate prescription generation
- Weather-dependent recommendation alerts

### Soil Testing and Fertility Services

**Soil Testing Programs**:
- **Standard Testing**: pH, organic matter, NPK, micronutrients
- **Advanced Testing**: Biological testing, enzyme activity, aggregate stability
- **Precision Soil Sampling**:
  - Grid sampling (1 sample per acre-2 acres)
  - Zone sampling (5-20 acre zones)
  - Accuracy within 20% of true mean
- **Cost**: $10-$30 per sample including lab analysis

**Lab Services**:
- University extension labs (affordable, turnaround 1-2 weeks)
- Private labs (premium services, rapid turnaround)
- Interpretation and recommendation reports
- Digital platforms for record access and trending

**Fertility Recommendations**:
- Nutrient removal credits based on yield goals
- Organic matter maintenance calculations
- Lime requirement for pH adjustment
- Micronutrient supplementation when needed
- Custom blended fertilizer ordering
- Application timing and method optimization

**Tissue and Sap Testing**:
- In-season nutrient status assessment
- Crop nutrient demand modeling
- Side-dress or foliar application decisions
- Comparative analysis (field or variety comparison)
- $15-$40 per sample including analysis

### Tissue Analysis and Plant Diagnostics

**Plant Tissue Sampling**:
- Collection of leaf or plant material at growth stage
- Representative sampling (20-30 leaves per sample)
- Lab analysis for macro and micronutrients
- Interpretation relative to sufficiency ranges
- Field variability assessment

**Applications**:
- In-season nutrient status monitoring
- Variety performance comparison
- Problem diagnosis (deficiency symptoms)
- Harvest timing for quality (sugar content, acid levels)
- Organic production compliance verification

**Digital Crop Monitoring**:
- Imagery-based nutrient stress detection
- Reflectance measurements (NDVI, spectral analysis)
- Ground-based sensors and plant scanners
- Real-time variability assessment
- Early warning system for intervention

## Pest Management and Scouting Services

### Professional Scouting

**Field Scouting Programs**:
- Regular field visits during growing season
- Pest and disease population monitoring
- Damage assessment and economic thresholds
- Weed identification and mapping
- Insect trap monitoring (pheromone, light traps)
- Beneficial insect presence and activity
- Detailed field notes and records

**Service Delivery**:
- Weekly or bi-weekly visits during season
- Walk-through vs. systematic grid sampling
- Threshold-based recommendations
- Communication via reports, email, or app
- **Cost**: $10-$25 per acre/season or hourly rate

**Pest Identification and Management**:
- Insect ID, life stage, damage assessment
- Disease identification and stage of infection
- Weed species identification
- Economic threshold determination:
  - At what population does control become profitable?
  - Crop value, damage potential, control cost
- Integrated pest management (IPM) recommendations
- Pesticide application advice and timing

### Pest Prediction and Forecasting

**Disease Forecasting Models**:
- Fungal disease development based on weather
- Examples:
  - Apple scab (temperature, leaf wetness hours)
  - Downy mildew in grapes (rain and temperature)
  - Corn leaf blight (humidity and temperature)
- Predictive models provide spray recommendations
- Reduce unnecessary applications

**Insect Degree Day Models**:
- Growing degree days (GDD) track insect development
- Base temperature threshold (typically 50°F for many insects)
- Forecasting emergence and life stages
- Optimal timing for control measures
- Historical and real-time weather integration

**Weather-Based Services**:
- Integration with local or farm-specific weather stations
- Real-time weather data and forecast interpretation
- Disease and pest pressure alerts
- Spray advisory notifications
- Equipment and labor readiness assessment

### Specialized Pest Services

**Organic Pest Management**:
- Biological control implementation
- Beneficial insect habitat creation
- Organic-approved treatment recommendations
- Continuous monitoring and documentation
- Certification requirement verification

**Invasive Species Management**:
- Identification and population monitoring
- Treatment recommendations and planning
- Follow-up assessment and success evaluation
- Coordination with regulatory agencies
- Cost-sharing programs and grant assistance

**Pollinator Service and Management**:
- Native pollinator habitat creation and enhancement
- Honey bee health monitoring (for managed hives)
- Attractiveness assessment for pollinators
- Cover crop and flowering plant selection
- Documentation for land stewardship programs

## Custom Agricultural Services

### Custom Application Services

**Spray Application (Herbicide, Fungicide, Insecticide)**:
- Commercial applicators with equipment and credentials
- Ground-based sprayers with wide swath (40-120 feet)
- Aerial application (helicopter or drone)
- Targeted application for precision agriculture
- **Cost**: $8-$20/acre depending on product and application method

**Applicator Requirements**:
- Pesticide applicator license (state regulation)
- Proper equipment maintenance and calibration
- Records retention and reporting (spraying logs)
- Insurance and liability coverage
- Safe handling and environmental protection

**Application Quality Control**:
- Calibration before and during season
- Proper overlap and coverage assessment
- Weather-appropriate conditions (wind, temperature, rain)
- Tank contamination prevention (cleanout)
- Drift minimization (buffer zones, appropriate nozzles)

**Specialty Applications**:
- Fungicide application at critical growth stages
- Insecticide application for precision timing
- Growth regulator application
- Micronutrient foliar feeding
- Herbicide-resistant weed management

### Fertilizer Application Services

**Variable Rate Application (VRA)**:
- Site-specific nutrient application
- Prescription maps based on soil tests or yield zones
- Application equipment with flow control
- Reduced input use (10-30% savings) and environmental impact
- **Cost**: $3-$8/acre for VRA service

**Liquid Fertilizer Application**:
- Liquid nitrogen, phosphate, potassium application
- Anhydrous ammonia injection (N supply)
- Starter fertilizer application with planter
- Sidedress or foliar application
- **Cost**: $5-$15/acre depending on product and timing

**Manure Application Services**:
- Manure spreading and incorporation
- Animal waste management
- Nutrient utilization and environmental compliance
- Record-keeping for regulatory reporting
- **Cost**: $15-$40/acre

### Custom Harvesting and Drying

**Combine Custom Harvesting**:
- Grain harvest services (corn, soybeans, wheat)
- Equipment and operator provided
- Timeliness advantage for large operations
- **Cost**: $15-$25/acre depending on yield and crop

**Custom Hay Services**:
- Mowing, tedding, baling, and hauling
- Specialized equipment for different hay types
- Forage quality testing (moisture, crude protein, ADF)
- Storage management

**Custom Drying and Storage**:
- Grain drying services
- Controlled atmosphere storage
- Pest management during storage
- Cleaning and quality maintenance
- **Cost**: $0.30-$0.60 per bushel

### Equipment Rental and Leasing

**Equipment Categories**:
- **Tractors**: Hourly or seasonal rental
- **Specialty equipment**: Sprayers, applicators, spreaders
- **Harvest equipment**: Combines, pickers, cutterheads
- **Post-harvest**: Grain dryers, augers, storage equipment
- **Support equipment**: Scales, moisture testers, samplers

**Rental Business Models**:
- Hourly rental rates ($50-$150+ per hour)
- Daily rental ($300-$2,000+ per day)
- Seasonal rental (entire crop cycle)
- Long-term leasing
- Operator-included services

**Benefits for Farmers**:
- Reduced capital investment
- Access to specialized equipment for specific tasks
- Maintenance and repair covered by rental company
- Flexibility to scale operations up or down
- Trial of equipment before purchase

**Data Processing and Interpretation**:

**Cost Structure**:

### Soil Electrical Conductivity Mapping

**EM38 Soil Scanning**:
- Measures soil electrical conductivity
- Correlates with soil texture, moisture, salinity
- Field mapping at 10-50 meter grid
- Identifies productivity zones
- Base map for precision agriculture

**Applications**:
- Delineation of management zones
- Variable rate input recommendations
- Soil property correlation
- Field stratification for research
- **Cost**: $15-$30/acre

### Yield Mapping and Analysis

**Data Collection**:
- Combine monitor data capture during harvest

**Analysis Services**:

## Market and Economic Factors

### Pricing and Business Models

**Fee Structures**:
- **Per-Acre Fee**: $5-$25/acre for consulting
- **Hourly Rate**: $75-$150+ per hour
- **Retainer Fee**: $500-$5,000+/month for ongoing relationship
- **Success-Based**: Commission or profit-sharing arrangement
