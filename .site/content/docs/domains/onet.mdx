# O*NET Domain

The Occupational Information Network (O*NET) is a comprehensive database of occupational information developed by the U.S. Department of Labor. It provides detailed descriptions of occupations, their requirements, and characteristics.

## Entity Types

Browse O*NET data by type:

- **[Occupations](/onet/Occupation)** - 1,016 standardized occupation definitions
- **[Tasks](/onet/Task)** - 17,951 work tasks performed in various occupations
- **[Skills](/onet/Skill)** - Competencies and abilities required for work
- **[Knowledge](/onet/Knowledge)** - Subject matter expertise areas
- **[Abilities](/onet/Ability)** - Enduring attributes of individuals
- **[Work Activities](/onet/WorkActivity)** - General types of job behaviors
- **[Work Contexts](/onet/WorkContext)** - Physical and social factors of work
- **[Tools & Technologies](/onet/Technology)** - Equipment and software used

## Data Source

All O*NET data comes from [www.onetcenter.org](https://www.onetcenter.org/), the official source for occupational information in the United States.
