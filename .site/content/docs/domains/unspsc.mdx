# UNSPSC Domain

The United Nations Standard Products and Services Code (UNSPSC) is an open, global, multi-sector standard for efficient, accurate classification of products and services.

## Entity Types

Browse UNSPSC data by type:

- **[Commodities](/unspsc/Commodity)** - 158,463 product and service codes
- **[Segments](/unspsc/Segment)** - Top-level categories
- **[Families](/unspsc/Family)** - Mid-level groupings
- **[Classes](/unspsc/Class)** - Detailed classifications

## Classification Hierarchy

UNSPSC uses a 4-level hierarchy:

1. **Segment** (XX000000) - Broadest grouping (e.g., "Live Plant and Animal Material and Accessories and Supplies")
2. **Family** (XXXX0000) - Major category within segment
3. **Class** (XXXXXX00) - Specific product/service group
4. **Commodity** (XXXXXXXX) - Individual product/service item

## Use Cases

- **Procurement** - Standardized purchasing categories
- **E-commerce** - Product classification for online marketplaces
- **Supply Chain** - Inventory and logistics categorization
- **Analytics** - Spend analysis and market intelligence

## Data Source

UNSPSC codes come from [www.unspsc.org](https://www.unspsc.org/), maintained by GS1 US.
