# APQC Domain

The APQC Process Classification Framework (PCF) is a taxonomy of cross-functional business processes intended to allow organizations to see their activities from a cross-industry process viewpoint.

## Entity Types

Browse APQC data by type:

- **[Processes](/apqc/Process)** - 37,708 business process definitions across all categories
- **Process Categories** - Hierarchical organization of processes
- **Process Groups** - Collections of related processes

## Framework Structure

The PCF organizes processes into categories:

1. **Operating Processes** - Core value-creating processes
2. **Management Processes** - Processes that govern operations
3. **Support Processes** - Processes that enable operations

## Process Hierarchy

Processes are organized in a hierarchical structure from high-level categories down to specific activities:

- **Category** (1.0) → **Process Group** (1.1) → **Process** (1.1.1) → **Activity** (1.1.1.1)

## Data Source

All APQC data comes from the [APQC Process Classification Framework](https://www.apqc.org/process-classification-framework), a globally recognized standard for process improvement.
