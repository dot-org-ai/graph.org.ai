## How It Works

This knowledge graph connects entities from multiple authoritative sources:

- **O*NET** - Occupations, tasks, skills, and more
- **APQC** - Business process framework
- **UNSPSC** - Products and services codes
- **NAICS** - Industry classifications
- **Career Clusters** - Education and career pathways

Navigate by **industry**, **function**, or **theme** to discover relevant occupations, processes, products, and more in context.
